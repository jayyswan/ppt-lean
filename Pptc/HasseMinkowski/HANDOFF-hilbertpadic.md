## NOT delivered (blockers / plan)

1. `hilbertSym_padic_odd_case10`  (α=1, β=0; RHS would be `(v/p)`, the quadratic character of
   the unit part of `b`).  Provable with the following plan, not attempted within budget:
   - v a square unit mod p => v is a square in ℤ_[p] (Hensel: `PadicInt.isSquare_of_zmod`),
     then `(p*u, v) = 1` since `z=v^{1/2}, x=0, y=1` is a nontrivial zero.
   - v a nonsquare unit mod p => NO nontrivial ℚ_[p] solution.  Key observation not requiring
     any norm-group theory: for v a nonsquare unit and any z,y with `z² - v y² ≠ 0`,
     `valuation (z² - v y²)` is **even**:
       * if `valuation (z²) ≠ valuation (v y²)`, no cancellation (`AddValuation.map_sub_eq_of_lt_*`)
         and the value is `min` of two even numbers;
       * if equal (so `valuation z = valuation y`), then `v(z² - v y²) = 2·v(y) + v((z/y)² - v)`
         and `(z/y)² - v` is a **unit**: reducing mod p, `(z/y)² ≡ v` would make v a square.
     But `valuation (p*u*x²) = 1 + 2·v(x)` is **odd** (and x ≠ 0, else v = (z/y)² is a square).
     Contradiction.  Formalising this needs `Padic.valuation`/`Padic.addValuation`
     (`Padic.valuation_mul`, `valuation_pow`, `AddValuation.map_sub_eq_of_lt_left/right`) plus
     the mod-p unit argument on `z/y ∈ ℤ_[p]ˣ`.
2. `hilbertSym_padic_odd_case11` (α=β=1; RHS `(-1)^{(p-1)/2} (u/p)(v/p)`).  Needs
   `(p,p)_p = (-1)^{(p-1)/2}`, equivalently `(p,p)=1 ↔ IsSquare (-1 : ZMod p)`; the ⇐ direction
   is `x²+y²=0` with `-1` a square in ℚ_[p]; the ⇒ direction is a 2-term valuation/parity
   argument on `z² - p(x²+y²) = 0`.  `FiniteField.isSquare_neg_one_iff` is available.
3. `hilbertSym.padic_odd_eq` (full Serre formula) and
   `instance : HasBilinHilbertSym ℚ_[p]` (`mul_left_eq`).  The missing ingredient is
   multiplicativity.  Realistic route for a future worker:
   (i) prove case10 + case11 above;
   (ii) prove `hilbertSym` is invariant under square classes (DONE: `hilbertSym_mul_square_eq`);
   (iii) either (a) establish that `ℚ_[p]ˣ/(ℚ_[p]ˣ)²` is the Klein four group generated by a
       nonsquare unit `g` and `p` (needs the structure of `ℤ_[p]ˣ` and of the square classes,
       more Mathlib work), then check the 4×4 table; or (b) prove the norm-group description
       `(a,b)_p = 1 ↔ b ∈ N(ℚ_[p](√a)ˣ)` and derive bimultiplicativity from norm groups.
   Note `p = 2` is out of scope (the `2`-adic formula uses `PadicInt.epsilon/omega`).
4. `p = 2`: deliberately not attempted.
