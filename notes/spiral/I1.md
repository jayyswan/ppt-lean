# I1: the Archimedean spiral meets lines and conics

Stream I1 of `pptc/PLAN-spiral.md`. Labels: [Proof] paper argument; [Wolfram] checked in
Wolfram Language (numbers below are 40 digits of 60-70 digit roots, residuals below 1e-60);
[Heuristic]; [Open]. "Reachable" means: the root theta* lies in a window of plan section 0
(theta in (0, 4 pi]); `restrict` isolates one crossing; the mirror half (theta < 0) is
`scale_x(-1)`. No Lean was touched. All numerics are Wolfram; no Python was used.

## Findings summary

1. **One master equation covers every case [Proof + Wolfram].** Write z = theta e^{i theta}.
   Every affine image of the spiral (rotation, uniform or non-uniform scale, translation, any
   shear; the class is closed under all of them by `affineMap_PConstructibleCurve` in
   `Basic.lean`) meets a conic C exactly where the plain spiral meets the pulled-back conic,
   which is again an arbitrary conic `a x^2 + b xy + c y^2 + d x + e y + f = 0`. Substituting
   (x, y) = theta (cos, sin):

   **`Re(A th^2 e^{2i th} + B th e^{i th}) + m th^2 + f = 0`**,
   with `m = (a+c)/2`, `A = ((a-c) - i b)/2`, `B = d - i e`, `f` real. [Wolfram: the identity
   was checked symbolically, together with `|A|^2 - m^2 = (b^2 - 4ac)/4`.]
   So `|A| < |m|`, `= |m|`, `> |m|` mean ellipse, parabola, hyperbola. Equivalently
   `m|z|^2 + Re(A z^2 + B z) + f = 0`. Three harmonic levels: frequency 0 and 2 weighted by
   `th^2`, frequency 1 weighted by `th`, plus a constant.
2. **Organising principle.** Affine image of the spiral meets a circle = spiral meets an
   ellipse. The matrix M enters only through `kappa = |A|/m = (s1^2 - s2^2)/(s1^2 + s2^2)`
   (s_i the singular values) and the axis angle alpha; uniform scaling is kappa = 0. [Proof]
3. **Reduction by the structure of (A, B, m, f):**
   - **B = 0** (conic centred at the spiral centre): **R**. `th^2 (m + h cos 2(th - alpha)) = -f`
     is a frequency-2 sine meeting a translated `y = c/x^2` power law.
   - **A = 0** (pulled-back form is a circle: similarity images of circles, including the
     off-centre circle; and lines): **R**. `cos(th - phi) = -(m th^2 + f)/(p th)`, a shifted
     sine meeting a sheared hyperbola.
   - **Worked example settled.** The off-centre circle gives `cos(th - phi) = (th^2 + q)/(2P th)`.
     The curve `y = (x + q/x)/(2P)` is the shear `(x, y) -> (x, y + x/(2P))` of the hyperbola
     `y = q/(2P x)`; `shear_PConstructibleCurve`, `hyperbola_shift_PConstructibleCurve` and
     `affineMap_PConstructibleCurve` (all in `Basic.lean`) give constructibility. Equivalently
     a sheared sine meets the hyperbola `XY = q/(2P)`, row 2 of
     `sheared-sine-intersections.md`. **R, not new.** [Proof + Wolfram, residual below 1e-60]
   - **Line through the centre**: **E** (`theta = phi + pi/2 + k pi`).
   - **Circle centred at the spiral centre**: **E** (`theta = rho`); the point
     `(rho cos rho, rho sin rho)` is **R** by `cos_Pconstructible`/`sin_Pconstructible`.
   - **Degenerate conics** (pair of lines): each factor is a line, **R**.
   - **Generic** (A and B nonzero, f arbitrary, A/B^2 not real): **?**. Two harmonics. The
     one-trig-function partner (eliminate cos) is an irreducible sextic in (theta, sin theta)
     for the sample tried, which is not a base curve. [Wolfram; Heuristic]
   - **Aligned** (conic mirror-symmetric about a line through the spiral centre, i.e. `A/B^2`
     real): a single trig function again, but the algebraic partner is the conic seen in
     coordinates (theta, u/theta): a quartic, or a cubic if f = 0 (e.g. `x(4 - 3y^2) = 4y`).
     **? [Heuristic]**: no base curve or affine image has that shape, but non-drawability is
     not proved. Parabola `y = k x^2` gives `sin th = k th cos^2 th` (stream I2's example).
4. **Negative result [Proof + Wolfram].** No uniform configuration (fixed spiral, fixed conic or
   line, infinitely many roots) contains the roots of `tan th = lam th + mu` (T1, `lam != 0`,
   so this includes `tan x = x` and T3), `(lam th + mu) tan th = 1` (T2), or
   `th tan(th - beta) = b` (T6). Sketch: at such roots `cos th = +-1/sqrt(1 + w^2)` with
   alternating sign (w the right-hand slope function), so the master equation becomes
   `R(th) +- S(th)/sqrt(1 + w^2) = 0` with R, S rational. Infinitely many zeros of each sign
   force R = S = 0, and the coefficient equations have only the trivial solution (Wolfram
   `Solve` returned A = B = m = f = 0 in all three cases). So the spiral's *box* (tangent
   lines) gives T3 while its conic intersections give nothing in T1/T2/T6: the two
   catalogues are disjoint. Inside the real windows (theta <= 4 pi) only the first 3 roots
   of `tan x = x` (4.4934, 7.7253, 10.9041) exist, so this is a structural statement, not a
   root count.
5. **Named things that appear, all R:** the **Dottie number** (spiral meets the circle on
   diameter [0,1]: `theta = cos theta`) and **Kepler's equation** (spiral meets the circle
   through the centre of diameter `e` in direction `phi = pi/2 - M`; then `theta = E - M`).
   Neither is new.
6. **Roots reachable [window facts from plan section 0, unchecked by me]:** for R families
   window limits are irrelevant (the roots are reachable without the spiral). For ? families
   only roots with theta <= 4 pi are. Bounded conics have finitely many roots (theta at most
   |centre| + radius, usually 1-3); unbounded conics give 3-7 in (0, 4 pi].

## 1. Derivations

Let `h = |A|`, `alpha = -arg(A)/2`, `p = |B|`, `phi = -arg B`. The master equation reads
`th^2 (m + h cos 2(th - alpha)) + p th cos(th - phi) + f = 0`.

- **Line** (`a = b = c = 0`): `th cos(th - phi) = k`. With `t = th - phi`: `(t + phi) cos t = k`,
  a translated hyperbola `y = k/(x + phi)` meeting the cosine curve. `phi` is P-constructible
  (`arccos_Pconstructible_of_mem_Icc`, arctan of constructibles). R. If `k = 0`: E.
- **Centred circle** radius rho: `th = rho`. E.
- **Centred conic** (`B = 0`): put `s = 2 th`: `cos(s - 2 alpha) = (-4f/s^2 - m)/h`: a sine
  meeting a translated, scaled `y = c x^-2` (`power_law` with b = -2). R. Covers centred
  ellipses and hyperbolas in any orientation (all kappa, alpha). The hyperbola `xy = 1` gives
  `sin 2 th = 2/th^2`.
- **Off-centre circle** (centre `P(cos phi, sin phi)`, radius s, `q = P^2 - s^2`):
  `th^2 - 2P th cos(th - phi) + q = 0`. Sheared coordinates `X = th`,
  `Y = cos(th - phi) - th/(2P)` satisfy `X Y = q/(2P)`. R.
- **Circle through the centre** (`q = 0`): `cos(th - phi) = th/(2P)`: a line meets a sine, the
  Kepler class. R. Dottie: `P = 1/2`, `phi = 0`. Kepler: `2P = e`, `phi = pi/2 - M`,
  `th = E - M` (verified below).
- **Conic through the centre, general** (f = 0, A and B nonzero, unaligned):
  `th (m + h cos 2(th - alpha)) + p cos(th - phi) = 0`. ?
- **Hyperbola in product form** `l1 l2 = k` with `l_i = p_i th cos(th - phi_i) + f_i`:
  `m = p1 p2 cos(phi1 - phi2)/2`, `|A| = p1 p2/2`. If both asymptotes pass through the
  centre it is centred: R. Otherwise generic: ?.
- **Parabola** (`|A| = |m|`, so `m + h cos 2t = 2m cos^2 t`):
  `2m th^2 cos^2 t + th (g cos t + g' sin t) + f = 0`. If `g' = 0` it factors into two
  lines `th cos t = c_i`: R. Otherwise ?. (Axis through the centre, aligned: `y^2 = 4x` gives
  `th (1 - cos^2 th) = 4 cos th`.)
- **Non-uniform image of the spiral meeting a circle:** `M = [[2,1],[0,1]]`, circle centre
  `(1,-1)`, radius 3 pulls back to `(m, A, B, f) = (3, 1 - 2i, 4, -7)`, `kappa = 0.745`.
  [Wolfram: the master-form residual vanishes at the geometric root.]

The shifted hyperbola `(x-1)(y-2) = 3` is a *base* curve (affine image of `power_law`), yet the
spiral meeting it is classed ? because its centre is off the spiral centre and the
asymptotes are not aligned: the R verdicts concern the equation's shape, not the curve's
being drawable.

Natural parameters, uniform over P-constructible values: lines `(phi, k)`; centred conics
`(kappa, alpha, f/m)`; circles `(P, phi, q)`; general conics `(m, A, B, f)` up to scale (5 real
parameters). Each is a P-constructible tuple because the conic coefficients are, with
`|A|`, `arg A`, `|B|`, `arg B` obtained by sqrt and arctan.

## 3. Other things noticed

- The master equation is the conic in complex form; R/E occur exactly on the strata B = 0,
  A = 0 (and degenerate conics), while the interesting generic stratum has dimension 5.
- The aligned-parabola partner `y = (sqrt(4 + x^2) - 2)/x = tan(arctan(x/2)/2)` has two
  parallel asymptotes `y = +-1`, like the quartic `y = x/sqrt(1 + x^2)` in M.md, but is a
  different curve. [Algebra only; relation unproved.]
- Counts come from a sign-change scan; tangential (double) roots are a box-stream (B) issue.
- Stream L could search the generic equation `th^2 (m + h cos 2(th - alpha)) + p th cos(th - phi) + f = 0`
  for physical models; I found no named instance except the R ones (Dottie, Kepler).
- Aligned case, algebraic fact: the partner is the conic in the coordinates
  (theta, u/theta), where u is the coordinate along the symmetry axis. Why it matters: it
  explains why the cubic/quartic is not a base curve.
