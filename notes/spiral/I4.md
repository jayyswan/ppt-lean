## Conventions

By W/F1/F2 every reachable arc is a window of
`z(psi) = c + lambda e^{i kappa} psi e^{-i eps psi}`, `eps = +-1` (mirror), `psi in [psi_a, psi_b]`, for all three spiral
presets (Warped Circle, Arch Up/Button, Arch Down; they differ only in windows and in lambda). `c` = pole, `lambda`
scale, `kappa` rotation; all are P-constructible and free (any similarity is available via `translate`, `scale`,
`rotate`). A general affine image is `z -> alpha z + beta conj(z)` (`M in GL2`). Write `S(c, lambda, kappa, eps)`.
The *polar parameter* of a point is `psi = |z - c| / lambda`, so "root" below means the pair `(psi1, psi2)`.

## Findings summary

1. **Same pole, any pitch, rotation, chirality: all E, closed form [Proof + Wolfram].**
   `S(0,l1,k1,+) cap S(0,l2,k2,+)`: moduli force `psi2 = mu psi1`, `mu = l1/l2`; angles force
   `(mu-1) psi1 = (k2-k1) + 2 pi k`. So `psi1 = (delta + 2 pi k)/(mu - 1)`, `delta = k2 - k1` - one point per integer `k`
   (finitely many in the windows). `mu = 1`: the curves coincide (`delta = 0 mod 2pi`) or never meet (rotated copies are
   the other arms of a multi-arm spiral; they are disjoint). Mirrored pair (`eps2 = -1`): `psi1 = (k1 - k2 - 2 pi k)/(1 + mu)`;
   `mu = 1`, `k1 = k2` gives `psi = k pi/..`: the two edges of a glyph such as ▲ meet exactly at `psi = pi, 2 pi`
   (both ends of the windows [pi, 2pi]), consistent with W. Coordinates are P-constructible (`cos_Pconstructible`,
   `sin_Pconstructible`, `pi_Pconstructible`). Worked example [Wolfram]: two ◢ Circle-pour boxes, 4.2 in and 3.5 in, same
   pole, second box rotated 90 degrees: `mu = 6/5`, `delta = pi/2`: `psi1 = 5 pi/2`, `psi2 = 3 pi`, residual 0 at 40 digits;
   both lie in [2 pi, 4 pi], so both arcs are the full ◢ edges (point `0.4375 + 0.75777...i` times the scale).
2. **Same pole, non-uniform image of one partner: ? (with E axis roots) [Wolfram, Heuristic].** `z2 = M(psi2 e^{-i psi2})`,
   `M z = alpha z + beta conj z`. Moduli: `psi1 = t |g(t)|`, `g = alpha e^{-it} + beta e^{it}`, `t = psi2`; angle:
   `psi1 + arg g(t) = 0 mod 2pi`. One unknown with an *elliptic-type* modulus `sqrt(a + b cos 2t)` inside a phase: not a
   polynomial-times-harmonics equation. Roots on the axes of `M` are E (`psi1 = psi2 = pi/2 + k pi` on the fixed axis;
   `psi1 = (s) psi2` on the stretched axis); the rest are genuinely transcendental, e.g. `M = diag(2,1)`:
   `t = 1.89342395807974056752769126576998...`, `psi1 = 2.16014746009227583844486782623846...`.
   Rank-one `M` (spiral squashed to a line through the pole) is E (line through pole).
3. **Different poles: ? [Wolfram, Heuristic].** `A u e^{-iu} - B v e^{-iv} = d` (`A = l1 e^{ik1}`, `B = l2 e^{ik2}`, `u = psi1`,
   `v = psi2`). Eliminating `v = |A u e^{-iu} - d|/l2` gives the single equation
   `arg(z1(u) - d) + eps2 |z1(u) - d|/l2 = k2 mod 2pi`: a square root of a quadratic in `u` with trig coefficients, inside
   a phase, plus a linear term. No single-harmonic collapse; the Delta-trick that works for the offset (below) fails
   because the two exponentials have different arguments. Not reducible to I1 conics. Root counts grow without bound with
   `|d|` (e.g. `d = 5+3i` equal pitch: 15 roots with `psi1 <= 15 pi`, 4 with both `<= 4 pi`).
4. **Mirror-symmetric pole pair: on-axis roots are R, off-axis roots are paired [Proof + Wolfram].** If `S2` is the reflection
   of `S1` in the perpendicular bisector `x = d/2`, intersections on the axis are `S1 cap line`, i.e. `psi cos(psi - ..) = d/2`
   (I1 line: R); off-axis ones come in swapped pairs `(u,v) <-> (v,u)`. Example `d = 6`: on-axis `psi cos psi = 3` roots
   5.312469711656567697366157998254..., 7.438875840167751477431559165148..., 11.26513535919150992842316279911...
   (these match the three `u = v` roots of the 2-D solver), off-axis pair `(1.7534739368571421802, 6.549593325688070935)` and its swap.
5. **Spiral cap its own offset: a clean reduction to one tan(x/2) curve [Proof + Wolfram], class ? (high degree).**
   Pairing `z(psi1)` with `z(psi2) + d nu(psi2)` is the *normal chord* condition. With `Delta = psi1 - psi2`, `t = tan(Delta/2)`,
   `D = d^2` (units of the pole-scale; `d_phys/lambda`):
   `psi1 (cos Delta + psi2 sin Delta) = psi2` (a quadratic in `psi2`), `D = Delta^2 + 2 psi1 psi2 (1 - cos Delta)`, and eliminating
   `psi2` leaves **Q(Delta, t, D) = -(D-Delta^2)^2 (1+t^2) - 4 Delta t (D-Delta^2) - 4 Delta^2 t^2 + 4 D t^4 = 0**, with
   `psi2 = [ (D - Delta^2)/(2t) + Delta cos Delta ] / (1 - cos Delta)`. So the problem is a quartic-in-both-variables
   algebraic curve `Q = 0` in the `(Delta, t)`-plane meeting the curve `t = tan(Delta/2)` (a scaled/sheared sine-type
   curve); `Q` is quadratic in `D`, irreducible over `Q(i)` [Wolfram `FactorList`], its discriminant in `D` is `16 t^5 (t^3 + Delta^2 t - 2 Delta)`. Not a `poly_graph` (degree 4 in t). Class **?** [Heuristic]: no base
   curve is `Q = 0`; non-reducibility unproved. Roots verified to 36 digits with exact residual 0 (direct test
   `z(psi1) = z(psi2) + d nu(psi2)`): `|d|=5`: `(psi2, psi1) = (1.50346610685545579187297488889800364925, 3.83952382394188018109639963351355504375)` (signed d = +5) and the d = -5 root `(psi2, psi1) = (5.0216852621682764306, 0.14118994037520637981)`;
   `|d|=3`: `(0.69854269039846114471622630730240, 2.66229150269024148284930181127402)`; `|d| = 2 pi`: `(5.4838041259888014038, 1.3255095068786386377)`
   and `(4.5342428435499471860, 2.0466200124918114629)`. Counts (scan, `Delta in [-14,14]`): `|d|=3`: 1, `5`: 2, `2pi`: 2, `8`: 3, `10`: 3.
   **Threshold [Wolfram scan, Heuristic]:** no intersection for `|d| < pi/2` in pole-scale units; the infimum `pi/2` is
   approached as `psi2 -> 0, psi1 -> pi/2` (the normal at the pole meets the spiral at `(0, pi/2)`), so for `|d_phys| < (pi/2) lambda`
   the offset never touches the spiral. Large `psi`: arms are `2 pi` apart so `|d| ~ 2 pi k` is the natural scale.
   The same Delta-trick applies when the partner offset has a different pitch/rotation but the same pole (angle
   `Delta + kappa`, [Heuristic, not computed]).
6. **Hypotheses of `offset` are satisfiable for a warp arc [Proof].** The input must be the *whole* curve `S` with `gamma '' Icc a b = S`.
   For a window arc `gamma(psi) = c + lambda e^{i kappa} psi e^{-i psi}` on `[psi_a, psi_b]`, `a<b`: `gamma` is injective
   (|gamma| strictly increasing), `C^infinity`, `speed = lambda sqrt(1 + psi^2) > 0`, no seam (`gamma b != gamma a`). So
   `offset` applies to the arc *as a whole*; a sub-arc (after `restrict`) is again a window arc so it too qualifies.
   The offset is `psi e^{i psi} + d nu = e^{i psi}[psi(1 - d/s) + i d/s]`, `s = sqrt(1+psi^2)`, with `|w|^2 = psi^2 - 2 d psi^2/s + d^2`
   and polar angle `psi + atan2(d, psi (s - d))` [Wolfram-checked algebra above]. Caveat [Open]: the arc must first exist
   as a `PConstructibleCurve` (needs the spiral constructor from stream D); and a glyph's compound outline contains other edges,
   so isolating the single edge needs `restrict`/the constructor itself.
7. **Spiral cap warp by-products (circles, rays): E [Proof + Wolfram].** Centred circle of radius `r`: `psi = r/lambda` (one point;
   in a ◢ edge `lambda = R/(4 pi)`, circle `(R/2)(1+v)`: `psi = 2 pi (1+v)`; check `v = 3/4`, `R = 2.1`: `|z| - R/2 (1+v) = 0`,
   `psi = 11.0 = 7pi/2 ... 2pi(1+3/4)`), valid iff `r/lambda` lies in the window **and** the point's angle lies in the circle arc's
   angular span (circle arcs cover only `2 pi (edge width/ink width)`). Rays through the pole (vertical edges):
   `psi = kappa - angle mod 2 pi`, one point per turn; on the glyph's own ◢ edge the ray `u` is met at `psi = 2 pi (1+u)`
   (exact, residual 0). Within the *same* glyph the spiral's ends are the only common points with its own circles (W).
   Circles/rays from a **different box** (other pole): circle -> off-centre circle, I1 **R**; ray/segment -> general line, I1 **R**.
   Circle of the same pole but different row/glyph with `v` rational: E with the inequality conditions above.
8. **Glyph-derived spirals of other presets: same normal form [Proof from F1/F2].** Arch Up/Button (`Theta = pi`, `kappa_rho = 1`)
   and Arch Down (`kappa_rho = 1/3`) edges are `S(c, lambda, kappa, eps)` windows `[Theta a(kappa_rho + w0)/h, +Theta a]`; only `lambda`,
   `kappa` and the window change. So all of 1-5 apply verbatim to pairs from different presets; mixed-preset same-pole pairs are
   E with `mu` the ratio of scales (any ratio since `scale` is free). Arch Down reaches `psi_1 ~ 0.002`, giving roots with a
   very small `psi`.
9. **Reachability of roots [F2 window law].** Each of the two arcs is an independent box, so a pair `(psi1, psi2)` is reachable
   iff each parameter lies in the F2 union `[0.055, ~40]` (Down: starts to ~0.002; ceiling ~50) and the individual windows
   (ratio <= 2, length <= 2 pi, Down ratio <= 4) can be chosen to contain them *and* a `restrict` can isolate one crossing.
   All E and example roots below are in range (`psi <= 4 pi` unless noted). Large-`|d|` roots (second coordinates up to 28 for
   `l2 = 1/2`) leave `[0, 4 pi]` but stay below 40.
10. **Negative / nothing found [Open-ish].** No example collapses the two-pole or affine cases to a named equation; the only named
    items are classical: *normal chords* (item 5), *the multi-arm spiral* (rotated equal-pitch copies never meet, item 1).
    No Lambert-W, Kepler or T1-T6 form appeared; as the two-pole system has no tan-curve partner, T1/T2/T6 are not reached
    by this route (not proved).

## Other things noticed

- The Delta-trick works exactly when both curves share the pole and the pitch: every point of both depends on the single
  rotation `e^{i psi2}`, so only `Delta = psi1 - psi2` and `psi2` appear polynomially. The result "polynomial in
  `(Delta, tan(Delta/2), psi2)`" is a *new* shape of equation (in I1 the unknown also appears as the argument of the trig).
  Worth giving stream L / F4: is `Q = 0` meeting `t = tan(Delta/2)` reducible by any base curve?
- Arm-spacing: equal-pitch rotated spirals never meet, which is why the same-pole table has no equal-`mu` roots.
- The normal chord curve `psi1 (cos Delta + psi2 sin Delta) = psi2` is independent of `d` - `d` is just the chord length along it.
  Its minimum length `pi/2` (approached at the pole) is the first non-trivial constant of the offset problem.
- Root counts here are sign-change scans (step 0.001), not certified like F2's; double roots could be missed. Counts for
  `affroots(1,0)` etc. in my scratch run were degenerate (coincident curves) and are not reported.
- The off-axis mirrored pairs `(u,v) <-> (v,u)` suggest a sum/difference substitution
  `e^{i(v-u)/2}[(u+v)cos(kappa-(u+v)/2) - i(v-u) sin(kappa-(u+v)/2)] = D`, which I derived but did not exploit.
