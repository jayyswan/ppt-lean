# Q2: the regime table rebuilt with Theorem H (and W / C3); does any route come into range?

Status: done (2026-11-06)

Research only. Labels: [Proof], [Proof sketch], [Wolfram], [Heuristic], [Open]. Every negative is scoped to
the route and the cited theorems; nothing here is a negative about the algebraic target.

**Dependency.** Theorem H (P1) is taken as given; stream Q1 is verifying it. Every "H applies / H fails"
below is conditional on it. Thresholds used (P1 sections 1.2, 2.1):
(a) dim X >= (D-1)2^D - 1 (D = deg X); (c) vars > (S-1)2^S with S = sum of degrees (nonsingular system);
(d) R forms of equal degree d: vars > R + R(R+1)(d-1)2^(d-1); (e) two forms D' > E' >= 2:
vars > (2+E')(D'-1)2^(D'-1) + E' 2^(E'-1). "vars" = number of homogeneous variables; a linear condition
(p_1 = 0) just lowers the ambient space and is not counted in S.

**Point kinds (audit).** H gives points over a number field L inside E (so P-valued coordinates), never
rational points. Routes that feed *coefficients* to poly_graph need Q-points: MI-1 (V'_K), the n = 9
sextic quadric (done classically), and A/C's 14 rational coefficients. Routes where only P-valued
parameters occur need only E/F-points: Theorem O in Z8's form (F-point on O_m plus d-4 algebraic
rationality conditions), Engine C, Theorem X (SR_F), Theorem R, offset towers.
[Proof sketch] H also works with L_0 in F instead of E and then returns F-points: L = L_1.M is solvable over
L_0, and real elements of solvable extensions of subfields of F lie in F (Z8).

## (2) Specific checks

**Theorem O fibres W_r and O_m (B2) [Proof of the arithmetic].** W_r = CI(1,3,5,7,12) in P^9, dim 4, degree
1260, general type: needs 10^9.5 variables, has 9. The degree-12 equation also breaks W's "product of the
other degrees <= 8". For general n, O_m has m = floor((n-1)/2) odd forms, S = m^2 - 1, variables ~ 2m; need
2m > (m^2-2)2^(m^2-1), false for every m >= 3. The singularity along the pairing spaces is not itself the
obstruction (the Birch singular locus has dimension m-1); the numerical gap is.

**Engine C (B2) [Proof of the arithmetic].** W^C_r (6,7,6) and W^C_0 (3,6,7) have every equation of degree
<= 8 but total degree 252 / 126 and S = 19 / 16. Lemma A fails (degree), H fails (9 variables against
10^7 / 10^6), W fails (no quadric in the ideal). "All equations <= 8" only helps the last step on a line
(B2), not points of the 5-fold.

**W2 big-F engines (A/C, n <= 12) [Proof of the arithmetic].** Excess over the n equations is 14; (d) wants
n(n+1)(d-1)2^(d-1) with d = 43 (about 10^16 at n = 10). B has 2/3/4 conditions of degree 420-945. Both far
out of range; A/C additionally need Q-points.

**Offset towers [Heuristic on degree growth, from W1].** The fibre over a fixed rational tau is n conjugate
equations, hence automatically of equal degree delta: exactly the shape of H(d). Threshold
M >= n + n(n+1)(delta-1)2^(delta-1) [Wolfram: delta = 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 give M >= 230, 890, 2650, 17610, 98570
at n = 10; 324, 1260, 3756, 24972, 139788 at n = 12; 860, 3380, 10100, 67220, 376340 at n = 20]. At depth j,
M = 3j + 4 but delta_j >= 2^j-ish (W1: degree 8 in d already at j = 1; curve degree (j+2)2^j). Need
delta 2^delta = o(M): fails for every j. Even delta_j = j + 7 would fail (2^j against 3j).

**Theorem X's I_{K'} [Proof].** A surface for every n; H(a) needs dim >= (D-1)2^D - 1 with D = (2n-2)!.
Moreover, general-type surfaces may carry Brauer-Manin obstructions, so HP is not expected over number
fields. No route from H.

**A fixed-degree CI in a growing projective space?** [Proof of the survey over this programme's families.]
None. The number of conditions is c = n + 1 - F with F <= 36 in every single-curve engine, so c grows with
n, and the p_k have degree k, so degrees grow too. The nearest fixed-degree shape is Theorem R's V_tau (all
quadrics), but its dimension is stuck at 2 while the number of quadrics is n - 3. A bounded number of
bounded-degree conditions would need F >= n - c, i.e. a number of effective P-parameters growing with n.
No known constructor combination does that: each base curve has <= 8 P-valued parameters, an intersection
of two doubles at most, and offset (the only unbounded one) has growing degree.

**Where H beats Lemma A at all [Proof of the comparison].** Lemma A handles p_1..p_c iff c! < n. H(c) needs
vars > (c(c+1)/2 - 2) 2^(c(c+1)/2 - 1): c = 3: 128 (Lemma A: 7); c = 4: 4096 (25); c = 5: 212992 (121);
c = 6: 1.99e7 (721) [Wolfram]. So for power-sum conditions H is never competitive. H wins only for many low,
equal degree conditions: c quadrics need vars > c + 2c(c+1) (c ~ sqrt(n/2)); c cubics, c ~ sqrt(n/8);
against Lemma A's 2^c < n (c ~ log2 n), and iterated Witt slicing (also ~ log2 n). No engine here has
quadric-only conditions in enough variables.

## (3) Does Theorem H change (BD)?

**Quantitatively, yes.** X2's (BD) via RW needs M - n >= exp(2^(D+2)(log D)^2) with D = delta^n: three
exponentials in n. With H(d), the degree of the whole fibre is irrelevant; only the number of equations R = n
and the per-equation degree delta enter:

  (BD-H)   M >= n + n(n+1)(delta-1)2^(delta-1)   [Heuristic, modulo the checks below].

Checks for a candidate family [Open]: the fibre Phi_tau (affine, n conjugate equations of degree <= delta in
p in A^M) must be smooth and geometrically integral, including its projective closure after homogenising
(no singularities at infinity), with a smooth real point (W1's real data give it at M = n), Lemma N for tau,
and E/F-points suffice since the parameters are P-valued.

**Weakest growth that proves all n.** For a family indexed by j with M(j) parameters of degree delta(j),
(BD-H) holds for some j, for every n, as soon as

  delta(j) 2^delta(j) = o(M(j)),   i.e.   delta(j) <= log2 M(j) - log2 log2 M(j) - omega(1),

independent of n. In words: the parameter degree may grow like the logarithm of the number of parameters;
constant delta with M -> infinity is the extreme case. With unequal degrees (BHB (c), no equal-degree
structure) the requirement is n delta 2^(n delta) = o(M), i.e. delta <= (log2 M)/n. Here equal degree is
natural because the n equations are Galois conjugates [Proof]. Towers (delta >= 2^j, M ~ 3j) are doubly
exponentially short. A family would need M exponential in delta: each new layer adds exponentially many
free P-parameters, or degree grows only additively while parameters grow exponentially. [Open: no drawable
mechanism known.]

## (4) Verdict

**No route comes into range, for any n and any target (totally real or not).** [Proof of the arithmetic;
the failures are failures of the thresholds, so they do not depend on whether H is correct.] Nearest misses:
- n = 10 MI-1: 9 variables against (e) = 36, and it needs Q-points, which H does not give (E-points exist
  anyway, Lemma A). Over F it becomes a (2,3,30,30) CI needing 10^21 variables.
- Engine C at n = 10: 9 variables against 10^6.
- n = 9 quadric: classical already.
Totally real: routes containing p_2 = 0 have no real points at all; the odd (Theorem O), Engine C and
Theorem X/R routes have real points but are far out of range.

What does change: (BD) becomes polynomial (M ~ n^2 delta 2^delta) instead of triple exponential, and H
extends to F-points. What remains to check:
1. Q1's verification of Theorem H (HP+WA for number fields in (c)-(d), smoothness at infinity for
   inhomogeneous fibres, the F-extension remark).
2. Whether any constructor combination gives parameter degree delta <= log2 M. Obstacle: Y1a's Prop T,
   each new parameter brings a new square root, i.e. degree doubling.
3. A multihomogeneous / block-linear Birch-type theorem would change the numerics [Open, not searched].
4. Quadric-only conditions with growing number: Theorem R's V_tau, but dimension 2; no threshold helps.
