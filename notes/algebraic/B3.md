## (1) Set-up

### 1a. Field of definition and meaning of an F-point [Proof]
- (N) K = Q[x]/q, q a totally real nonic, Gal = S9 or A9 (or PSL(2,8) per B1). Fix a Q-basis w_1..w_9 of K.
  tau = sum c_i w_i with c in F^9 (i.e. tau in K_F = K (x) F). p_k(tau) = Tr_{K/Q}(tau^k) is a form of degree k in c
  with Q-coefficients. So X = {p1 = p3 = p5 = 0} subset P^8 (equivalently CI(3,5) in the trace-zero P^7) is a
  Q-variety, and S = good component(s) of its Fano scheme F_1(X) subset G(2,8) is a Q-scheme (Pluecker coords
  with Q-structure). An F-point of S = a line spanned by two F-vectors u, v in K_F = exactly what Z8/A2 need.
  No Weil restriction is involved: the Weil-restriction structure is already built in (P^8 = P(R_{K/Q} A^1)).
- Over Qbar (or Omega) K (x) Omega = Omega^9 (roots of q), so X_Qbar = X_split = {sum y = sum y^3 = sum y^5 = 0},
  and S is the K-twist of S_split by the cocycle Gal(Qbar/Q) -> S9 (permuting coordinates). For totally real K,
  K (x) R = R^9 and X(R), S(R) are literally the split real objects: real lines in {sum y^k = 0, k = 1,3,5} in RP^8.
- (D) Same with n = 10, sigma in K_F, Y0 = {p1 = p3 = 0, S6 = 0} in P^9 (S6 = -p2^3/192 + p3^2/36 + p2 p4/16 - p6/6,
  B2 1b), T = good part of F_1(Y0) subset G(2,9) (after p1: lines in P^8), a Q-scheme, twist of T_split by S10.
- Consequence [Proof]: Galois acts on geometric components of any Q-subvariety defined by an S_n-equivariant
  recipe through H = Gal(Omega F/F), which (B1, B2) is A9/S9/PSL(2,8) (resp. contains A10 or is primitive).
  H has no proper subgroup of index <= 8 (A9: min index 9; PSL(2,8): 9). So a component orbit of size m > 1
  never contains an F-rational member unless m >= 9 and even then the member is not F-rational (stabiliser
  must contain H). Upshot: useful F-curves on S/T must be (i) geometrically irreducible S_n-invariant
  constructions, or (ii) built from F-data with no symmetry (F-points / F-linear spaces of P^7, P^8).
  Examples of (i)-type failures: lines meeting a fixed pairing P^3 (945 conjugate components), lines through
  the coordinate points, etc.

### 2c. Structure [Proof sketch unless marked]
- Pluecker degree: deg S = #(sigma2-lines) + #(sigma11-lines) >= 12 + 13 = 25 (real lower bound, 2b); the virtual
  value 344925 minus a non-clean excess is the only upper information. So Lemma A (degree <= 8) is out for S and
  for every linear section of S [Wolfram lower bound]. For T: virtual 9.3 x 10^6; Lemma A out (B2 already).
- Kodaira dimension: K_S = O_S(13) ample on the smooth good locus, K_T = O_T(18): general type (assuming the
  closure's singularities along the G(2,4)-contacts are not too bad). No rational/elliptic fibration: a
  fibration S -> B with fibre D would have 2p_a(D) - 2 = 13 H.D + D^2 = 13 H.D, and no S9-natural map to a curve
  exists (the natural maps from S go to G(2,8), to X (universal line), and to the 4-dim moduli of binary septics
  p7|_l). Irregularity not computed [Open].
- Natural S9-invariant curves on S (geometrically, components undetermined): Gamma_Q = {l tangent to the quadric
  p2 = 0} -- **no real points** [Proof: p2 = sum y^2 is positive definite on real lines, so p2|_l has no real
  double root]; Gamma_7 = {p7|_l has a multiple root}; Gamma_{2,4} = {p2|_l, p4|_l share a root}; Gamma_pl = {l lies
  in a plane contained in X3} (a line of the cubic X3 lies in a plane of X3 in codim 1). Each is cut by an
  invariant Pluecker condition of degree m >= 1, so its class is ~ m H restricted, p_a >= 1 + 7 m deg S >= 176.
  Gonality unknown; no S9-equivariant map to P^1 of low degree is visible. Reducible invariant curves (lines
  meeting some pairing P^3 / some {y_i = y_j = y_k}) are useless by 1a (component orbits of size >= 9).

## (3) Curves for Theorem G

### 3a. What F-curves exist [Proof sketch]
By 1a every F-curve on S (resp. T) is either S_n-intrinsic and geometrically irreducible (2c list) or built
from F-linear data of P^7 (resp. P^8), i.e. intersections of S/T with F-rational Pluecker-linear conditions
(Schubert cycles of F-flags and, more generally, F-rational Pluecker hyperplanes). K = Q[x]/q primitive of degree
9/10 gives no extra F-rational linear subspaces beyond these (no subfields; the only canonical vector is 1, already
used by the trace).

### 3b. Schubert curves on S and their F-pencils
- C_Pi = S cap sigma1(Pi), Pi an F-rational P^5 of the trace-zero P^7: an F-rational hyperplane section in Pluecker
  space, p_a(C_Pi) = 1 + 7 deg S >= 176. Real points: yes for an open set of Pi (S(R) != empty, F-points dense in
  the real Grassmannian).
- Its two visible F-pencils: (i) pencil of P^4's in Pi through an F-P^3: degree = #sigma2-lines >= 12; (ii) pencil of
  hyperplanes through Pi: degree = #sigma11-lines >= 13 (2b; real fibre counts, so the map degree is at least this
  regardless of complex count). Both > 8, so not admissible single steps. Monodromy of these pencils: the fibres
  are the lines of X cap H_t, an incidence-type problem with no reason for imprimitivity or solvability; not
  computed [Heuristic].
- Any other low-degree F-pencil on C_Pi [Proof sketch, citing Serrano, "Extension of morphisms defined on a
  divisor", Math. Ann. 277 (1987)]: on a smooth surface, a degree-k map on a smooth ample curve C with C^2 > (k+1)^2
  extends to S. With k <= 8 and C^2 = deg S (expected in the 10^4-10^5 range; proven only >= 25), a P-rational g^1_8
  on C_Pi would come from a pencil |D| on S with D.H <= 8, i.e. a 1-parameter family of ruled surfaces of degree
  <= 8 in X filling S -- a fibration of S, which 2c does not provide. Intermediate curves D_1 in a chain have the
  same issue (degree <= 8 maps out of C). So Plücker sections are dead ends unless S is fibred [Heuristic: deg S
  > 81 not proven; Serrano needs smooth S near C].
- Curves cut by two Schubert conditions on S are finite (points): S cap sigma2(Lam) and S cap sigma11(H) are >= 12
  resp. >= 13 real points, permuted by Galois of F with no forced small orbit.

### 3c. T (decic, dim 3)
- F-surfaces: T cap sigma1(Pi) (Pi a P^6 of P^8), T cap sigma11(H) (lines in Y0 cap H). F-curves: T cap sigma1^2,
  T cap sigma2(Pi_5), T cap sigma11(H) cap sigma1(Pi), T cap Fano(Y0 cap H cap H') = lines in a codim-2 section
  (sigma22: codim 4 > 3, empty) -- the useful ones are C = T cap sigma2(Pi_5) and C' = F_1(Y0 cap H) cap sigma1.
- Their visible pencils have degree = a codim-3 Schubert count on T (sigma3, sigma21, sigma111-type), virtual
  2.66 x 10^6 for sigma3. Adjunction: K_C = (18 + 2) H|_C for a complete intersection of two Pluecker hyperplanes,
  p_a = 1 + 10 deg T. Same Serrano-type obstruction (now on a surface section of T) [Heuristic].
- T has one advantage over S: dimension 3, so it contains F-surfaces T cap sigma1(Pi) whose Fano-type structure
  could be searched, but these are again general type (K = 19 H) [Proof sketch].

### 3d. Verdict for (3)
No F-curve on S or T with an admissible chain (Theorem G) or degree <= 8 (Lemma A) was found. Every F-rational
construction available reduces to Pluecker-linear sections, whose natural F-pencils have degree >= 12 (S, real
lower bound) and whose other pencils would have to come from a fibration of S/T by low-degree curves, which the
S_n-structure does not supply [Proof sketch + Heuristic]. [Open]: irregularity of S; whether some intrinsic
invariant curve (Gamma_7, Gamma_{2,4}, Gamma_pl) has low F-gonality.

## (4) Explicit test

- Real points on the candidate F-curves exist [Wolfram]: C_Pi and the fibres of its pencils have real points
  (2b: 12 real good lines meeting one rational P^4, 13 real good lines in X cap H for one rational H).
- Regularity [Wolfram]: on the sigma11 run (SeedRandom[11] for H, SeedRandom[5], 40 starts) the 4 good solutions
  found all have Jacobian (13 x 14) of full rank 13, smallest nonzero singular value 0.06-0.22 (the 14th
  direction is the rotation gauge). So these are regular real points; they persist under perturbation of H.
  Hence for **every** totally real K, F-rational hyperplanes H (dense among real ones in K_F coordinates) near
  the tested one have >= 4 (and plausibly >= 13) real good lines: the fibre count is a property of the real split
  model, not of K.
- No curve qualified for Theorem G, so there was nothing to push to an explicit P-point; no end-to-end
  construction was run. Not run: (D) real sigma3 count (timeouts), monodromy of the sigma11 pencil, the intrinsic
  curves Gamma_7, Gamma_{2,4}, Gamma_pl.

## (5) Uniformity

- Everything found is K-independent [Proof sketch]: S, T are twists of the split S_split, T_split by the S_n
  cocycle; Schubert numbers, canonical class, the real locus (= split real locus for totally real K) and the
  pencil degrees are invariant under twisting. The only K-dependence is which curves/points are F-rational, and
  by 1a the S_n-intrinsic ones are F-rational for every K while the component-orbit obstruction applies to every
  K with H = A9/S9/PSL(2,8) (resp. primitive of degree 10). So the failure of this route is uniform for S9/A9
  nonics and S10/A10 decics; equally, a structure that worked (an intrinsic low-gonality curve or a fibration of
  S_split) would work uniformly.
