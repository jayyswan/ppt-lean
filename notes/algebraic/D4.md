# D4: two P-quartic graphs, one rotated (degree 9)

Stream D4 of `PLAN-degree9.md` (D0 row J), 2026-11-13. Research only. Backend: Wolfram (wolfram
MCP). Log: `HANDOFF-alg-D4.md`.

**Verdict: killed for the Lemma A route (scoped).** The single condition on the generator is a
hypersurface of degree **≥ 36**. That is far above the < 9 Lemma A needs and above E's 10. No
step-by-step structure was found, and the symmetric case `Q = R` is impossible.

## 1. Formulation [Proof]

- **The curves.** `y = Q(x)` and `x = R(y)`, with `Q, R ∈ F[x]_{≤4}`. These are Lemma Q graphs;
  `rotate` gives the second. They cross at the roots of `R(Q(x)) − x`, of degree 16.
- **The condition.** A generator `τ ∈ K_F` works iff `χ_τ | R∘Q − x`, i.e. `σ = Q(τ)` and
  `τ = R(σ)`.
- **Count (D0 row J, confirmed there).**
  - The pairs `(Q, R)` modulo the affine maps of x and of σ form a 6-dimensional family.
  - Nonics modulo affine maps of x form a 7-dimensional space.
  - So the image `I₄₄` (degree-9 factors of `R∘Q − x`) is a hypersurface, and there is
    **1 condition** on τ.
- **Its degree.** In the centred coefficients `e₂ … e₉` (weights 2 … 9), `I₄₄` is weighted
  homogeneous of some weight w. The good generators then form a hypersurface of degree w in τ's
  centred coordinates.

## 2. The weight of `I₄₄` [Computed, mod p]

**Method.**
1. Take random `Q, R` over `F_p`, p = 10007, and factor `R∘Q − x` mod p.
2. Each sub-multiset of factors of total degree 9 is an `F_p`-point of `I₄₄`. Centre it.
3. For weight w, evaluate all weight-w monomials in `e₂ … e₉` on (number of monomials + 60)
   points, and take the rank mod p.

**Positive control.** The cubic∘cubic family `R∘Q − x` (Q, R cubic, codimension 3) shows
relations at weight 4 already (nullity 1 at w = 4, 6; 3 at w = 8; growing after).

**Result.**

| w | monomials | points | nullity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10, 16, 20, 24, 28 | 11 … 349 | +60 | 0 |
| 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35 | 468, 529, 617, 698, 804, 907 | +60 | **0** |

- If there were a relation of weight w ≤ 29, multiplying by `e₂^a e₃^b` would give one at some
  weight in [30, 35]. So **there is no relation of weight ≤ 35**: `deg H ≥ 36`.
- Caveat: a relation over ℚ reduces to a nonzero relation mod p (scale it to a primitive integer
  polynomial first). It still vanishes on the sampled `F_p`-points for all but finitely many p.
  Only p = 10007 was run.
- Weight 40 and above did not fit in a 60-second call.

## 3. Structure [Proof]

- **`Q = R` is impossible.** Suppose a Galois-stable 9-set of roots of `R∘R − x` is the root set of
  χ_τ.
  - Then `R` permutes the 9-set and commutes with the Galois action.
  - The centraliser of a primitive group in `S₉` is trivial, so `R` fixes all 9 points.
  - But `R(x) = x` has only 4 roots.
  - The same argument rules out any shape in which the crossing set carries a nontrivial
    Galois-equivariant self-map.
- **Fibres.** For a fixed rational Q, the condition on R is again "a degree-9 factor of
  `R∘Q − x` has field K". That is the original problem on a smaller family, with 4 conditions on
  3 parameters. No order was found in which each step has degree ≤ 8.
- **Cubic∘cubic** has no overshoot, but it is codimension 3 (3 conditions on τ). It is a control
  only, as the plan said.
