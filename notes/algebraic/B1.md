## (0) Lemma N gap -- CLOSED for totally real q [Proof + Wolfram]

Setting. q in Q[x] irreducible of degree 9, TOTALLY REAL, splitting field Omega (so Omega is a subfield of R),
G = Gal(Omega/Q). F = P cap R_alg. E := F cap Omega, H := Gal(Omega/E). Since Omega/Q is Galois,
Gal(K F / F) = Gal(Omega F/F) restricted = H acting on the 9 roots (K F a field iff H transitive).

**Lemma B0 (what H can be) [Proof].**
1. Every x in Omega with [E(x):E] <= 8 lies in E. Proof: min poly of x over E has degree <= 8 and
   coefficients in E (subset of P); x is real because Omega is real; P is closed under real roots of such
   polynomials, so x in P, and x algebraic real, so x in F cap Omega = E.
   Group form: **H has no proper subgroup of index <= 8.**
2. Hence H is perfect: a nontrivial solvable quotient would give a normal subgroup of prime index p, and
   every prime dividing |S9| = 2^7 3^4 5 7 is <= 7. (No solvable-tower lemma needed.)
   In particular H is in A9 (indeed sqrt(disc) is real, in P, so in E).
3. If H is intransitive, every orbit has size <= 8, so the point stabilisers have index <= 8, so equal H:
   H fixes every root, H = 1, Omega in F, **every root of q is in P**. Done without any route.
4. If H is transitive: imprimitive transitive groups of degree 9 lie in S3 wr S3 (solvable), and the
   affine primitive groups 3^2:K (K in GL(2,3)) are solvable; perfect transitive subgroups of S9 are
   therefore exactly **A9 and PSL(2,8)** (acting on P^1(F_8)); PGammaL(2,8) = PSL(2,8):3 is not perfect
   [Proof, using the list of primitive groups of degree 9 (Dixon-Mortimer App. B / Sims)].
   PSL(2,8) has no subgroup of index < 9 (maximal subgroups 2^3:7, D18, D14 of index 9, 28, 36)
   [Cited, ATLAS], so it survives item 1.

**Dichotomy [Proof].** For totally real nonic q (any Galois group):
  either all roots are in P (H = 1), or H = A9, or H = PSL(2,8) in its 9-point action.
(If G is solvable or imprimitive, H = 1 always. If G = PSL(2,8) or PGammaL(2,8), H in {1, PSL(2,8)}.
If G = A9, S9, H in {1, PSL(2,8), A9}.)
Note: the case "H transitive but not containing A9" is NOT excluded (P might contain the degree-720
fixed field of a PSL(2,8)); but it is harmless:

**Lemma N holds for H = PSL(2,8) [Proof].** X2's proof of Lemma N uses only: (i) KL/L has no
intermediate field, i.e. H primitive -- PSL(2,8) is 2-transitive, point stabiliser 2^3:7 maximal; (ii)
H 2-transitive -- PSL(2,8) is sharply 3-transitive. So both conclusions hold over F unconditionally.
Other uses also survive: Theorem R's (ND2) (needs primitive), Y1a 4.1 "l_tau only L-line" (needs no
quadratic subfield of K_F/F: true, 2^3:7 maximal) and Aut(K_F/F) = 1 (2^3:7 self-normalising).

**Z5 survives too [Wolfram].** Z5 needs the F2-heart M = F2^9/<1> (= sum-zero subspace, 9 odd)
irreducible under H. Wolfram: PSL(2,8) generated by t->t+1, t->w t (w^3=w+1), t->1/t on F8 u {inf};
order 504, PGammaL (add Frobenius) order 1512, both inside A9 (all generators even). For each of the
255 nonzero sum-zero vectors v, rank over F2 of its H-orbit = 8 (min = max = 8). So M is irreducible
for PSL(2,8), hence also for PGammaL(2,8) and **for A9** (contains a PSL(2,8)) -- this also closes Z5's
open item "verify the heart is irreducible for A9 in char 2". (M is the Steinberg module of SL(2,8).)
Input: see HANDOFF log / code in section (0) appendix below.

**Consequence.** For totally real nonics with group S9/A9 the route (Theorem R + Z5 + A1 window) runs
over L = F with H in {A9, PSL(2,8)}; both have irreducible heart and are 2-transitive, so A1's window
and Lemma N apply verbatim. The A2 gap is closed (for totally real q; for non-totally-real q items 1-3
need a real conjugate in each degree-<=8 subfield, true for odd index only -- not needed here).

## (2) Constructions -- structural candidates

### 2.1 Curves with a deck stabiliser (Z5 open item) -- impossible over F [Proof]
Stab(C) in M = F2^9/<1> is H-stable for C defined over F (Z5 step 1: sigma(eps.C) = sigma(eps).C).
By (0), H in {A9, PSL(2,8)} and M is H-irreducible [Wolfram], so Stab(C) in {0, M}; M is excluded
(Z5). So NO F-curve on V_tau has a nontrivial deck stabiliser; the "Z/2 stabiliser" candidate (Z5 open,
D = C/sigma) only exists for imprimitive H, which (0) shows never occurs for totally real nonics
except H = 1 (all roots already in P).

### 2.2 Special tau (extra symmetry of the 9 points) -- impossible over F [Proof]
Over F-bar, low-genus curves DO exist on special V_tau, e.g.:
- a line M in P^2 through 4 vertices l_1 cap l_2, l_3 cap l_4, l_5 cap l_6, l_7 cap l_8 exists iff an
  involution of Delta = P^1 swaps tau_1<->tau_2, ..., tau_7<->tau_8 (pole of M = centre of the
  involution); then pi^{-1}(M) splits into 16 components, each a (Z/2)^4-cover of P^1 branched at 5
  points: genus 5, degree 128/16 = 8 [Proof]. Through 3 vertices (one collinearity, codim 1): 8 comps
  of (g,e) = (17,16); through 2 vertices (always): 4 comps of (49,32).
These need F-bar structure that is not F-rational: the plane curve D = pi(C) is F-rational, so the
partition of {tau_i} it induces is H-stable; H is primitive, so no such pairing exists. More generally,
a finite group Gamma in PGL_2(F-bar) permuting the tau_i gives Gamma -> S9 normalised by H; for H >= A9
this forces Gamma = 1 (normal subgroups of S9), and for H = PSL(2,8) Gamma cap H normal in a simple
group and C_{S9}(PSL(2,8)) = 1 force Gamma = 1. So no F-rational tau has a symmetric configuration.
(Consistent with Z5: the (5,8) components above have nontrivial-orbit structure and are not F-curves.)

### 2.3 Lines on V_tau, hyperplane sections, residual curves [Proof + Wolfram]
- Two lines l_eps, l_eps' (eps mod +-1) meet iff eps, eps' differ in exactly one coordinate (mod +-1),
  in one point (alpha + beta tau_j = 0) [Proof: equal on >= 2 coordinates and opposite on >= 2 forces 0].
  So the 256 lines form the 9-cube graph mod antipody; l^2 = -5 on V (adjunction, K_V = 3H).
- **64-line hyperplanes [Proof].** For a 3-set T and signs eta on T, the hyperplane
  sum_{i in T} eta_i c_i z_i = 0 with (c_1,c_2,c_3) = (tau_2-tau_3, tau_3-tau_1, tau_1-tau_2) contains every
  l_eps with eps|_T = +-eta: 2^6 = 64 lines = degree 64, so the hyperplane section IS 64 lines.
  336 such hyperplanes over F-bar; none is F-rational (they are permuted by H with T).
- **F-rational hyperplanes contain only l_tau [Proof].** c in K_F (trace pairing) contains l_eps iff
  sum c_i eps_i = sum c_i eps_i tau_i = 0; an F-hyperplane containing one line of weight k >= 1 contains its
  H-orbit, and the vectors (1 - 2*1_S), |S| = k, span F-bar^9, so c = 0. Residual of an F-hyperplane
  through l_tau: class H - L0, (g, e) = (124, 63). Not in the window.
- Line lattice [Wolfram]: Gram of {H, 256 lines} has rank 220 (b2 = 766, h^{1,1} = 544, p_g = 111).
  A9-invariant part: span of H and orbit sums L0..L4 (sizes 1, 9, 36, 84, 126), rank 4, relations
  L4 = 9L0 + 5L1 + 2L2 and L0+..+L4 = 4H (= pi^* Delta, the 256 lines). L4^2 = 0.
  Integral invariant sublattice of the numerical lattice (Smith form, elementary divisors 1,2,4,8,32,96),
  basis as intersection patterns (D.H, D.l_w0, .., D.l_w4):
  (1,-5,1,0,0,0), (-4,-1,3,0,-2,1), (-2,-2,-2,-2,4,-2), (7,4,14,-11,5,-1); Gram in this basis
  {{-5,-1,-2,4},{-1,-80,134,5},{-2,134,-248,-140},{4,5,-140,-803}}.
- **Noether-Lefschetz test [Wolfram; conclusion Heuristic].** Enumerated all invariant classes D with
  1 <= D.H <= 23, D.l >= 0 for every line, and D^2 >= 4 - 3e (p_a >= 3), exhaustively (ellipsoid
  bounding boxes from NMinimize/NMaximize): **none**. Harness check: at e = 63, 64 the search returns
  exactly H - L0 (p_a 124) and H (p_a 129); at e = 40 none.
  So an F-curve in the Class A window must have a class OUTSIDE the saturation-free line lattice:
  it needs Picard classes beyond H and the 256 lines, i.e. a Noether-Lefschetz jump of V_tau (or extra
  algebraic classes present for all tau that I have not identified). With p_g = 111 the NL loci in the
  6-dim tau-moduli have expected codimension up to 111 [Heuristic]. (Caveat: saturation of the lattice
  and, for H = PSL(2,8), finer orbits -- see 2.6 -- could add invariant classes.)


### 2.4 Uniform parametrisation of even-contact curves (covariant candidates) [Proof + count]
Write Q = c(s-x)(s-y), so P^2 = Sym^2 P^1 and D = Gamma/swap, Gamma = {R(x,y) = 0} symmetric of
bidegree (2e,2e); l_i pulls back to {x = tau_i} + {y = tau_i}; m(x) = prod (x - tau_i) (over F, uniform).
- **Lemma [Proof].** If R = A(x,y)^2 - m(x) m(y) B(x,y) with A, B symmetric over F (bidegrees (e,e),
  (2e-9, 2e-9)), then R(tau_i, y) = A(tau_i, y)^2: D has EVEN contact with every l_i, for every tau,
  uniformly. Count: e = 8 gives 45 + 36 = 81 coefficients (80 projective) = 152 - 72, the same as
  "all 16-ics minus the 72 even-contact conditions": this family is (essentially) all even-contact
  curves, now written uniformly over the generic nonic.
- Two obstacles remain, neither automatic:
  (a) genus: generic member has g(D) = 105 (smooth 16-ic; Gamma genus 225, branched at 32 diagonal
      points); (3,8) needs 102 nodes: 80 - 102 = -22, + 6 tau-moduli = -16 (= section (1)).
  (b) lifting: even contact makes the (Z/2)^8 cover over the normalisation D~ unramified; it splits
      completely iff the classes eta_i = (1/2) div(l_i/l_9) in Jac(D~)[2] vanish. Otherwise each
      component has genus 1 + 2^r (g_D - 1) (r = rank of <eta_i>), far outside the window.
  So the uniform family exists, but its window members need ~100 extra singularities: no structural
  reason found to produce them. (Example a = 5, b = 1: e = 5, g_D = 36 generic.)

### 2.5 Fermat-type cover (Y1a 4.3) [Proof]
rho_i = sqrt(c) sigma_i(x) sigma_i(y) with C_0: sigma_i^2 = tau_i - w ((Z/2)^9-cover of P^1 branched at
tau_1..9 and infinity: g(C_0) = 769). Natural curves in C_0 x C_0: diagonal / graphs of deck maps (give the

### 2.6 Natural maps / fibres
F-rational maps from V_tau: pi to P^2 (fibres finite), composites with F-pencils of plane curves.
Fibres = pi^{-1}(plane curve D); low genus requires D in 2.4 with obstacles (a), (b). Quotients

No further F-rational fibrations found [Heuristic].

### 2.7 Summary of (2)
Every structural source of low-genus curves on V_tau that I could identify (deck stabilisers,
symmetric tau, lines and hyperplane residuals, Fermat cover, covariant/even-contact families) is
either impossible over F (because H in {A9, PSL(2,8)} is primitive with irreducible F2-heart) or lands
far outside the window. The (5,8)/(17,16) curves of 2.2 show the window IS populated over F-bar on

## (5) PSL(2,8) / PGammaL(2,8) side task [Wolfram + Proof]
- Z5's "irreducibility fails there" is wrong: the F2-heart for PSL(2,8) on P^1(F8) is the Steinberg
  module, irreducible (verified: every nonzero sum-zero vector has orbit span of rank 8, section (0)).
  So Theorem Z5 ((n-8)e <= 4g-4), the stabiliser argument 2.1, Lemma N (sharply 3-transitive) and A1's
  window apply verbatim to PSL(2,8) and PGammaL(2,8) nonics.
- Orbits of PSL(2,8) and of PGammaL(2,8) on the 256 lines l_eps: sizes 1, 9, 36, 84, 126 -- identical
  to A9 [Wolfram] (3-transitive on 3-sets, transitive on the 126 4-sets). So the invariant line lattice
  and the NL test of 2.3 are identical: no Class A class.
- 2.2 also holds (C_{S9}(PSL(2,8)) = 1). So V_tau's structure over F is NOT different for these groups;
  no low-genus shortcut there [Proof for the listed mechanisms].
- PGammaL(2,8) = PSL(2,8):3: the cubic resolvent field is in P, and over it the group is PSL(2,8),
  consistent with (0).
