# W2: two-graph and big-F engines at n = 10, 11, 12

Stream W2 of `pptc/PLAN-algebraic.md` (wave 3). Labels: [Proof], [Wolfram], [Heuristic], [Open]. Backend: Wolfram for every
number (no Python). Every obstruction is scoped to the route it blocks; nothing here concludes a negative about the target.
No `.lean` edits.

Notation. K = Q[x]/(q), n = deg q, s = number of conjugate pairs, chi_tau = characteristic polynomial of tau in K,
p_k = Tr(tau^k). A crossing polynomial N must be divisible by chi_tau (overshoot allowed, cofactor of degree D - n).

## Findings summary

1. **The three families are nested [Proof].**
   - (C) H3's F = 13 family and (A) H1's two-graph engine are the same family: a rational sextic graph y = G(x) against an affine
     image of a rational sextic graph reduces to N(u) = mu*u - Gt(A(u)) (H3's algebra), and (A) is the case where the second
     curve is the exact 90 degree transpose x = R(y) (so N = u - P(R(u)), the fixed-point equation tau = R(P(tau))).
   - (B) H3's F = 9 family is (A) with deg P = 2. In plane terms it is **a sideways parabola x = (y/E)^2 against a rational
     sextic graph y = H(x)**, with N(u) = H(u^2) - E*u, crossing point (X, Y) = (u^2, E*u). Shifting u adds one parameter.
     So B is the only one of the three whose degree (12) is close to n.
2. **Exact ranks [Wolfram, exact Jacobian rank at a random integer point].**
   | family | parameters | rank | rational-only columns | P-capable columns |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | A: x - R(P(x)), deg 36 | P, R: 7 + 7, all rational | **12** (monic map; 13 as a cone) | 12 | none (90 degree rotation is exact) |
   | C: Y - G(X), X = a1 u + a2 H + a0, Y = b1 u + b2 H + b0, deg 36 | G, H: 7 + 7 rational; 6 affine entries | **13** (cone) | G+H alone: 12 | affine data adds exactly **1** (overall scale of N) |
   | B: Q(u) - G(P(u)), deg 12 | G: 7 rational; P, Q quadratics: 6 | **9** (cone, includes the u-shift) | G alone: 7 | P,Q alone: 6; adds the u-shift and scale only |
   The A rank 12 is the number H1 left unchecked: it equals 14 parameters minus the 2-dimensional affine reparametrisation
   (P, R) -> (M o P, R o M^-1). The extra P-valued affine data in C buys one dimension (a scale, irrelevant to divisibility),
   and in B buys the shift and a scale; by the kernel argument in section 1 these must in effect be rational for rational tau.
3. **Conditions on tau.**
   - A and C: n + 1 - 13 = n - 12, i.e. **none for n = 10, 11, 12** over C. Verified directly: the Jacobian of
     (R(P(tau_i)) - tau_i)_{i<=n} with respect to (P, R) has full rank n for n = 8, 10, 11, 12 at a random point and at the real point
     P = R = T6 with n of its 36 real roots, and saturates at 12 for n = 13 [Wolfram]. So the incidence variety submerses onto
     K tensor C for n <= 12. All the cost is in the *arithmetic*: a rational point of the 14-dimensional incidence variety I_K,
     which has a trivial component (P linear, R its inverse, tau free: dimension n + 2) and a nontrivial one.
   - B: n - 8 conditions = **2, 3, 4** at n = 10, 11, 12, cut out in the trace-zero hyperplane by odd power-sum identities
     (section 3). Locus degrees **420 (n = 10), 945 (n = 11), 945 (n = 12)** [Wolfram].
4. **Realness.**
   - A, C: capacity 36 real roots. x - T6(T6(x)) = x - T36(x) has 36 real simple roots, and the Jacobian above has rank n at any n of them,
     so an open set of real tau in R^n (n <= 12) is reached: **no realness obstruction for totally real q at n <= 12** [Wolfram + implicit
     function theorem]. The obstruction is again arithmetic.
   - B: Descartes capacity of {0,1,2,4,...,12} is 12 [Proof], attained: x - T6(2x^2 - 1) = x - T12(x) has 12 real roots [Wolfram].
     p_2 is unconstrained in B, so the totally real trace-form obstruction (p_2 > 0) of the `y^m = sextic` shapes does not apply.
5. **Searches (section 5).** H5-style LLL search adapted to B's power-sum conditions: **no hit** on S_10 (nonreal s = 1, totally real) and S_11
   (s = 5) in the first LLL box. A positive control (a field accidentally in the B family) is found immediately, so the code works.
   For A and C there is no linearization and nothing to search with: the naive heuristic exponents are hugely negative.
   **Not a first.** No rational tau on a field outside the engine family was found; the totally real n = 10 and any n = 11 targets stay [Open].
6. **Constructive existence (section 6), not a Tschirnhaus hit.** B-engine fields exist that are S_10, S_11, S_12 by Frobenius types (Jordan),
   both totally real and with nonreal roots. So the engine step does carry S_n for n = 10, 11, 12, and totally real S_n up to n = 12 [Wolfram].
   This shows the Aₙ filter is passed by one crossing of a sideways parabola with a sextic graph; it says nothing about arbitrary K.
7. **Recovery for D > n (section 7).** Explicit y-windows for every B instance, and a stress test (D = 36, n = 12, min root gap 4.2e-4).
   Windows are plain rational intervals; they exist exactly when the root of N is simple and real.

Net verdict, scoped: none of the three gives a cheap rational engine **by search** at n = 10-12. A and C leave no conditions over C but
turn the problem into rational points on a 14-fold with no linear structure found. B leaves 2, 3, 4 conditions of degree 420 to 945
with heuristic exponent -12 to -13, against +4 / +1 / -3 for the sextic power-law engine at n = 10 / 11 / 12.

## 1. Parameters, rationality, and what P-data can do

**Reduction [Proof].** curve 1: y = G(x), G rational of degree <= 6 (a `poly_graph`). Curve 2: M(graph of H), M a P-affine map (6 entries),
H rational of degree <= 6. Eliminating H, N(u) = Y(u) - G(X(u)) with X = a1 u + a2 H + a0, Y = b1 u + b2 H + b0 collapses
(Y = lambda X + mu u + nu) to N = mu u - Gt(A(u)), Gt = G - lambda X - nu, A = X. When M is the exact transpose, A = R and N = u - P(R(u)).

**Which parameters are rational.** `poly_graph` coefficients are rational: G, H (and P, R in A). Rotation by a P-angle, scales and shifts of
the plane are P-valued (C), but the measured ranks above show they contribute **one** dimension in C (scale) and the shift/scale in B.

**Why P-valued scale and shift do not create rational-tau solutions by themselves [Proof sketch].** Take tau rational in K and the shape
vectors w_e = tau^e (e in the support E). The dependency space {c in F^E : sum c_e w_e = 0} is the kernel of a *rational* matrix, hence
defined over Q. The rational coefficients (h_j, E) of B must lie in it. An irrational scale lambda multiplies h_j by lambda^(2j), forcing
lambda^2 rational (take j = 0 and 1); an irrational shift t0 must be a common root of n rational polynomials, so the same gcd as the
rational case. P-data therefore only change *where the algebraic number sits*, not the conditions on tau. Lemma A (H2) gives a P/F_{<n} point
only for loci of degree < n, and 420, 945 > n.

## 3. B in power sums, degree, smoothness [Wolfram, Proof of the reformulation]

N = chi_(tau - t0) * c with c = u^2 + c1 u + c0 (n = 10), c = u - gamma (n = 11), c = 1 (n = 12) has no u^k term for odd k = 3,...,11. The
twelve roots have e_1 = e_3 = e_5 = e_7 = e_9 = 0, equivalently the odd power sums p_k(rho) + P_k(cofactor roots) = 0 for k = 1, 3, 5, 7, 9,
with rho = tau - t0. With tau_bar the trace-zero part:

- n = 12: p_3 = p_5 = p_7 = p_9 = 0 on the 11-dimensional trace-zero space (this is the odd power-sum complete intersection).
- n = 11: gamma = 11 t0 from k = 1; four equations of degrees 3, 5, 7, 9 in (tau_bar, t0).
- n = 10: g1 = 10 t0 from k = 1, g2 from k = 3, three further equations in (tau_bar, t0).

**Degree of the locus** (number of points on a generic linear section of the cone of codimension-matched dimension, Groebner basis mod p,
standard monomials counted, zero-dimensional in every case) [Wolfram]:

| n | unknowns on the plane | solutions |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | (g1, g2, t0, s, t) | **420** (seed 11, p = 32003 gave 419; seed 77, p = 65521 gave 420; 419 read as a non-generic plane) |
| 11 | (gamma, t0, s, t, u) | **945** |
| 12 | (t0, s, t, u, w) | **945** |

945 = 3*5*7*9 is the Bezout number, so for n = 11, 12 the generic slice is a **reduced complete intersection with no solutions at infinity**
[Wolfram]: the cone W-bar (dimension 7 in the trace-zero space; W = W-bar + shift has dimension 8) is generically reduced. For n = 10 the count 420 is
below the weighted Bezout number 472.5, so the elimination is not a complete intersection in those weights. Global smoothness is not established [Open].

Low-degree structure: none found. The only known sublocus is tau_bar with +-pairs (chi even), where all odd power sums vanish
trivially (dimension n/2, imprimitive tau, useless for a primitive K) [Proof]. No lines are expected [Heuristic]: degree 420 to 945 in
dimension 7.

**A side effect worth recording [Proof].** At n = 10 the B conditions involve e_1, ..., e_9 only; the constant term e_10 of chi never appears.
So if one B-engine polynomial exists, so does the whole family chi - delta. This is why accidental hits appear in section 5.

## 4. Fixed-sigma slices of A and C [Wolfram]

Fix sigma = x (any generator of K may be called x). Then tau = R(x) with R of degree <= 6 mod affine (a P^5) and the condition
"x in span(1, tau, ..., tau^6)" has codimension n - 7, expected slice dimension 12 - n (a surface at n = 10, a curve at n = 11, points at n = 12).
Porteous degree with excess h_{n-7}(0,0,1,...,6): **21, 266, 2646, 22827, 179487, 1323652** for n = 8,...,13.
At n = 8 the single equation is det = t^2 * (irreducible of degree 19) on a random line: the trivial point R = id has multiplicity 2, and
the nontrivial part is irreducible of degree 19 (no projection from the trivial point rationalises it). A scan of all integer R in
[-4, 4]^5 (59049 candidates) at n = 8 returns only the trivial point. Nothing suggests rational nontrivial points at n = 9, 10, 11, 12.

## 5. Searches

**Method (H5 adapted).** q monic in Z[x], roots to 60-150 digits. Trace-zero sublattice by NullSpace, LLL in the Minkowski metric (real
embeddings and sqrt2 Re, sqrt2 Im; weight 10^7..10^12 on the embedding block). Basis norms: H5's s = 1 field 4.5-5.7 and 33.9 (reproduced);
n = 11, s = 5 field 3.5-71.8; totally real fields q = prod(x - a_i) - 1: 100 to 4400 for random a.
Enumerate c in {-1,0,1}^(n-1) up to sign. For each vector compute the power sums S_2, ..., S_9 numerically and the polynomials in t0 for
k = 5, 7, 9 (n = 10; coefficients derived symbolically in S_j with the cofactor roots eliminated), find the real roots of the first, test the others
at relative 1e-9 (1e-6 in the first runs), and verify survivors exactly (gcd in t0 of the exact integer polynomials). About 2 ms per vector.
Unit test of the formulas: at the engine field below the residuals are 0 to 40 digits at the true t0.

## 7. Recovery when D > n

Rule [Proof]. For a B hit, N has degree 12 with cofactor degree 12 - n. Work in the plane coordinate Y = E u, which is injective in u.
The crossing in the window [x_lo, x_hi] x [y_lo, y_hi] is unique iff exactly one real root u_k of N has E u_k in [y_lo, y_hi]
(the x-range just needs to contain X = u^2; take [0, 1 + max u^2]). Choose the endpoints as rational midpoints between consecutive real roots
of N (rational approximation to 1/100 suffices when the gaps are at least 0.1). Then `restrict` on both curves leaves one crossing, `inter_y`
gives Y, and u = Y / E. Roots of the cofactor are handled by the same windows. If the root is not simple, use a window containing no other real root;
only the target root being isolated matters.

For a Tschirnhaus hit tau the root y0 = tau(beta) is isolated this way and beta follows by gcd(q(x), tau(x) - y0) [H3 finding 1].

## 8. What would change the verdict

- A, C: a rational parametrization or a thin-set-free structure of the 14-fold I_K (unirationality of the n-fold fibre product twist at n = 10, 11, 12), or P-level
  elimination with degree < n. None found: the trivial point has multiplicity 2 and the nontrivial slice at n = 8 is irreducible of degree 19.
- B: points on a degree 420 / 945 variety of dimension 7; no applicable form-in-many-variables theorem (variables 9-11 against degrees up to 9).
- n = 11 power-law engine with exponent +1 (see section 5).

## Other things noticed

- H1's 14 rational parameters for A reduce to 12 by the (P, R) -> (M o P, R o M^-1) action; rank 12 is exact, so H1's guess was right.
- A is nested in C and B in A, so any hit for B is automatically a hit for A and C; the converse needs deg P > 2.
- B at n = 12 has the same odd power-sum shape as N2/H1's odd engine (degrees 3, 5, 7, 9), with the Bezout number 945; B is the n = 12 member of the odd-type supports.
- x - T6(T6(x)) = x - T36(x) factors into cyclotomic-type pieces; its real locus is an open smooth piece of I for all n <= 12 and is a convenient
  test bed for the recovery windows (min gap 4.2e-4, so windows have denominators of order 1000).
- The search code is ours to reuse: the power-sum polynomials in t0 are degree 6 / 9 / 12 (n = 10, after clearing denominators 18, 2700, 9000), and the
  cofactor-root elimination makes the cost per vector about 2 ms in the Wolfram kernel.
- The accidental positive controls came from constructing the totally real S_10 field as prod(x - a_i) - 1 with additively structured a; choose a randomly.
- 945 = 3*5*7*9 appears both here (n = 11, 12) and in H1's odd-shape table, but H1's 10395 uses factors up to 11 at n = 13.
