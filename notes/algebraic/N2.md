# N2: rational nonics, arithmetic routes (s = 0, 1, 2)

Labels: [Proof], [Wolfram], [Heuristic], [Open]. Obstructions are scoped to routes, never to the target.
Notation: K = Q[x]/(q), tau in K (or K tensor L), chi_tau its characteristic polynomial, e_k / p_k its
elementary symmetric functions / power sums, s = number of conjugate pairs.

## Findings summary

1. **Rational nonics with s >= 1 are already closed in the repo [Proof, existing].**
   `root_Pconstructible_of_rat_nonic_nonreal` (Nonic/Nonic.lean) covers every q in Q[X] of degree 9 with a
   nonreal root, so s = 1 and s = 2 included. Why the rational case escapes the Descartes obstruction of
   THREE-PAIRS section 3: with rational tau the resolvent is rational, so the engine is `y^9 = sextic(y)`
   with a *rational* sextic (`poly_graph`, degree <= 6), and only X^8, X^7 must vanish, i.e. centred
   p_2 = 0 (p_1 = 0 by centring). Meyer (trace form indefinite once a nonreal root exists, rank 6 slice) gives
   such a tau. Realness is automatic (tau rational, so y = tau(beta) is real); the only "obstruction" is that
   p_2 = 0 forces s >= 1 (a sum of squares), which is exactly the hypothesis. The PLAN table row
   "P with s in {1,2}: open" is therefore about *P* coefficients (only quartic graphs drawable, so p_1..p_4 = 0).
   **The remaining rational case is s = 0 (totally real) only.**
2. **Odd engine at n = 9, exact conditions [Proof + Wolfram].** The engine needs chi_tau = t*Q(t^2) - c, Q monic
   quartic, i.e. e_1 = e_3 = e_5 = e_7 = 0, equivalently p_1 = p_3 = p_5 = p_7 = 0 (Newton, n = 9, checked in
   Wolfram: setting p_1,p_3,p_5,p_7 = 0 with p_even free kills e_1,e_3,e_5,e_7; the converse is the standard
   weight/parity induction). c = e_9 != 0 automatically when q is irreducible (K a field, tau != 0). Q has P
   coefficients, drawable by Lemma Q; `c` enters through `scale_y`. Recovery: alpha is a root of tau(x) - y,
   degree <= 8. Variety V = {S_1=S_3=S_5=S_7=0} in P^8, CI of degrees (1,3,5,7), dimension 4, Sigma deg = 16 > 9.
3. **New: overshoot engines cut the odd route from 4 conditions to 2 for rational tau [Proof of the algebra;
   Wolfram examples; Lean engine not written].** Details in section 2.
4. **Partial classes** (section 4): reducible; degree-3 subfield; every solvable Galois group; Tschirnhaus-class of
   the overshoot family. Remaining transitive groups not reached by towers of steps <= 8: exactly
   PSL(2,8), PGammaL(2,8), A9, S9 [Proof modulo the classification of transitive groups of degree 9].
5. **Experiments** (section 5): S9 totally real nonic with an explicit rational overshoot factorization;
   box search for W-points in a fixed S9 field finds only the degenerate point.
6. No ℚ-point or low-degree point on V or W was found for a given totally real S9 field. [Open]. No negative
   about the target is claimed.

## 1. Meyer / Hasse-Minkowski side (sub-question b)

- [Proof, existing] s >= 1, rational q: p_1 = p_2 = 0 suffices because rational sextics are drawable.
  Nothing further is needed for s = 1, 2.
- [Proof] Descartes for the P-coefficient engines (quartic graph, support size 6). Capacity of a support
  = sum over adjacent exponent gaps of (1 if the gap is odd, 2 if even), an upper bound on the number of real
  nonzero roots (V(N) + V(N(-t))). Computed (Wolfram) for all engines `t^k * G(t^n) - a t^m`, n <= 4,
  G of degree 4 (P) or 6 (rational), degree >= 9, constant term present:
  - `y^9 = quartic` and its reciprocal: capacity 5 (needs s >= 2, as in THREE-PAIRS).
  - Only n = 2 reaches capacity 9 with a quartic: the odd engine (k=1, m=0) and the even engine
    (k=0, m=9, conditions e_2=e_4=e_6=e_8=0). Both cost 4 conditions. **So for P-coefficient s = 1 (7 real
    roots) the shape must be odd or even.** Odd has no quadric condition; the even engine has the single quadric
    e_2 = 0, form Tr(t^2) - Tr(t)^2 of signature (8,1) for s = 0 (indefinite, so Meyer gives isotropic rational
    vectors), but e_4, e_6, e_8 remain, of degrees 4, 6, 8. Meyer therefore settles only e_2.
  - With a rational *sextic*, any integer power law has capacity <= 8 (7 sextic terms + one monomial: at most
    2 + 6 = 8). So no `y^m = sextic` with integer m hosts a totally real nonic; translating the power law by
    a P number does not change this (substitute u = x - h).
- [Heuristic] Extending the rational sextic engine to q with coefficients in a number field F fails as stated:
  chi_tau has F coefficients, not Q. Open whether a descent (tau with rational chi) exists.

## 2. Overshoot engines (new)

Idea: the crossing polynomial N need not have degree 9; it only has to be divisible by chi_tau. Then N = chi_tau*rho
with a cofactor rho found by *linear algebra* over the base field, and the root beta of chi_tau is an isolated
crossing (choose the `restrict` window). If the drawable family of N's is a linear space with F free
coefficients (support size F) in degree d', the conditions on tau are 10 - F, independent of d' [Proof,
dimension count: the space chi*rho has dim d'-8, the family has dim F, ambient d'+1].

- Rational graphs reach F = 7 + 1 = 8, so **2 conditions** (quartic P graphs: F = 6, 4 conditions, as before).
- Valid totally-real-capable supports with F = 8 [Wolfram table]: (a) odd: `N = t*P6(t^2) - a`, degree 13, cofactor
  rho of degree 4; (b) even: `N = a t^13 - P6(t^2)`; (c) `N = P6(t^2) - a t^m`, m odd <= 11, degree 12, rho cubic.
  Capacity 12-13 >= 9, no Descartes obstruction. (Rows with m even and k=0 are degenerate: N even.)
- Condition for (a): rho (deg <= 4, 5 unknowns) must make the even coefficients t^12..t^2 of chi*rho vanish:
  a 6 x 5 linear system, rank <= 4. Equivalently: **1, tau, tau^3, ..., tau^13 are linearly dependent over Q in K**
  (eight vectors in a 9-dim space). Write chi = chi_e(t^2) + t chi_o(t^2), rho likewise:
  chi_e rho_e + x chi_o rho_o = const.
- Locus W_O in P(K) = P^8: Porteous, codim 2, dimension 6, degree h_2(0,1,3,5,7,9,11,13) = 1428 [Wolfram:
  h_2 = 1428]. Variant (c) with m = 1: columns {0,1,2,4,...,12}, degree 1107. Real points: with
  N = T_13(t) - 1/3 (13 real roots) and any 9 of its roots, the 9 x 8 matrix [t^{2j+1}, 1] has rank 7 [Wolfram,
  singular values], so real points of W exist in every totally real K (no sign obstruction); real affine
  dimension 7 (Jacobian = diag(1/N') * M, kernel = the scaling).
- Needs tau with *rational* coordinates (so N is rational and poly_graph of degree 6 applies). The tau = 1
  point (constants) lies on W and is useless (recovery needs tau nonconstant).
- Counting principle for other n [Heuristic]: conditions = n + 1 - F. Rational graphs of degree <= 6 give
  F = 8: n = 7 unconditional, n = 8 one condition, n = 9 two. If rational graphs of degree 8 were drawable
  (F = 10), every rational nonic would be unconditional (N = t*P8(t^2) - a; Bezout between chi(t) and chi(-t)
  gives rho of degree <= 8 for any chi coprime to chi(-t)) [Proof of the algebra]. This ties to PLAN 2.4:
  the quantity that matters is the number of free *rational* coefficients drawable in a sparse linear family.
- Other free parameters do not help rational tau: translating the power law by h gives P(t^2+h) with ℚ(h)
  coefficients, which is no longer a rational sextic; the shear/translation freedom (b x + k) gives P
  coefficients on only two terms and makes the rational-coefficient conditions on N worse (11 equations on 5
  unknowns) [Proof by counting].

## 3. Odd route for s = 0 with number-field structure (sub-question a)

Setting: tau in K tensor L, L reached by steps of degree <= 8, with Tr_{KL/L}(tau^k) = 0 for k = 1,3,5,7.
- [Proof] The trace form on K is positive definite when K is totally real; every quadric trick (rank >= 5
  indefinite) is unavailable for p_2 and irrelevant to p_3, p_5, p_7 (odd degrees).
- [Proof] The shift-invariant form of the odd condition lives on P(K/Q) = P^7: CI (3,5,7), degree 105. The even
  engine with a P-translation h costs the same number (3 conditions after solving the quadratic
  e_2(tau-h) = 0 for h, which has real solutions iff 9 p_2 >= p_1^2) but has degree 4*6*8*2 = 384 on a double cover.
  No gain.
- [Proof] Pairing locus (tau_i = -tau_pi(i)) needs a nontrivial centralizer of the Galois group in S_9 on the roots,
  so it has no rational points for S_9 (A_9 filter of NOTES); lines through such points are excluded too.
- [Proof] tau = f(alpha) with deg f = d gives P^{d-1} after centring, with 3 equations of degrees 3,5,7: expected
  dimension d - 4, so d >= 4 is needed, and then a slice has up to 105 points (generically S_105, outside any
  <= 8 tower). Subfields: none (K has prime-to-8 structure for S_9: no proper subfield, no automorphisms).
- [Heuristic] V and W are of general type or close to it (Sigma d_i = 16 > 9; deg W = 1428), so rational points of
  bounded height are not expected beyond degenerate loci (circle-method exponent 9 - 16 < 0 for V).
  [Open]: points of V or W over fields reachable by <= 8 steps, or over P (transcendental coordinates are allowed,
  but fixing 4 coordinates freely just reproduces a degree-105 system over the function field).
- Computation of W: unirational *as a family of nonics* (fix rho, then chi is an affine 3-dimensional linear
  solution space; total 7 = 4 + 3). So the class of nonics solved is large, but asking that a *given* K contain such a
  tau is the 2-condition problem above.

## 5. Wolfram experiments (sub-question d)

- **Explicit totally real S9 nonic in the overshoot family [Wolfram].** With
  rho = t^4 - 2t^3 - 23t^2 - 19t - 1 and
  q = t^9 + 2t^8 - (96485202/1231) t^7 - (192890389/1231) t^6 + (2222772064/1231) t^5 - 1481845 t^4 - 1350 t^3
  + 65782 t^2 - 2955 t - 419,
  the product q*rho equals t^13 - (96518439/1231) t^11 + ... + 419: **only odd powers plus the constant**
  (checked). q has 9 real roots (Sturm) and is irreducible. Frobenius: type (1,8) at p=7, (9) at p=13, (4,5) at p=17,
  (2,7) at p=47. A degree-9 transitive group containing a 5-cycle is primitive (5 does not divide 1296) and
  contains A_9 (Jordan); the 8-cycle is odd, so the group is S_9. So this overshoot engine solves a totally real
  S_9 rational nonic with tau = alpha. [Wolfram + Proof of the group step.] (Coefficients denominators: scale by 1231.)
  Random search found totally real members rarely (3 of about 1000 samples with larger parameters); this family is
  not dense in height.
- **Fixed field test.** q_1 = (x-1)...(x-9) - 1 is totally real and S9 (Frobenius types (1,8) at p=19, (4,5) at p=17).
  Exhaustive search over tau with coordinates in {-1,0,1}^9 (normalised, 9841 vectors), rank of
  [tau, tau^3, ..., tau^13, 1] mod 1000003: the only hit is tau = 1. [Wolfram] This is a box search, not a statement
  about W(Q); the heuristic above predicts nothing more at this height.
- Dimension checks: W real affine dimension 7 (rank 7 of M at points of W from T_13 roots); Newton equivalence of
  odd conditions verified symbolically; Porteous degrees 1428 / 1107.
- Not done: dimensions of the Tschirnhaus varieties for P-points over small fields (a degree-105 slice calculation
  would be an S_105 equation; skipped as it cannot lie in any tower).

## 6. What would close the rational totally real case

- A P-point of W (2 conditions), from a source of structure not yet found; or
- more free rational coefficients in a sparse totally-real-capable family (F = 10), e.g. a way to draw an
  odd/even rational polynomial graph of degree 7-8 or to supply two extra independent free coefficients
  with rational (not just P) meaning; the offset/affine towers of N4 should be scored by exactly this count;
- or any condition-lowering by cofactors for the dense engines of NOTES (the conditions-versus-solving trade-off).

## Other things noticed

- The PLAN section 1 table says "P with s in {1,2}; any totally real: open". For ℚ coefficients only s = 0 is open;
  worth stating in the summary.
- Overshoot principle (conditions = n + 1 - F) is probably useful for N4/G3: re-score every sparse engine by the
  number of free coefficients F rather than by the vanishing coefficients of a degree-n crossing polynomial. The
  NOTES census counts conditions for N of degree exactly n and so overstates cost for sparse linear families.
- Rows (n=2, k=0, m even) in the support enumeration are degenerate: N even forces degree >= 18.
- NOTES-general "Descartes: at most about 10 real roots" for quartic sparse shapes: the exact bound is the gap
  formula above (5 for y^9 = quartic, 8 for any integer power law times a sextic).
