# N4: new engines from offsets, rational-exponent power laws, box readouts, towers

Stream N4 of `pptc/PLAN-algebraic.md`. Labels: [Proof], [Wolfram], [Heuristic], [Open]. Nothing here
concludes a negative about the target; every obstruction is scoped to the route it blocks.
Backend: Wolfram for every number below (no Python used). Sample sizes are stated.

## Findings summary

**Bookkeeping used throughout.** For a family `W` of crossing polynomials (monic, degree 9, in the
root variable `t`), let `W~` be its closure under `t -> t + b` (Tschirnhaus shift is free).
`r = rank` of the Jacobian of (parameters + shift) -> monic nonic coefficients. Then

> conditions on the Tschirnhaus element = `9 - r`.

This reproduces the census: `y^9 = quartic` has `r = 6` (3 conditions), the odd shape has `r = 6`,
two cubic Béziers `r = 8` (1 condition), `y = x^3` x Bézier `r = 7` [Wolfram, control rerun: 7].

1. **Descartes gap theorem for engines [Proof].** If the crossing polynomial, in the engine's own
   root variable, has two consecutive present exponents at distance >= 3, it has fewer than 9 real
   roots. (Count sign changes of `P(t)` and `P(-t)`: an adjacent pair at gap `g` contributes at
   most 2 if `g` is even, at most 1 if odd, so the total is at most `sum w(g_i) < sum g_i = 9`
   unless every gap is 1 or 2.) Consequently a *sparse* (lacunary) shape serves a totally real
   nonic only if its support has all gaps <= 2, hence at least 6 terms. A support with `m` terms has `9 - m` conditions, so the best sparse
   totally real shape has 3 conditions (more terms give fewer, but are not sparse). Real-rootedness is shift invariant, so the test may be applied in any variable
   where the support is sparse, including the Möbius image `t -> 1/t` (support `k -> 9-k`).
   p2 = 0 is forced exactly when both `t^8` and `t^7` are absent; the gap condition already excludes
   that, so for these shapes "avoid p2 = 0" and "pass Descartes" coincide.
2. **Complete list of totally-real-compatible sparse supports of degree 9 [Wolfram enumeration]:**
   55 supports with `0, 9` present and all gaps <= 2; 30 up to reversal (6 terms: 3, 7 terms: 10,
   8 terms: 12, 9 terms: 4, 10 terms: 1). The 6-term classes (3 conditions) are exactly
   - **A** `{9,7,5,3,1,0}`: the odd engine (census row 2);
   - **B** `{9,8,6,4,2,0}`: reversal of A (`t -> 1/t`), i.e. `power_law 9/2` x quartic graph;
   - **C** `{9,7,5,4,2,0}`: self-reversed, **new shape**.
3. **Which supports power laws x graphs reach [Wolfram enumeration, p,q <= 9, graph degree d <= 6,
   aligned frames, signed exponents]:** of 42 degree-9 configurations only two have all gaps <= 2:
   `y = a x^(-1/2)` x quartic (A) and `y = a x^(9/2)` x quartic (B). B is A under `t -> 1/t`, so
   the rational-exponent route adds **no new engine** beyond the census odd engine. Class C is not
   reached by an aligned power-law x graph. [Open]: whether a non-aligned or composite crossing
   realizes C (conditions would again be 3).
4. **Power law x (shifted, sheared) power law, aligned, `(a t^p - e t^q - y0)^q' = c (t^q - x0)^p'`
   [Wolfram]:** 69 degree-9 configurations; Jacobian rank (5 parameters + shift) is 3 (24 cases),
   4 (6), or 5 (39); i.e. **>= 4 conditions**, and **none** has a gap-<=2 support, so none can serve
   a totally real nonic [Proof by item 1]. Dominated.
5. **New engine N4-1: cubic Bézier x Neile parabola `y^2 = x^3`.** Crossing polynomial
   `y(s)^2 - x(s)^3` (degree 9, i.e. `R = M^3 - y^2`).
   - Rank 7 at two random rational points [Wolfram, exact], so **2 conditions**; same count as
     `y = x^3` x Bézier (control rerun, rank 7). No hidden collapse (the scaling `(x,y) -> (l^2 x,
     l^3 y)` only rescales the polynomial, which monic normalization already removes).
   - Degrees: no polynomial relation among the centered coefficients `e2..e9` of weighted degree
     <= 17 (mod p, 252 monomials; the control `y - x^3` shows its known weight-4 and weight-5
     relations at once). So **both conditions have degree >= 18 in tau**, versus (4, 5) for `M^3 + v`.
     [Wolfram; weights 18+ timed out.]
   - Realness: Newton found a Bézier with 9 distinct real roots
     `-2.408, -2.116, -1.760, -1.196, -0.946, 0.297, 1.922, 2.293, 2.390`
     [Wolfram, 1 of 120 starts converged]. **Serves s = 0.**
   - Sub-family cubic graph x affine Neile (or x affine `y = x^3`): rank 6 (3 conditions) [Wolfram].
6. **New engine N4-2: parabola offset x conic through 3 known offset points.** The offset of
   `y = x^2` is a rational sextic (parameter `u`, `t = (u^2-1)/(4u)`, `w = (u^2+1)/(2u)`); a
   general conic meets it in 12 points (degree 12 in `t` after rationalization), and a conic through
   three offset points `O(u_i)` (P-constructible since `u_i in P`) leaves a nonic.
   - Parameters `u1,u2,u3, d`, 2 conic parameters, plus shift: Jacobian rank **7 of 7** at a random
     point (singular values 119 ... 0.009, 120-digit finite differences) [Wolfram], so **2
     conditions**, and the family has no hidden collapse.
   - Degrees: no relation of weighted degree <= 14 among centered `e2..e9` (mod p, 123 monomials),
     so conditions have **degree >= 15** [Wolfram].
   - Realness: **9 real roots occurs** (3 of 400 random parameter draws; every real `t` gives a real
     point on the correct sheet) [Wolfram]. **Serves s = 0.**
   - Why no more parameters: parabolas are all similar, so the only shape parameter is `d`.
7. **Offset Bézier x line through a known offset point (census row 6) is now measured:** rank **7**
   of 11 parameters (cubic Bézier 8, `d`, `sigma0`, line direction), so **2 conditions**, matching
   the census estimate; the cut is exactly the similarity group (4) [Wolfram, 150-digit differences].
   Realness: in 500 random draws the degree-10 squared polynomial had at most 8 real roots (so at
   most 7 real roots of the nonic); a totally real nonic was **not observed** [Wolfram sample; not a
   proof either way, [Open]].
8. **Offset degree table [Wolfram, Groebner elimination, `w = |grad F|` saturated]**, degree `D` of
   the two-sided implicit offset:

   | base curve | parabola | `y=x^3` or sheared | Neile `y^2=x^3` | `y^3=x^4` | ellipse | `y=x^4` | `y^2=x^5` | cubic Bézier |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | `D` | 6 | 10 | 8 | 10 | 8 | 14 | 16 | 10 (census) |

   Nonic extraction needs `D*e - (known points) = 9` for a partner of degree `e`: a line (`e=1`)
   through one known offset point with `D = 10`; a conic (`e = 2`) through three with `D = 6`. Those are
   the two cases above plus the `y=x^3`/`y^3=x^4` offsets, which have far fewer parameters
   (shape + `d` + line + shift: <= 6 parameters, so >= 3 conditions, probably 4 after the similarity
   quotient [Heuristic]). Circle partners pass through the circular points and give even counts,
   so they need a known point too.
9. **Affine-offset towers, depth 2 [Wolfram].** `Off_{d2}(A(Off_{d1}(parabola)))`, `A` a generic
   non-similar affine map: generic-line section degree **16** in the `u` parametrization (the
   numerator of degree 28 contains a repeated degree-6 factor, which I read as spurious clearing of
   denominators; either way D2 >= 16). A line can contain only two freely chosen known points, so
   **no nonic is extractable from a line at depth 2**; conics give `2*16 - 5 = 27`. This blocks
   depth-2 towers *as nonic engines by Bézout counting* (scoped: it does not exclude partner curves
   with special multiplicities, nor engines of other degrees). Parameters grow by 7 per level while
   `D` grows faster, so the tower does not supply a bounded-cost family [Heuristic, one data point].
10. **Box / extremum engines (c) give nothing of degree 9.**
    - [Proof sketch] At a smooth point the tangent of `Off_d(gamma)` is parallel to `gamma'`:
      `x_off'(s) = (1 - d kappa) x'(s)`. So an extremum of an affine coordinate on any offset tower
      whose affine maps are applied at some stage is a tangent-direction condition on the *base*
      curve, with the offset only adding `+-d` times a unit-normal component. Equations: Bézier,
      quadratic; graph of degree `m`, degree `m - 1 <= 5`; power law `x^b`, binomial
      `u + v a b x^(b-1) = 0`. All of degree <= 5, inside the existing <= 8 steps.
    - New extrema occur at **cusps** of an offset (`1 - d kappa = 0`, x turns around). For a cubic
      Bézier the cusp equation `S^3 = d^2 K^2` has degree 12 (`S` quartic, `K` quadratic). For a random
      Bézier: irreducible over Q, 2 real roots, and mod-p factor types include `1+11`, `2+10`, `5+7`,
      `3+9`, `1+4+7`: it contains an 11-cycle and elements of order 35 and 28, so it is not inside `M12`
      or `PSL2(11)` and contains `A12` [Wolfram, 40 primes; Heuristic for the group]. Cusp points are
      points of the evolute `E(s)`, rational in `s`. This is a degree-12, not degree-9, readout:
      it does not carry `A9` as an engine unless a cubic factor can be forced [Open].
11. **Bounded-in-`n` check (input for G3).** None found.
    - Sparse shapes: `n - |support|` conditions; totally real needs `|support| >= ceil(n/2) + 1`
      [Proof], and the primitives give `|support| <= 6` for `q = 1, 2` aligned with a P-coefficient
      graph, so totally real via power law x graph is possible only for `n <= 9` (`q = 2`, `d = 4`),
      or up to `n = 13` using a degree-6 rational graph (5 parameters only, so more conditions)
      [Heuristic].
    - Bézier x degree-`m` curve (`n = 3m`): rank <= 7 or 8, conditions `>= n - 8` grow linearly.
    - Offset of `y = x^k`: `D = 4k - 2` (k = 2, 3, 4 measured), <= 6 parameters, conditions `>= n - 6`.
    - Offset towers: see 9.

Ordered by (conditions, lowest condition degree, evidence of realness). "Cost" below means the cost

**Reading of the ranking [Heuristic].** The two new cond-2 engines (rows 2-3) have no proved
realness obstruction at s = 0, unlike the cube route whose lines were killed by the
three-equilateral-triangles proof. But their conditions have much higher degree than the odd route's
(3,5,7), and both are dense, so none of the line/Fano structure the odd route and `M^3 + v` use is
visible. Neither beats the odd route on the metrics measured here; they add independent codimension-2
targets whose points are not blocked by the A9 filter *per se* (no discrete structure on the roots is
forced) [Open: nobody has searched them for P-constructible points].

## Other things noticed

- The A <-> B identification is the census remark "even engine = odd engine under `tau -> 1/tau`"
  made precise: shapes are classified up to shift and Möbius `t -> 1/t`. Any future enumeration
  should quotient by that, or it double counts.
- `R = M^3 - y^2` (N4-1) is the `M^3 + w` family with `w` a square of a cubic; the census's
  `M^3 + w4` needs `w4` of degree 4 and one quartic condition, so N4-1 pays a second condition and a
  degree jump for a *drawable* curve (cuspidal, rational) instead of the missing genus-1 curve. This
  does not contradict Round 7: Neile is rational.
- Parity: a circle passes through the circular points, so circle x offset intersection counts are even;
  a nonic from a circle partner always needs a known point.
- Two stray numerical hints for stream G2: offset degree is `4k - 2` for `y = x^k`, and the
  second offset of a rational offset curve has the sheet structure of a hyperelliptic cover
  (`S2` is not a square), so depth-3 towers leave the rational world.
