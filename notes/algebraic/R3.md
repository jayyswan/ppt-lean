## 1. Setup and conventions

- `K = ℚ[x]/(q)`, basis `1, β, …, β^{n−1}`; `K` is totally real.
- For `τ ∈ K`, `W_τ = ⟨1, τ, τ²⟩_ℚ` and
  `V_τ = {ρ ∈ K : ρ² ∈ W_τ}`.
- A point `ρ ∈ V_τ` off `ℓ_τ = ℚ⟨1,τ⟩` gives a binary form
  `Q(s) = c s² + b s + a` by `ρ² = a + b τ + c τ²`.
  It is positive definite iff `c > 0` and `4ac − b² > 0`.
- `ℓ_τ` itself gives rank-1 forms: `(u + vτ)² = u² + 2uvτ + v²τ²`.
  A `ρ` with `c = 0` gives a linear form, not a binary quadratic; it is also unusable.
- Search method: enumerate primitive `ρ ∈ [−H,H]^n` up to sign, evaluate the `n−3`
  quotient quadratic forms exactly over ℤ, solve for `(a,b,c)` over ℚ, and classify
  `deg` (rank 1), `lin` (`c = 0`), `ind`, `nd`, or `pd`. All arithmetic is exact;
  NumPy is used only to evaluate the integer quadratic forms.
- Scripts: `notes/algebraic/scripts/R3_search.py` (field arithmetic and quotient forms),
  `R3_experiments.py` (box enumeration), `R3_scan.py` (τ-classes), `R3_readout.py`
  (Theorem R linear algebra), `R3_Lsearch.py` (real quadratic L). Counts are in
  `R3_counts.txt`, read-out output in `R3_readout.txt`.

## 5. The n = 6 K3: genus-1 fibrations and their fields of definition

**5.1 The net.** `V_τ ⊂ ℙ⁵` is the base locus of the net of diagonal quadrics
`q_w = ∑_{i=1}^6 w_i z_i²`, `w ∈ W_τ^⊥`, with `dim W_τ^⊥ = 3`. This is the n = 6 case of
the net in Z5 §0 and A1 §(b); here the net is 3-dimensional and the discriminant sextic
splits into six lines. Over `K` (or `L̄`) it is `∏_{i=1}^6 ℓ_i`, where `ℓ_i = {w_i = 0}`. A
generic point of `ℓ_i` is a rank-5 quadric; the 15 points `ℓ_i ∩ ℓ_j` are rank-4 quadrics.
`[Proof]`

**5.2 The fibrations.** Fix `i ≠ j`. The subspace of `W_τ^⊥` with `w_i = w_j = 0` is
1-dimensional, because `W_τ ∩ ⟨e_i,e_j⟩ = 0`; let `w^{ij}` be its generator. All four
remaining weights are nonzero (a nonzero quadratic cannot vanish at three of the `τ`'s), so
`q_{ij} = q_{w^{ij}}` has rank 4. Its zero locus is the cone with vertex
`L_{ij} = ⟨e_i,e_j⟩` over a smooth quadric surface `S_{ij}` in `ℙ³ = {z_i = z_j = 0}`.
The point `P = (1,…,1)` lies on `S_{ij}`, since `∑_{k≠i,j} w_k = ∑_k w_k = 0`
(`w ∈ W_τ^⊥` and `1 ∈ W_τ`). The two rulings of `S_{ij}` give two maps `V_τ → ℙ¹`: over a
line `λ` in a ruling, the fibre is `V_τ ∩ ⟨L_{ij}, λ⟩`; `q_{ij}` vanishes on this `ℙ³`, and
for generic `λ` the other two net quadrics cut a complete intersection of two quadrics, of
degree 4 and genus 1. Moreover `V_τ ∩ L_{ij} = ∅` (the functionals `w_i, w_j` are independent on
`W_τ^⊥`, so both coordinates would have to vanish), hence the map is defined. `[Proof]`

**5.3 Galois action and fields of definition.** `G = Gal(K^{gal}/ℚ) = S₆` (certified in
§2; base change to `L` does not change the action). The stabilizer of the unordered pair
`{i,j}` is `H_{ij} ≅ S₂ × S₄` (order 48, index 15). Galois acts by `σ(ℓ_i) = ℓ_{σ(i)}` and
`σ(q_{ij}) = q_{σ(i)σ(j)}`, so the stabilizer of `q_{ij}` is the stabilizer of the unordered
pair. It is exactly `H_{ij}` because `H_{ij}`
is maximal in `S₆` and `q_{ij}` is not ℚ-rational (a ℚ-rational point of `ℓ_i ∩ ℓ_j` would
lie on all six conjugate lines, which have no common point). Hence `q_{ij}` is defined over
`F_{ij} = (K^{gal})^{H_{ij}}`, a degree-15 field whose Galois closure has group `S₆`; it is
not solvable. Since `H_{ij}` is maximal, `F_{ij}` has no intermediate fields, and
`F_{ij} ∩ L = ℚ`, so the degree over `L` is also 15. For the rulings, use the point `P`:
the tangent plane is `∑ w_k z_k = 0`; putting `z_k = 1 + y_k` gives `∑ w_k y_k = 0` and
`q_{ij} = ∑ w_k y_k²`. The tangent form has radical spanned by `P`; on the complement
`∑ y_k = 0` the induced binary form has discriminant
`64 w₁w₂w₃w₄/(w₁+w₂+2w₃)²`, whose square class is
`δ_{ij} = ∏_{k≠i,j} w_k` (indices relabelled `1..4` on the complement).
`[Proof; discriminant Wolfram]`

The canonical square root of `δ_{ij}` is `s_{ij} = ∏_{k≠i,j} √w_k`. Under `σ ∈ H_{ij}`, the
cofactor vector transforms as `v ↦ sgn(σ) P_σ v`; the product of the four square roots
therefore transforms by `sgn(σ)² = 1`. Hence `s_{ij}` is fixed by `H_{ij}`, so
`δ_{ij} ∈ F_{ij}^{×2}` and both rulings are defined over `F_{ij}`. Thus `q_{ij}` and the two
dual genus-1 fibrations are all defined over the same degree-15 field `F_{ij}`; a single
fibration has stabilizer `H_{ij}` (not `S₂ × A₄`). The norm is a consistency check, not an
obstruction: Wolfram gives
`N_{F_{ij}/ℚ}(δ_{ij}) = 7^30 · 23^6 · 53^12 = disc(q6)^6 = (7^15 · 23^3 · 53^6)²`, a square.
`[Proof; norm Wolfram]`

**5.4 Consequence.** None of the 30 genus-1 fibrations obtained from the 15 rank-4 net
members is defined over `ℚ` or over the real solvable closure `ℚ^solv ∩ ℝ` (the `L` of the
solvable route). This confirms the review's guess: a single fibration is fixed by
`S₂ × S₄` and defined over the degree-15 field `F_{ij}`; there is no quadratic extension.
So the obvious K3 fibrations do not supply `L`-points for that route. This
is scoped to these 30 fibrations: it is not a classification of all elliptic fibrations on
the K3, it does not address a larger `L ⊂ P`, and it is not a negative about
`algebraic_Pconstructible`. If one had descended, its fibres would be genus-1 curves over
`L` and would have points of degree ≤ 4, hence solvable; the descent is the first obstruction,
and it fails here. `[Proof]`

## 8. Real quadratic and cubic L

### 8.1 Formulation

Let `L = ℚ(√d)`, `K_L = K ⊗ L = K + √d K`. Write `ρ = A + √d B` with `A,B ∈ K` and
`τ = τ₀ + √d τ₁`. Then

```
ρ² = (A² + d B²) + √d (2 A B),
W_τ = L⟨1, τ, τ²⟩.
```

For a fixed τ, `ρ² ∈ W_τ` is a system of linear conditions on `(A²+dB², 2AB)`; in the
enumeration we build the 6-dimensional ℚ-basis of `W_τ` and its 8-dimensional orthogonal
complement, then evaluate the corresponding quadratic forms on all `ρ` in the box. The
coefficients `a,b,c ∈ L` are recovered by an exact linear solve, and positivity is tested by
exact signs in `L` (not floating point).

### 8.2 H = 1 results

All runs use `H = 1` in all `2n` rational coordinates (n = 5: 10 coordinates, `3¹⁰ = 59,049`;
n = 6: 12, `3¹² = 531,441`; n = 7: 14, `3¹⁴ = 4,782,969` points per τ). "Genuine" means
`c ≠ 0` and `4ac − b² ≠ 0` as an element of `L`. `[Computed, exact]`

This box is close to vacuous: every coordinate lies in `{−1, 0, 1}`. It also finds nothing at
n = 5, where positive-definite forms are known to exist (§3), so it has no positive control

### 8.3 The higher-box frontier

For `τ ∉ K`, the quadratic case has 14 rational coordinates; H = 2 is `5¹⁴ ≈ 6.1 × 10⁹`
points per τ, and the cubic case has 21 coordinates, so even H = 1 is `3²¹ ≈ 1.0 × 10¹⁰`
points per τ. The present brute force is therefore exhausted at quadratic H = 1 and does not
reach cubic H = 1. A structured τ or a smarter parametrization of `V_τ(L)` would be needed to
go further. `[Open]`

## 9. Verdict

*Labels for this section: R2's strict bound is `[Proof]` modulo G2's catalogue (R2.md); the
n = 5 identities, read-out and fibration norm are `[Wolfram]`; the box searches are
`[Computed, exact]`; the verdict is `[Heuristic]`; the unsearched cases are `[Open]`.*

- **R2 input.** The strict linear minimum is `J_min(n) = n − 2`; at n = 7 this is `J = 5`.
  The conic/determinantal count gives `J = n − 6 = 1` at n = 7, but it is Type II (a
  determinantal condition, not free parameters), so it is not a theorem of the same shape.
- **R3 input.** The n = 5 control passes (all four Y1a forms, plus five new ones, exact
  read-out for J = 4 and for the cubic-Bézier J = 3). The n = 6 K3 warm-up and the n = 7
  search find no off-line ℚ-point of the stated heights. The quadratic L-search at H = 1 is
  close to vacuous and has no positive control. The fibrations from the 15 rank-4 net
  members do not descend to the real solvable `L` (§5).
- **Kill criterion.** **Not established by R3.** The R2/R3 criterion asks for R2's count
  plus no structured point at n = 7 with the n = 5 control passing. R2's half holds, but
  R3's half is a bounded-height ℚ-point statement on a general-type surface; Bombieri–Lang
  makes the null expected, and it says nothing about `V_τ(L)`. The route is **parked, not
  closed**: it needs `n − 2` simultaneous `L`-points on a general-type surface, with no
  group-law handle (R1), no way to lower the count (R2), and no computation that can see
  `L`-points (R3). `[Heuristic]`
- **Not closed by this stream.** R2's Type II conic/determinantal search at n = 7, J = 0/1
  ("the interesting search") is a different construction and is not tested here. The cubic
  Bézier's real crossing count and the higher L-search are also `[Open]`.

## Other things noticed

*Labels: form identities, discriminants and the fibration discriminant/norm are `[Wolfram]`;
the convention check, search statistics and the float-trap diagnosis are `[Computed, exact]`.*

- **Y1a form 3 typo.** Y1a §5 lists `(b₁₁,b₁₂,b₂₂) = (41/4, 27/4, 101/4)`. In the
  `(a,b,c)` convention this is `(101/4, −27/2, 41/4)`, not `−27`. The search found
  `41s² − 54s + 101`, which matches the corrected value and confirms the convention.
- **Five new n = 5 forms.** Besides Y1a's four, the search finds `s²−2s+5`, `s²+s+6`,
  `81s²−107s+42`, `25s²+49s+66`, `89s²−48s+92`. They are genuine positive-definite forms
  with `Q(β)` a square; they simply were not in Y1a's list. The n = 5 solution set is larger
  than the four used.
- **Growth of the n = 5 count.** The number of positive-definite classes grows
  `1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9` for H = 6..11. This is the expected rational-surface behavior and is a
  useful sanity check that the n = 6/n = 7 linear growth (line points only) is a real
  dimension drop, not a search artifact. `[Heuristic]`
- **The K3 did not warm up as hoped.** The plan's expectation that n = 6 elliptic fibrations
  give F-points is not realized in the ℚ box: the only ℚ-points of height ≤ 9 for τ = β (and
  ≤ 5 for the 48 sampled τ) are on the line, and the 30 fibrations from the rank-4 net
  members do not descend to the real solvable `L` (§5). Whether another fibration has rank 0
  or sections above the box is `[Open]`. This weakens the "n = 6 is a bridge" heuristic.
- **A floating-point trap in the L-search.** The first L-positivity test used `√d`
  numerically and reported several "positive definite" forms at n = 6, τ = β + √5. Their
  discriminants were exactly zero in `L` (e.g. `3220 − 1440√5 − (3220 − 1440√5)`), with the
  float cancelling to ~2 × 10⁻⁴. The exact `L`-sign test removed all of them. Any future
  L-search should use exact signs, not floats.
- **Discriminants.** q5 has prime discriminant 38569; q6 has `7⁵ · 23 · 53²`; q7 has
  `17 · 3457 · 15601 · 669577`. The q6 and q7 discriminants are not squares, consistent with
  the S₆/S₇ cycle-type certificates.
