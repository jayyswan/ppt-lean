# H5: cracking degree 10 (and 11), concretely

Stream H5 of `pptc/PLAN-algebraic.md`. Labels: [Proof], [Cited], [Wolfram], [Heuristic], [Open]. Every obstruction is
scoped to its route; nothing here concludes a negative about the target. No `.lean` edits, no commits.
Notation: K = Q[x]/(q) (or F0[x]/(q) for coefficient field F0), n = deg q, s = number of conjugate pairs,
p_k = Tr(tau^k), chi_tau = characteristic polynomial of tau in K, V' = {p_2 = p_3 = 0} in P(K/Q) = P^8 (centred tau).
Reading H3 first is assumed (`H3.md`): I reuse its facts (free recovery by Euclid, F-counts, cited thresholds) and do not redo them.
**Use of the degree-9 assumption (D9): none of the results below uses it.** (Tower steps have degree <= 6; recovery is a gcd.)

## Findings summary

1. **Towers close every decic except Galois group A10 or S10 [Proof; list of primitive groups Cited/memory, see 1].**
   Imprimitive K: a real quadratic or quintic subfield, then one step of degree 5 or 2. The 7 primitive groups other than A10, S10
   (A5, S5 on 10 points; PSL(2,9)=A6, PGL(2,9), S6, M10, PGammaL(2,9)) are reached by chains of subgroup indices (6,5,3,2) or
   (2,6,5,3,2) etc., **all steps of degree <= 6**, with the fixed fields real because complex conjugation lies in every subgroup of the chain.
   Group computations done in Wolfram on PGammaL(2,9) acting on P^1(F_9) (section 1). So the **whole open problem for degree 10 is Galois group
   A10 or S10**, and any route to them needs an engine step carrying A10 (Aₙ filter), which is not a tower.
2. **Rational decics with a nonreal root: the route is concrete, and it WORKS on the examples tested; one ingredient is unproved.**
   Engine `power_law 10` x rational sextic graph (`y^10 = G(y)`, G in Q[y], deg <= 6). It needs rational tau in K with
   p_1 = p_2 = p_3 = 0, i.e. a **rational point of V'_K = {Tr tau^2 = Tr tau^3 = 0} in P(K/Q)**.
   - V'_K is a **smooth** complete intersection of a quadric and a cubic in P^8: dimension 6, degree 6, canonical class O(-4) (Fano of index 4)
     [Proof, section 2].
   - Real points exist iff s >= 1 [Proof]; Meyer gives rational points on the quadric alone; the cubic is the missing ingredient.
   - **[Wolfram] explicit success on a Galois-S10 decic with s = 1**: q = x^10-10x^8+35x^6-50x^4+25x^2-x-3 has
     tau = -4+6x+38x^2-30x^3-57x^4+19x^5+24x^6-3x^7-3x^8 with
     chi_tau = y^10 - 6557 y^6 + 1501 y^5 + 172900 y^4 + 136838 y^3 - 710506 y^2 - 358949 y + 9839 (irreducible; y^9, y^8, y^7 coefficients 0).
     Found in 2 seconds by an LLL-reduced lattice box search (section 3). Further: s = 5 field (S10), s = 3 field: points at box height 3;
     H3's s = 4 example: none up to height 4 (section 3).
   - **Exact missing ingredient (MI-1):** for every decic K over Q with a nonreal embedding, V'_K(Q) != empty. Cited Hasse principles need
     >= 29 (BDH, secondary) / 37 (BHB) variables; we have 9. Heuristic H^4 growth, evidence above. It is a statement about each K, so it cannot be
     discharged in Lean by search; it needs a theorem (section 4 lists what would prove it).
3. **P-coefficient decics with >= 3 conjugate pairs: the THREE-PAIRS chain lifts only to the quintic engine `y^10 = quintic(y)`
   (3 power sums), not to the drawable quartic engine (4 power sums)** [Proof of the count, Heuristic for the realness of the lifted chain].
   Aux degrees (2,3,5,sqrt,6,4) as for the nonic. The drawable quartic needs p_2..p_5 = 0 on a P^8 variety of degree 120 and general type
   (K = O(5)), with Descartes threshold s >= 2 [Proof]. Quintic with arbitrary P coefficients is not drawable (one rational invariant J, G2).
   The exact missing ingredient is a **P-point on V4' = {p_2 = p_3 = p_4 = p_5 = 0}**, or on {p_2 = p_3 = p_4 = 0} with J(G_tau) in Q
   (section 5).
4. **Totally real decics: no low-degree route.** p_2 > 0 kills every `y^m = sextic` shape [Proof]. All 89 sparse degree-10 supports with gaps <= 2:
   the only 6-term one is the even polynomial {10,8,6,4,2,0}; 15 seven-term, 35 eight-term supports [Wolfram]. Rational-sextic overshoot odd engine:
   3 conditions of Porteous degree 32340 [Wolfram]. Square lift: realness is repaired, cost goes up (H3). Net: **no known low-degree totally real decic
   route; scoped to the engines listed** (section 6).
5. **Degree 11** (section 7): all transitive groups of prime degree need an engine step of degree 11 [Proof]; the rational sextic engine needs
   p_2 = p_3 = p_4 = 0 in P^9 (no theorem applies; smoothness of this CI is not automatic).

## 1. Towers: which degree-10 groups are already covered

**Setting.** q irreducible of degree 10 over a field F0 contained in P and real, alpha a real root, Omega the splitting field inside C, G = Gal(Omega/F0)
acting on the 10 roots, c = complex conjugation restricted to Omega (an element of G of order <= 2), H = Stab(alpha), so c in H.

**Lemma T [Proof].** If there is a chain G = G_0 > G_1 > ... > G_k with c in G_k <= H and every index [G_i : G_{i+1}] <= 8 (or <= 9 using D9), then alpha is
P-constructible. Proof: L_i = Omega^{G_i} is contained in R because c fixes it; L_{i+1}/L_i is generated by a primitive element whose minimal polynomial over L_i
has degree [G_i : G_{i+1}] and a real root, with coefficients in L_i (induction). alpha lies in L_k because G_k <= H. QED.

**Imprimitive K [Proof].** A block system gives a subfield F of K, real, of degree 2 or 5; alpha is a root of a degree-5 or degree-2 polynomial over F.

**Primitive groups of degree 10.** There are 9: A5, S5 (acting on the 10 pairs of 5 points), PSL(2,9) = A6, PGL(2,9), S6 = PSigmaL(2,9), M10, PGammaL(2,9),
A10, S10 [Cited from memory: OEIS A000019 has value 9 at n = 10; the page returned HTTP 403 so I could not re-read it; Dixmier-Mortimer tables unverified].

**Computation [Wolfram].** I built PSL(2,9), PGL(2,9), S6, M10 and PGammaL(2,9) as permutation groups on P^1(F_9) (orders 360, 720, 720, 720, 1440), and
A5, S5 on pairs (orders 60, 120). For involutions c with a fixed point (necessary because c fixes alpha):
- in PGL(2,9) and M10 all 45 such involutions lie in PSL(2,9) (outer involutions of PGL are fixed-point-free; M10 \ A6 has none);
- in S6 and PGammaL(2,9) there are 45 + 30 such involutions; the 30 extra (4 fixed points) are in S6 \ A6.
- Chains containing c, found by search: A6: orders 360 > 60 > 12 > 4 > 2 (indices 6, 5, 3, 2); S6 with c outer: 720 > 120 > 24 > 8 > 2 (6, 5, 3, 4);
  A5 on 10 points: 60 > 12 > 4 > 2; S5 with c a transposition: 120 > 24 > 8 > 2. For PGL, M10 (c in A6): first step to A6 (index 2), then the A6 chain.
  PGammaL(2,9): step to A6 (index 4) or S6 (index 2), then the chain above. c = 1 (totally real): any chain, e.g. A6 chain.
  G_k = <c> <= H. All indices <= 6.

**Corollary T10 [Proof modulo the list of primitive groups].** Every irreducible decic with coefficients in a real subfield of P whose Galois group is not A10 or S10 has all
its real roots P-constructible by steps of degree <= 6 (no D9).

**Why A10, S10 are different [Proof].** Subgroups of S10 of index < 10 are S10 and A10; A10 has no proper subgroup of index <= 9; so the chain stops at A10,
which does not lie in H. This is the Aₙ filter of NOTES-general at n = 10; the engine must carry A10.

## 2. Rational decics with a nonreal root: the engine and its variety

**Engine E10 [Proof of algebra; Lean engine not written].** tau in K rational (coordinates in Q), p_1 = p_2 = p_3 = 0 (after the rational shift tau - Tr(tau)/10 which
`translate_x` implements). Newton gives chi_tau(y) = y^10 - G(y), G in Q[y] of degree <= 6, so y0 = tau(beta) is an isolated crossing of `power_law` y = x^10 with
`poly_graph G` (negative y by reflection). Recovery of beta: gcd(q(x), tau(x) - y0) over Q(y0) is linear (H3 finding 1), no D9; if deg tau <= 8 the octic theorem
also applies. Descartes capacity of the support {10,6,5,...,0} is 8 [Wolfram], so s >= 1 is needed, exactly the p_2 requirement.

**Smoothness [Proof].** Write the conditions in P(K) = P^9 as p_1 = p_2 = p_3 = 0 (equivalent to V' after centring). Over C, K tensor C = C^10 and tau = (t_1..t_10).
The differentials of p_1, p_2, p_3 are rows (1), (t_i), (t_i^2) times (1, 2, 3), dependent iff the t_i take at most 2 distinct values. If they take values a (mult m) and b
(mult 10-m) with p_1 = p_2 = 0 then a = -(10-m) b/m and p_2 = 10(10-m) b^2/m, so b = 0 = a. Hence there are no singular points; V' is a smooth CI of type (2,3) in
P^8, dim 6, degree 6, K_{V'} = O(-9+2+3) = O(-4). (Contrast the nonic: V = {p_2 = 0} is a quadric.)

**Real points.** The real cone {p_2 = 0} on trace-zero space is nonempty iff s >= 1 [Proof: signature (r1+s-1, s)]. Real points of V' itself (cubic also zero) are [Heuristic/sketch]: p_3 is odd, so it vanishes on the cone wherever x and -x lie in the same connected piece; confirmed numerically by the explicit rational points of section 3 (s = 1, 3, 5).

**Local points [Proof for good primes, Open otherwise].** At a prime p where the reduction of V' is smooth (p not dividing disc(q) and p > 3 so that traces are separable), Chevalley-Warning
(degrees 2 + 3 = 5 < 9 variables) gives a nontrivial F_p-point; it is a smooth point of a smooth variety, so Hensel lifts it to Z_p. At the finitely many bad primes solubility is [Open] in general
(not checked); the examples below show it holds there in practice.

**What the cited theorems give.** Variable counts, with N = 9 trace-zero variables:
- Heath-Brown, *Cubic forms in ten variables* (Proc. LMS 47, 1983): nonsingular cubic, >= 10 variables [Cited]. Hooley (nonary cubic forms): nonsingular, 9 variables, needs local solubility [Cited, secondary, hypotheses not read].
  Heath-Brown, *Cubic forms in 14 variables*: any cubic form [Cited, secondary].
- A rational isotropic subspace of {p_2 = 0} has dimension <= floor(9/2) = 4 over Q, and <= s over R. The cubic restricted to it has <= 4 variables [Proof]. Even a maximal one is far below 10.
- Browning-Dietmann-Heath-Brown (JIMJ 2015, arXiv:1309.0147): Hasse principle for a smooth quadric-cubic pair, nonsingular case, in > 29 variables [secondary, via search summary;
  unverified]. Browning-Heath-Brown Cor 1.8: 37 [H3, Cited]. **None applies with 9 variables.**

## 3. Experiments on concrete S10 decics [Wolfram throughout]

**Method.** For q monic in Z[x]: roots to 80 digits, power sums s_k = Tr(alpha^k) exactly. The sublattice L0 = {tau in Z[alpha] : Tr tau = 0} (rank 9) is computed with `LatticeReduce`,
then LLL-reduced for the Minkowski form sum |sigma(x)|^2 (real embeddings, and sqrt2 Re, sqrt2 Im for each pair; **the weight of the transformation block must be small compared to the
embedding block, otherwise the basis is wildly unreduced; my first attempt gave a basis vector of norm 7225 and the second 34**). Exact integer tensors T2[i,j] = Tr(b_i b_j), T3[i,j,k] = Tr(b_i b_j b_k) via
Tr(f) = sum f_j s_j. Enumerate c in [-m, m]^9 by looping over 4 coordinates and vectorising the other 5; test p_2 = 0 first, then p_3 on the few survivors. p_1 = 0 is automatic.

**Fields and results.**

(Pair counts are the numbers of conjugate pairs.) Run time for m = 3 was about 2 s per field: the p_2 = 0 survivors are sparse, which is why box searches in the raw power basis (H3) saw almost none.

**The s = 1 example in full.** q = x^10 - 10x^8 + 35x^6 - 50x^4 + 25x^2 - x - 3. tau(x) = -4 + 6x + 38x^2 - 30x^3 - 57x^4 + 19x^5 + 24x^6 - 3x^7 - 3x^8 (degree 8 in x).
Resultant_x(q, y - tau) = y^10 - 6557 y^6 + 1501 y^5 + 172900 y^4 + 136838 y^3 - 710506 y^2 - 358949 y + 9839, irreducible. Eight real roots
-8.2243, -3.5609, -3.0802, -0.5138, 0.02607, 2.0206, 5.7916, 7.5398, and these are exactly tau at the 8 real roots of q (printed to 60 digits). So the engine `y^10 = 6557 y^6 - 1501 y^5 - ...`
produces each y0, and beta follows from the octic theorem applied to tau(x) - y0 (degree 8), or from a gcd. **This is a complete worked instance of the route, with auxiliary degrees: none.**
The lattice step is a search, not a construction a Lean proof can use.

**Reading [Heuristic].** Hits appear at height 3 in a reduced basis in 3 of 5 fields; the miss at s = 4 had much larger basis norms (lattice determinant larger), consistent with the counting heuristic
N(H) ~ c H^{9-2-3}/(coefficient scale). This is evidence for MI-1, not a proof.

## 4. What a proof of MI-1 would need

MI-1: for K a decic field over Q with s >= 1, V'_K(Q) != empty.
- Local solubility everywhere (real: done; good primes: Chevalley-Warning; bad primes open) **and** a Hasse principle for smooth Fano CIs of type (2,3) in P^8. Known theorems need 30+ variables.
  Possible leverage: V' contains a 7-dimensional family of lines (Fano scheme of lines: dim G(2,9) - 2*... = 14 - 7 = 7) and is probably unirational once it has one rational point; the single point is the issue.
- Isotropic-subspace reduction: s >= 3 gives a rational isotropic 3-space L (peel hyperbolic planes by Meyer on dims 9, 7, 5 [Proof]); p_3|_L is a plane cubic and the parameter space of L is a rational 12-fold, so one needs a rational L whose plane cubic has a rational point
  (the same problem again, cubic curves instead of V').
- A local-to-global argument special to trace forms (Serre, Bayer-Fluckiger-Parimala) for the discriminant side only [Open].
- Replacing the sextic by a drawable family with F >= 10: the parabola x sextic family F = 9 (H3) leaves 2 conditions at n = 10 of unknown degree; the graph x transposed graph F = 13 is unconditional over C but
  nonlinear (H3). Both are [Open] for rational points; neither was evaluated here.

## 5. P-coefficient decics: THREE-PAIRS lifted to dimension 9

**Engines.** `y^10 = quartic`: conditions p_2, p_3, p_4, p_5 (shift handles p_1). Support {10,4,3,2,1,0} has Descartes capacity 6, so s >= 2 [Wolfram]. `y^10 = quintic` (P-coefficient quintic is not drawable beyond its one rational invariant
J = A^3/B^2 of G2): conditions p_2, p_3, p_4 and J in Q, capacity 6, s >= 2 again.

**Chain of auxiliary degrees, lifted from THREE-PAIRS [Heuristic: not re-run at dimension 9, realness certificate not done].**
1. P0 with p_1 = p_2 = p_3 = 0: cone point (degree 2), definite 2-planes from conjugate pairs, a totally isotropic plane, binary cubic (degree 3). In dimension 9 there is more room; needs s >= 3 for the same structure.
2. Lines through P0 in {p_1 = p_2 = p_3 = 0}: x satisfies two linear conditions and Q, C_2, C_3. In dimension 9 the space W is 6-dimensional, so the direction locus is a **surface** S' of degree 12 in P^5 (it was a curve in P^4 for the nonic);
   take a rational hyperplane section to reduce to the nonic's curve; conics via the pencil det(Q + lambda C_2) of degree 6 on the 6-dimensional W, square roots, then a sextic.
3. p_4 along the line: a quartic. Result: tau with p_1 = ... = p_4 = 0 (the quintic engine), all auxiliary degrees <= 6.
4. **p_5 (or J in Q) is not reached.** The line has one parameter, used by p_4. The varieties: V4' = {p_2..p_5 = 0} in P^8: dimension 4, degree 120, K = O(5) [Proof of the numbers]. Lines in V3' = {p_2 = p_3 = p_4 = 0} (dim 5) form a 2-dimensional family
   sweeping a 3-fold; a generic point of V3' has no line through it (nine equations of degrees 1,2,1,2,3,1,2,3,4 on P^4's worth of directions: 6 conditions on 4 unknown dimensions after the linear ones) [Proof by count]. Planes in V2' through P0 would be lines of S', which a general S' does not contain.
   Using a 2-parameter family (P0 free in V2', u in S') the condition "p_4 and p_5 have a common root on the line" is one resultant equation on a 7-fold with no visible structure [Heuristic].
**Exact missing ingredient (MI-2):** a P-point of V4', or of V3' with J(G_tau) in Q, using auxiliary equations of degree <= 9 with real roots. Cheaper alternatives (H3 families, offsets) have rational structure only.
The Hilbert-type 27-lines step has Galois group W(E6) containing PSp4(3) of order 25920 which is not a section of S9 [Heuristic: from memory of subgroup lists], so it is not available even with D9.

## 6. Totally real decics

- Realness [Proof]: p_2 > 0 for every real tau (also over any real field of definition), so all `y^m = sextic` shapes fail, and Hilbert's isotropic plane has dimension 0.
- Sparse supports [Wolfram]: 89 supports of degree 10 containing 10 and 0 have all gaps <= 2 (needed for ten real roots by the gap theorem of N4). By size: 6 terms: 1 (the even polynomial {10,8,6,4,2,0}, which forces roots +-); 7: 15; 8: 35; 9: 28; 10: 9; 11: 1.
  The only 8-term supports with a rational-sextic origin are the odd/even/m-odd types of N2 (capacity 12-13).
- Odd engine, n = 10: N = t P6(t^2) - a, degree 13, cofactor cubic. Condition: 1, tau, tau^3, ..., tau^13 are Q-dependent (8 vectors in 10 dimensions); codimension 3 in P^8, Porteous degree h_3(0,1,3,5,...,13) = **32340** [Wolfram; the same code reproduces N2's 1428 for h_2].
  Variant P6(t^2) - c t (columns 0,1,2,4,...,12): degree 22275. Real points exist (N = T_13 - 1/3, N2). No rational point is known; no structure like Meyer applies (odd conditions).
- Square lift g(y) = f(y^2 + c) [H3]: realness fixed (2 real roots, signature (11,9) at n = 10), p_1 = p_2 = 0 solvable by Meyer, but a sparse engine at degree 20 needs 13 conditions. Using instead tau in K only (the subfield) returns to the totally real problem [Proof: the Meyer vector for the lift has coordinates (a, b), and the engine needs a statement about a degree-20 polynomial].
- **Statement:** the single missing ingredient for totally real decics is a P- or Q-point of a codimension-3 determinantal locus of degree 32340 (odd engine), or any drawable family with F >= 11 and linear cofactor. No lower-degree route found [scoped].

## 7. Degree 11

- **Towers cannot work for any transitive group of prime degree [Proof].** G_k <= H = Stab(alpha) gives [G : G_k] divisible by [G : H] = 11, so some step has index >= 11 > 10. Primitive groups of degree 11: C11, D11, 11:5, 11:10, PSL(2,11), M11, A11, S11 [Cited from memory: 8, consistent with A000019]. The solvable ones are reached by real radicals/trigonometry (power laws `x^11 = a`, `arccos`), not by this mechanism [Heuristic, not worked].
- Rational sextic engine: p_2 = p_3 = p_4 = 0 in P^9 (CI of type (2,3,4), K = O(-1), Fano of index 1); smoothness is not automatic: 3 distinct values with multiplicities summing to 11 can solve p_1..p_4 = 0 for special multiplicities [not checked]. Meyer handles the quadric; no theorem handles the rest.
  Real points need s >= 1. Not searched numerically (time).

## Other things noticed

- H3's box search used the raw power basis (eccentric form). A Minkowski-LLL basis of the trace-zero lattice finds p_2 = 0 vectors by the hundreds and p_2 = p_3 = 0 vectors at height 3. Worth reusing for the nonic (`Nonic`) analogues and for n = 11.
- A decic root with an octic Tschirnhaus polynomial is covered by the octic theorem directly; the free-recovery gcd lemma (H3) removes the need for composition structure.
- One root suffices: with D9, once one real root beta of an irreducible decic is P-constructible the other roots are roots of the nonic q(x)/(x - beta) over P(beta). So A10, S10 reduce to the construction of a single real root, which does not help the engine problem but halves the bookkeeping.
- Descartes capacity bookkeeping (sum of 1 for odd gap, 2 for even gap) is a clean one-liner (`cap`) in Wolfram and matches N2's table; for `y^10 = quartic` and `quintic` it equals 6 (s >= 2), for sextic and septic 8 (s >= 1).
- The two-curve crossing of a rational sextic graph with a rational-affine parabola (F = 9, H3) is the natural next test: 2 conditions at n = 10, and Meyer would handle one if one of them is quadratic. The structure of those two conditions was not computed.
- Unverified: OEIS A000019 value 9 (page 403); BDH threshold 29; Hooley's hypotheses; list of primitive groups of degree 10 (taken from memory; the chain computations only used the groups themselves).
- The sign of Newton conventions: chi_tau has y^9, y^8, y^7 coefficients zero in the printed example, so the engine shape is y^10 = 6557 y^6 - 1501 y^5 - 172900 y^4 - 136838 y^3 + 710506 y^2 + 358949 y - 9839.
