# D1: cubic Bézier against a rational sextic graph

Stream D1 of `PLAN-degree9.md`, 2026-11-13, run after E (`D1E.md`). Research only. Backend: Wolfram
(wolfram MCP). Labels as in D0. Every negative is scoped to this route. Log: `HANDOFF-alg-D1.md`.

**Verdict.**
- **Over ℚ: dominated by E.** It is the same arithmetic problem (a ℚ-point on a twisted
  hypersurface), with degree 30 instead of 10.
- **Over F: a new single-condition F-row.** With `g` fixed rational and `X`, `Y` P-valued,
  `c_τ = 1`, with real capacity 18. It has the same shape as M1/G/J. Its degree was not measured.

## 0. Set-up

The Bézier `(X(t), Y(t))`, with `X, Y` cubic, meets `poly_graph g` (`g ∈ ℚ[x]`, degree ≤ 6). The
crossing polynomial is `N = Y(t) − g(X(t))`, of degree 18. A generator `τ ∈ K` works iff
`χ_τ | N`. The `t`-affine reparametrisation normalises `X = t³ + bt` (shift kills `t²`), and
scaling `t` moves `b` by squares.

## 1. Over ℚ (all dials rational) [Computed, exact + Proof]

- **Degree.** For fixed `X` the condition on `τ` is
  `det[Λ_j(τᵢ)] / Vandermonde = 0`, with
  `Λ = (1, t, t², t³, X², …, X⁶)`. That is the column degree sum `66` minus `36`, so **30**.
  - On `q₃`, along `τ = β + sβ²`, the 66-degree determinant is exactly the Vandermonde
    (sum-resolvent) factor of degree 36 times an **irreducible** factor of degree 30.
  - This holds for `X = t³` and for `X = t³ + t`.
- **With `b` free.** In the weighted space `ℙ(1⁹, 2)` the locus is a hypersurface of weighted
  degree 30, with `K = O(30 − 11) = O(19)`. E is a hypersurface of degree 10 in `ℙ⁸`, with
  `K = O(1)`.
- **Fibre over a fixed generator.** It is D0's plane curve, of total degree 20 in `(a, b)`, which
  has high genus. Over ℚ the extra dial gives no advantage.
- **Comparison with E.** D1 needs the same kind of point (rational `τ`, rational `g`) on a far more
  general-type variety. Every reason E is hard applies to D1, more strongly. **Dominated** [Proof
  of the degree count; dominance is heuristic in the Bombieri–Lang sense].
- **Power sums.** For `X = t³` the analogue of D1E §1 holds: the condition depends only on `p_k`
  with `3 ∤ k`, by the `ω`-twist of `χ(t)` [Proof sketch]. But the condition has degree 30 > 9,
  so `p_k` for `k > 9` re-enter through `p₁ … p₉`, and nothing is gained. E's reduction works
  only because its degree, 10, involves `p_k` with `k ≤ 7`.

## 2. Over F: `g` fixed rational, everything else P-valued [Computed, numeric rank]

- **Dials.**
  - `Y ∈ F[t]₃` (4, linear) and the scale `c` of `g` (1, linear).
  - `X ∈ F[t]₃` (4, nonlinear). `translate_x`/`scale_x` of the graph are absorbed into `X`.
  - `g` is chosen in advance, rational.
- **Drawability.** As in D1E §7: P control points, the rational `poly_graph`, `restrict` +
  `inter_x` to read `X(τᵢ)`. Then `τᵢ` is a real root of the cubic `X(t) − X(τᵢ)` over P, and
  `β ∈ F[τ]`.
- **Rank** (D0's root-Jacobian census, 100 digits, `g = x⁶ − 3x⁵ + 2x⁴ + 5x³ − x² − 4x + 1`, two
  seeds): `r = 8`, so **`c_τ = 1`**. The solution set is 8-dimensional in `(τ, X, Y, c)`, a
  reparametrisation-saturated hypersurface in `τ`-space, with finite fibres.
- **Real capacity 18, attained.** With `g = T₆` (rational) and `X = T₃`, `g(X) = T₁₈`, and
  `T₁₈ − Y` has 18 real roots for small `Y`. There is no realness obstruction for totally real K.
- **Geometric form** [Proof sketch]. Put `ξ = X(τ) ∈ K ⊗ F`. A solution is a set of 9 points
  `(ξᵢ, g(ξᵢ))` on the graph that lie on a plane cubic admitting a polynomial parametrisation (a
  cubic with one place at infinity). That is 3 conditions on `ξ`. They become 1 on `τ`, because
  `ξ` carries 4 dials.
- **Place in the census.**
  - It is a single-condition F-row, like M1 (Bézier × Bézier, degree about 111), G (Bézier ×
    P-quartic, about 180) and J.
  - With `g` quartic it is a sub-case of G (rational `Q`). With `g` sextic it is not: no P-sextic
    graph is drawable, and the row has real capacity 18 against G's 12.
  - New feature: a 7-parameter **rational** family of F-hypersurfaces (one per `g`). A special
    `g` could make the hypersurface reducible or of low degree.
- **Not measured.** The degree of this hypersurface: it needs a 5 × 5 polynomial system in
  `(s, X)`, beyond a 60-second MCP call. Lemma A would need degree < 9. Given M1/G, that is not
  expected [Heuristic].

## 3. Recommendation

- D1 over ℚ is closed as dominated by E. No search on `q₃` was run, deliberately: by §1 it would
  be a weaker version of D1E §4.
- D1 over F joins M1/G/J as a single-condition F-row. The one new handle is the choice of rational
  `g`. A stream on that should measure the hypersurface degree for 2–3 special `g` (Chebyshev
  `T₆`, `x⁶`-like, products of linear factors) by homotopy continuation outside the MCP. Only if
  some `g` gives degree < 9, or a reducible locus, is it worth more.
- **H** (two rational sextics, one rotated) is next in the plan's order.
