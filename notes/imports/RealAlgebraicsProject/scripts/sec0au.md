Topic: priority 1, scope (i): the [9,1] 3-field test of RHJ_10 (S69.4, Next (1)). **Mechanisms named:** (a) Brassil–Reichstein's fixed-point method (Kollár–Szabó going up, Reichstein–Youssin going down) as the complete source of 3-field obstructions; (b) a direct valuation analysis of y = (α + βπ + γπ², c) on K9 = L(π), π³ = ut, over the prime-to-3 closure of k((t)). **Test that could fail:** whether some 3-field F′ and some E′ = K9 × F′ have X-generators but no F′-point of V. **Result: the test fails, so RHJ_10 is false.** The fixed-point test passes for every abelian 3-subgroup of S_10 (S70.1), so BR's method sees nothing, but an explicit valued 3-field has X_{E′}(F′) ∋ smooth generators and V_{E′}(F′) = ∅ (S70.2). **S70.3 (consequences).** *unchecked* (short).
- **RHJ_10 is false**, even over prime-to-3 extensions of F = ℚ(B): a solution a = Σ c_j(e)θ^j (or a 0-cycle of degree prime to 3 of such) specialises at a θ′ ∈ X_{E′}(F′) chosen in the dense ball of S70.2 away from the denominators and the zero locus of a, giving a nonzero point (or a 0-cycle of degree prime to 3, hence a point) of V_{E′}(F′). So **no field-independent transfer X_A(ℚ) → V_A(ℚ) exists** (rational, multi-valued of degree prime to 3, or via any construction valid over all fields of characteristic 0), and the S68.3 reduction of scope (i) to RHJ_10 + X-local conditions is void.

Over ℚ these are arithmetic (Selmer-type) objects; a nonsingular cubic form in ≥ 10 variables can be anisotropic over 3-fields (BR, S66.1) and always has a ℚ-zero (Heath-Brown), so it remains a legitimate input.
- Nothing changes for the conditional route (S65.4: Colliot-Thélène's conjecture plus local solubility), for the local results (no local analogue: over ℚ_p the residue cubics D_u are isotropic by Chevalley–Warning) or for scope (ii).
