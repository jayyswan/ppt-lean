**S69.1 (multiplicity lemma over 3-fields).** *unchecked* (short, complete) + [numerical] (`mult69.py`).
- Let F′ be a 3-field of characteristic 0 (every finite extension has 3-power degree; so μ3 ⊂ F′ and every element is a square), E′ an étale F′-algebra of degree 10, and Q ∈ V_{E′}(F′) a point of multiplicity m with 3 ∤ m. **Then V_{E′}(F′) contains smooth generators of E′.**
- Proof: in P⁸ = P{Tr = 0} take a generic F′-plane Π through Q. Π ∩ V is finite of degree 15; Q has intersection multiplicity exactly m (the directions of Π miss the projectivised tangent cone, of dimension 5 in the P⁷ of directions); away from Q, Π misses Sing V (7875 lines; the cone over them from Q has dimension ≤ 2) and the non-generator locus N = V ∩ ⋃{y_i = y_j} (dimension 5; cone of dimension ≤ 6, codimension 2 among directions). The residual scheme has degree 15 − m ≢ 0 mod 3 and is defined over F′; its closed points have residue degrees 3^a, so one is F′-rational, a smooth generator. The conditions are open and F′ is infinite.
- The same holds over any field F′ in which every 0-dimensional scheme of degree prime to 3 has a rational point.

**S69.2 ([3,3,3,1] passes; two factors of equal degree d ∈ {2, 3, 4}).** *unchecked* (short) + [numerical] (`mult69.py`).

**S69.3 (audit of S68.4: the type [9,1]).** *unchecked* (short).
- S68.4 listed [9,1] with the remark "X can fail" (S66.1), but the test asks for V-generators whenever X_{E′}(F′) has generators, and that case was not treated. Let E′ = K9 × F′ with K9 a field over a 3-field F′. The Galois closure is a 3-group, so K9 ⊃ M ⊃ F′ with M = F′(t^{1/3}) and K9 = M(ρ), ρ³ = m ∈ M (Kummer, μ3 ⊂ F′).
- (a) V_{E′} has no F′-rational singular point (a Galois-stable two-valued pattern is constant on K9, and constants give only 0), so S69.1 gives nothing beyond a smooth point.
- (b) For β ∈ M, y = (βρ, 0) has Tr y = Tr y⁵ = 0 and Tr y³ = 3Tr_{M/F′}(mβ³): **V contains the plane cubic curve {Tr_{M/F′}(mβ³) = 0} ⊂ P(Mρ)** (and its analogue in P(Mρ²)), whose points are smooth generators. So whenever X has the point (ρ, 0) (i.e. Tr_M m = 0), V has it too; and a cyclic Kummer K9 = F′(σ), σ⁹ ∈ F′, gives (σ, 0) ∈ V (S64.4(a), e = 9).
- (c) From a smooth F′-point θ of X the tangent-line parametrisation y(v) = Tr(v³)θ − 3Tr(θv²)v pulls Tr y⁵ back to a degree-15 form G on P⁶ vanishing to order 5 along B = {Tr(θv²) = Tr v³ = 0} (the lines of X through θ). An F′-curve meeting B in a 0-cycle of degree prime to 3 would give an F′-point of V, but B can have index 3 (plane sections: 6 points, orbits 3 + 3), so nothing is forced.
- **So the single open p-field test of RHJ_10 is now [9,1] with X_{E′}(F′) ∋ generators** (it replaces [3,3,3,1]). A counterexample there kills every field-independent transfer; a proof leaves RHJ_10 with no p-field obstruction.
