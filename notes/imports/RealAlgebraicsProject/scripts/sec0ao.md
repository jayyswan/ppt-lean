## 0ao. Session 64 (Nov 20): results

Topic: priority 1, scope (i) (rational nonics): V_K = {Tr u³ = (Tr u)³, Tr u⁵ = (Tr u)⁵} ⊂ P(K). **Mechanism named: the Hasse principle for V_K** (S63.6). This session tests its necessary half **uniformly in K**, as S63.6 item (2) asked. **Test that could fail:** a single local algebra K_v (a Frobenius type at an unramified p, or a ramified étale ℚ_p-algebra) with V_K(ℚ_v) = ∅ would kill the route for every nonic field with that completion, a set of positive density. **Result:** no obstruction found. Every real place and every unramified prime is covered for every K (S64.1, S64.2, a proof). Most ramified local algebras are covered by explicit constructions (S64.3, S64.4), and the cases that failed a random p-adic search were search failures, not obstructions (S64.5). So local solubility of V_K reduces, for each K, to the ramified primes that the lemmas don't cover, and the scope (i) gap is the global step.

Notation: y = (u_1, …, u_9, ζ), ζ = −Tr u; V_K ≅ the twist of V̄ = {p1 = p3 = p5 = 0} ⊂ P⁹. In characteristic 0 or p ≥ 7, **a point is singular iff the values y_i² take at most 2 distinct values** (rows 1, y_i², y_i⁴ of the Jacobian); the singular points are pairing configurations. Pair number m(K_v): the largest m such that the Galois set of K_v has a stable subset of size 2m with a free equivariant involution (two isomorphic factors swapped, or a factor with an index-2 subfield; each even unramified factor counts, since unramified fields are cyclic).

**S64.1 (involution lemma).** [proof, short] *unchecked*. If m(K_v) ≥ 2, write K_v = A × B with σ an automorphism of A of order 2 acting freely, deg A = 2m. Take u = (a, 0) with σ(a) = −a and a² not a scalar in A^σ (possible since dim A^σ = m ≥ 2). The conjugates of u come in pairs ±, so ζ = 0 and every odd power sum vanishes: u ∈ V_K. The squares take the values of a² (at least 2 distinct, nonzero) and 0, so u is smooth. **Consequences:** every real place of every K (K ⊗ ℝ = ℝ^r × ℂ^s has m ≥ 4; a ℂ factor is a pair), every ramified algebra with such a structure, and **26 of the 30 unramified Frobenius types** at every p. The types with m ≤ 1 are **(9), (7,2), (7,1,1), (5,3,1)** (`sing64.py`, enumeration of the partitions of 9).

**S64.2 (all unramified primes, every K).** [proof: Chevalley–Warning + exact counts, *unchecked* write-up] + [exact mod p] (`sing64.py`) + Hensel certificates (`hens64.py`) + [numerical] (`plane64.py`).
- For p unramified in K, F3 and F5 reduce to forms over F_p in 9 variables (the twist of V̄ by the Frobenius type), and 3 + 5 < 9, so **#V(F_p) ≡ 1 mod p** (Chevalley–Warning: the affine zero count is ≡ 0 mod p). Hence **if #Sing(F_p) ≢ 1 mod p, a smooth F_p-point exists** and lifts by Hensel.
- For the four types with m ≤ 1, every F_p-point of Sing lies in the family where each odd Frobenius orbit carries a constant value (a non-constant odd orbit has ≥ 3 distinct squares) and a 2-orbit carries (d, d^p). Exact counts (`sing64.py 7 11 13 17`): **#Sing(F_p) = 0, 1, 3, 2** for (9), (7,2), (7,1,1), (5,3,1) when p ≥ 11. The points are: none; (0, d) with d ∈ F_{p²} of trace 0; (0, a, −a), (0, a, 0), (0, 0, b); (0, 0, a) and (c, −c, −c). At p = 7 the counts are 0, 2, 10, 5 (seven equal values vanish in characteristic 7). So (9), (7,1,1), (5,3,1) are done for every p ≥ 7, and (7,2) for p = 7.
- **(7,2), p ≥ 11:** S64.3(a) with A = U7. The cubic c(w) = Tr_{U7}(w³) − (Tr w)³ in 7 variables has an F_p-zero (Chevalley–Warning, 7 > 3). It has no singular F_p-zero: a singular zero has all eight values z_i² equal (z = (w, −Tr w)), and a 7-cycle cannot carry the pattern ±a (constant gives 48c² = 0, alternating signs is impossible on an odd cycle). Hensel then gives a smooth ℚ_p-zero, and S64.3(a) gives smooth ℚ_p-points of V_K.
- **p = 2, 3, 5, 7:** explicit Hensel certificates (a 2 × 2 minor of valuation m with v(F3), v(F5) ≥ 2m + 1) for all four types (`hens64.py <type> <p> 30000 12 1`). At p = 2 and 7: m = 0, v ≥ 1, after 1–119 samples. At p = 3 and 5: m = 1, v ≥ 3, after 32–10717 samples.
- Confirmation: smooth F_p-points by plane sections for **every prime 7 ≤ p ≤ 10⁵ and all four types** (`plane64.py <type> 7 100000`; 9589 primes each, at most 12 planes).
- **Theorem S64.2: for every nonic field K and every prime p unramified in K, V_K has a smooth ℚ_p-point.** With S64.1, local solubility of V_K reduces to the finitely many ramified primes of K. The Lang–Weil step of S63.5 is no longer needed for unramified primes.

**S64.3 (tangent-cone lemma at a pairing point).** [proof sketch, p-adic implicit function theorem] *unchecked*.
- (a) K_v = Q × A, where Q gives one pair (ℚ_v × ℚ_v or a quadratic étale algebra, ε ∈ Q anti-invariant, e0² = ε² ∈ ℚ_v^×) and deg A = 7. Suppose the cubic c_A(w) = Tr_A(w³) − (Tr_A w)³ has a ℚ_v-zero w0 with ∇c_A(w0) ≠ 0 and w0² not a scalar. Put u = (λw, σ/2 + ε) with σ = λ⁵s. Then P3/λ³ = c_A(w) + 3e0²λ²s + O(λ⁴) and P5/λ⁵ = Tr w⁵ − (Tr w)⁵ + 5e0⁴s + O(λ⁴). At λ = 0 the Jacobian in (w, s) has rank 2, so solutions exist for every small λ ≠ 0. The squares take the values λ²·conj(w²) (at least 2 distinct) and ≈ e0², so these points are smooth points of V_K.
- (b) K_v = ℚ_v × A′ with deg A′ = 8 (the ℚ_v factor pairs with the 10th value ζ). Put u = (1, λw′), w′ = w0′ + λ²τe with Tr e = 1. Then P3/λ³ = Tr w0′³ − 3τ + O(λ²) and (P5 − (5/3)P3)/λ³ = −(5/3)Tr w0′³ + O(λ²). This needs a ℚ_v-point of {Tr w′ = 0, Tr w′³ = 0} ⊂ P(A′) with w′² not a scalar (the Jacobian then has rank 2).

**S64.4 (ramified factors: Kummer and graded-cubic points).** [proof, short] *unchecked*.
- (a) **Kummer lemma.** Suppose a factor L = M(π) has π^e ∈ M, [L : M] = e and **e ∉ {1, 3, 5}**. Then u = (π, 0, …, 0) has Tr π^k = 0 for k = 1, 3, 5 and ζ = 0, so u ∈ V_K. It is smooth when π² has ≥ 2 distinct conjugates (e ≥ 4, or e = 2 with [M : ℚ_v] ≥ 2). This covers tame factors with e ∈ {4, 6, 7, 8, 9} (every tame totally ramified extension of U_f is Kummer) and wild ones of the form x⁷ − 7c, x⁹ − 3c.
- (b) **Graded cubic.** Take tame factors L_j = U_{f_j}(π_j) with π_j³ = ζ_j p (e_j = 3, p ≠ 3), and put u = (a_j π_j) on them, 0 elsewhere. Then Tr u = Tr u⁵ = 0 and Tr u³ = 3p Σ_j Tr_{U_j}(ζ_j a_j³). The form Σ Tr(ζ_j a_j³) in F₃ := Σ f_j variables is a diagonal (Fermat) cubic over F̄_p, smooth for p ≠ 3. **If F₃ ≥ 3** it has a nonzero smooth F_p-zero (Hasse–Weil for the plane cubic when F₃ = 3, Chevalley–Warning when F₃ ≥ 4), which lifts by Hensel to a smooth point of V_K (9 or more distinct squares together with 0). This covers the (e, f) = (3, 3) nonic fields, three ramified cubic factors, and (3,2) × (3,1).
