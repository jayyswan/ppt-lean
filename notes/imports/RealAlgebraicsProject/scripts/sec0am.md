**S62.1 (row B5: the identity, and L_135 is genuine).** [exact] (`b5sym.py`, sympy) + [numerical] planted end-to-end check (`b5plant.py`).
- Bézier (X, Y) = (α(t + s)² + β, cubic) and the graph y = S(x), S ∈ ℚ[x] of degree 5, under a P-affine map (x, y) ↦ (ax + b, cy + dx + e). The α, β dials are absorbed into the map, so τ ∈ K is good iff for some s, ζ ∈ P the 10 points y = (τ + s, ζ) ∈ K × P satisfy **p1 = p3 = p5 = 0** (degree-10 power sums: the w⁹, w⁷, w⁵ coefficients of Π(w − y_i) vanish), so that Π(w − y_i) = E(w²) + R(w) with E a monic quintic and R an odd cubic, **and E is P-affinely equivalent to a rational quintic up to linear terms**.
- Quintics modulo x-affine maps, y-scaling and added linear terms have **one invariant**: I = B2²/B3³ with, in power sums of the 10 points (odd ones zero), **B3 = (p2² − 10p4)/40** and **B2 = −(p2³ − 15p2p4 + 50p6)/300** (sympy, via Newton's identities; depressing checked). For I0 ∈ ℚ, x⁵ + I0x³ + I0²x² (I0 ≠ 0) or x⁵ ± x³ (I0 = 0) is a rational representative; the map uses a = √(−B3) or a rational expression in B2, B3 (P Euclidean). So **τ is good iff y ∈ L_135^{(10)}(K × P) with I(y) ∈ ℚ**.
- **The slice ζ = s = 0 is L_135 itself:** for u ∈ L_135, w·χ_u(w) = E(w²) + R(w) with E = V⁵ + a2V⁴ + a4V³ + a6V² + a8V and R = a7w³ + a9w. **Every u ∈ L_135 with distinct nonzero conjugates is genuine**: the Bézier is (t², −R(t)), birational (t = y/(a7x + a9)), and its crossings with the mapped graph are the nine u_i and t = 0. (Row E's analogue w³χ_u has an odd part of degree 5 and is genuine only on V_odd, S59.2; one auxiliary crossing instead of three removes that.)
- **Reduction (*unchecked*, short): r = 9 closes for K (over ℚ, and over P since S is rational and every dial is P) if the weight-0 function I(p2, p4, p6) takes a rational value at a P-point of L_135**, i.e. if some Ω_{I0} = L_135 ∩ {B2² = I0·B3³}, I0 ∈ ℚ, has a P-point (I0 = 0: the sextic hypersurface 50p6 − 15p2p4 + p2³ = 0 of L_135). This is one rationality condition on the P-dense L_135. Compare row E (two conditions on H_E, S60.4) and D_K (the single hypersurface W = L_135 ∩ {6p4 = p2²}); here the targets form a countable family whose union is dense in L_135(ℝ).
- Planted (`b5plant.py 1`, `b5plant.py 2`): Newton gives a real point of Ω_0 (residual 10⁻¹⁷); the depressed E has B2 ≈ 10⁻¹⁶, B3 = −0.3816; the Bézier (t², −R(t)) crosses the image of y = x⁵ − x³ under (x, y) ↦ (ax + h, a⁵y + mx + n), a = √(−B3) = 0.6177, exactly at the nine u_i and t = 0 (10 real crossings, error 10⁻¹³).
- Also (*unchecked*, short algebra): for u ∈ X = {p1 = p3 = 0} and ζ = −9s, p3 of the 10 points is 3s(p2(u) − 240s²), so s² = p2(u)/240 (a quadratic step over P) gives a second branch of 10-point sets with p1 = p3 = 0. On it p5 of the 10 points is p5(u) + s(5p4(u) − (59/60)p2(u)²).

**S62.2 (real test).** [numerical] (`b5real.py <r> <n> <N> <seed>`, 1500 Newton samples of real points of {p1 = p3 = p5 = 0}).

**S62.3 (linear and planar families; monodromy).** [exact mod p] (`b5deg.py`, msolve, p = 32003; `b5frob.py`, msolve over 10 primes; Singular cross-check `b5sing.py`).
- H5 = {u ∈ K : some s gives (u + s, ζ) ∈ L_135^{(10)}} (the good locus with I free, a hypersurface over ℚ containing L_135 + P·1) has **degree 15** on a random line (full Bézout 3·5; factor degrees mod p [2, 2, 11]).
- The good locus B5_{I0} (codimension 2 in K) has **degree 90 for I0 = 0 and 180 for a random I0** on a random plane (Bézout 3·5·6, 3·5·12). Control: L_135 ∩ {B2 = 0} on a centred 3-space, 90.
- One integer plane, 10 primes 40009 … 40111: common subset sums of the factor degrees **{0, 90}** (irreducible over ℚ), and the type **[17, 73]** at p = 40013, whose 17th power is a 73-cycle. 73 is prime with 45 < 73 ≤ 87, so the Galois group over ℚ is primitive and **contains A_90** (Jordan). No decomposition.
- **Method warning:** msolve misreads integer coefficients of about 2³¹ and above (the largest here was 3.7·10¹¹). It gave the type [1, 2, 87] at 40013 against Singular's [17, 73], and agreed after the coefficients were reduced mod p in Python. Earlier msolve inputs built over GF(p) (e60r.py, e61slice.py, b5deg.py) are unaffected.
