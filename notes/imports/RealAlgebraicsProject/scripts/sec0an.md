**S63.1 (I is a squared skewness).** [exact] (`b5q.py`, sympy). With V_i = y_i² (the 10 crossing parameters squared), mean m, variance σ², third central moment μ3: **B3 = −(5/2)σ², B2 = −(5/3)μ3, so I = B2²/B3³ = −(8/45)·γ², γ = μ3/σ³ the skewness of the ten values y_i².** Hence I ≤ 0 on real points (S62.2's B3 < 0 is σ² > 0), I ∈ ℚ ⇔ γ² ∈ ℚ, and the observed bound I ≥ −2/5 is |γ| ≤ 3/2.

**S63.2 (the bound I ≥ −2/5 and its extremal configurations).** [numerical] (`skew63.py 150 1`, SLSQP on real {p1 = p3 = p5 = 0} ⊂ ℝ^10). 148 of 150 starts converge, all to γ² = 9/4 (I = −2/5), and **every maximiser is a pairing configuration** y = (±a, ±b, ±b, ±b, ±b) (y² two-valued with multiplicities 2 and 8; any ratio a : b, a one-parameter family; a two-point distribution with weights 1/5, 4/5 has skewness exactly 3/2). No nondegenerate local maximum was found. Pairing configurations have no P-points for 2-transitive groups and are not field generators. **Candidate (d) of S62.4 ruled out.**

**S63.3 (row B5 over ℚ: no rationality gap; V_K is Fano).** [exact] (`b5q.py`) + [numerical] planted (`plant63.py`). Let K_ℚ be a nonic field over ℚ and u ∈ K_ℚ ∖ ℚ. With ζ = −Tr u ∈ ℚ the ten numbers {u_i} ∪ {ζ} have p1 = 0, and p3, p5 of the ten are Tr(u³) − (Tr u)³ and Tr(u⁵) − (Tr u)⁵. So
- **V_K := {u ∈ P(K_ℚ) : Tr(u³) = (Tr u)³, Tr(u⁵) = (Tr u)⁵}**, a complete intersection of degrees (3, 5) in P⁸ over ℚ, of dimension 6, with **K_V = O(−1): Fano of index 1**. (The 9-point L_135 is its hyperplane section Tr u = 0, a Calabi–Yau 5-fold.)
- **A ℚ-point u of V_K closes K_ℚ with one crossing, all data rational:** (w − ζ)χ_u(w) = E(w²) + R(w) with E ∈ ℚ[V] a monic quintic and R ∈ ℚ[w] an odd cubic; the Bézier (t², −R(t)) (rational control points) crosses the ℚ-quintic graph y = E(x) exactly at t = u_i and t = ζ, and t = −Y/(r3X + r1) recovers u_i from the crossing point (genuine as in S62.1: R ≢ 0, distinct crossings). Every u ∉ ℚ generates K_ℚ (primitive nonic groups have no intermediate fields), and the roots of the original nonic are ℚ-polynomials in u. **I is automatically rational**, so no P-point or rationality condition is involved.
- Compared with row E over ℚ (S60.1, S61.1: the good locus Gn = 0 is a degree-10 hypersurface in P⁸, K = O(1), general type) and row G (incidence of weights (4, 5, 7, 8) over P(1⁹, 1, 2, 3), K = O(9)), row B5 is the only row found whose over-ℚ incidence is Fano. *unchecked* (weighted canonical-class counts, short).

**S63.5 (local solubility of V_K for the test fields).** [exact mod p] + Hensel (`locplane63.py`, `loc63.py`), Lang–Weil *unchecked*.
- Method: coordinates in a basis of an order that is maximal at the primes in question (PARI `nfinit([f, listP])`); F3, F5 as integer forms in the 9 coordinates. For p ≥ 7: random affine F_p-planes, common F_p-roots of F3, F5 via a resultant, and a point where the 2 × 9 Jacobian has rank 2 mod p (Hensel gives a ℚ_p-point). For p ∈ {2, 3, 5}: random points mod p^N and a 2 × 2 minor of valuation m with v(F3), v(F5) ≥ 2m + 1 (multivariate Hensel). **Note:** on an unramified residue algebra F3 ≡ 0 mod 3 and F5 ≡ 0 mod 5 identically (Tr(a^p) = Tr(a) and t^p = t in F_p), so smooth points mod 3 and mod 5 never exist and the p-adic search is needed there. Validated on the planted field (global point): 2 (m = 2), 3 (m = 3), 5 (m = 1), 7 (m = 0).
- **PSL(2,8) r = 9 test field** (disc 3⁴·37⁶·2977717⁴, matching LMFDB 9.9.163…809.1): ℚ_2 (m = 0), ℚ_3 (m = 2, v(F) = (6, 5)), ℚ_5 (m = 1); smooth F_p-points for every other prime p ≤ 10⁶ (all on the first few planes), including the ramified 37 and 2977717.
- **S_9 control** (disc 5·2897·2192761·4172210359447267·1200747702100008604489847184402079; the 55-digit part of the discriminant factors as the last three): ℚ_2, ℚ_3, ℚ_5 by Hensel; smooth F_p-points for every other p ≤ 10⁶ and at all five ramified primes (p = 806257 needed more than 12 planes; found with 200).
- Large p (*unchecked*): for p ≥ 7 unramified, V_K mod p is a twisted form of V̄ = {p1 = p3 = p5 = 0} ⊂ P⁹, a normal complete intersection (singular locus = the pairing curves, dimension 1), hence geometrically integral; Cafure–Matera (|#C(F_q) − q⁷| ≤ 182q^{6.5} + 5·15^{13/3}q⁶ for the affine cone, degree 15) gives smooth points once 182/√q + 624260/q < 1, i.e. q ≥ 8.5·10⁵, which overlaps the computed range. Real points: S62.2.
- **So V_K is everywhere locally soluble for both test fields.** If V_K satisfies the Hasse principle, both close over ℚ (the PSL(2,8) r = 9 test field included). For smooth complete intersections of dimension ≥ 3 the Hasse principle is Colliot-Thélène's conjecture (no Brauer–Manin obstruction is expected there); **V_K is singular along the pairing curves** (configurations with y_i² two-valued, coming from 5 pairs ±a, ±b), so what is needed is the Hasse principle for a desingularisation, whose Brauer group has not been computed (*unchecked*). No unconditional theorem covers a (3, 5) system in 9 variables (circle-method ranges are far larger).
- Why not search: for a Fano of index 1 the count of ℚ-points of height ≤ H is about σ·H with σ ≈ (product of local densities)/(size of the coefficients of F3, F5); for these fields σ is tiny, so naive or fibred searches cannot reach the expected heights (*unchecked*, heuristic).
