## 0ap. Session 65 (Nov 21): results

Topic: priority 1, scope (i): the global step for V_K (nonics, A = K × ℚ) and W_K (decics, A = K). Both are twists of the same variety **V̄ = {p1 = p3 = p5 = 0} ⊂ P⁹** by the degree-10 étale algebra A, so everything here is stated for V_A. **Mechanism named: the Hasse principle through the Brauer–Manin obstruction** (Colliot-Thélène's conjecture: on a smooth projective, geometrically rationally connected variety over a number field, rational points are dense in the Brauer–Manin set). **Tests that could fail:** (a) a nonzero class in H¹(ℚ, Pic X̃̄) for a resolution X̃ (non-permutation Picard lattice from the exceptional divisors, as for nodal Fano threefolds); (b) a transcendental class, Br(X̃̄) ≠ 0 (Artin–Mumford type, coming from the singularities); (c) singularities worse than klt, which would leave rational connectedness, and so the conjecture's hypothesis, open. **Result: none of the three occurs.** For every A, any smooth projective model X̃ of V_A has Br(X̃) = Br(ℚ), and X̃ is rationally connected. So there is no Brauer–Manin obstruction of any kind, and under Colliot-Thélène's conjecture V_A has Zariski-dense ℚ-points as soon as it has smooth local points everywhere (which S64 proves at every real place and every unramified prime, for every K). The unconditional global step remains open.

**S65.1 (the singular locus of V̄).** [proof, short] *unchecked* (write-up). In characteristic 0 a point y of V̄ is singular iff the 3 × 10 Jacobian (rows 1, 3y_i², 5y_i⁴) has rank ≤ 2, i.e. iff the values y_i² take at most two distinct values (a 3 × 3 Vandermonde in three distinct y_i² is nonzero). With values ±a (net count d_a) and ±b (net count d_b), a² ≠ b², ab ≠ 0, the equations p1 = p3 = 0 read d_a a + d_b b = d_a a³ + d_b b³ = 0, whose determinant ab(b² − a²) ≠ 0 forces d_a = d_b = 0; if b = 0 then d_a = 0. So **Sing(V̄) is the union of the lines L_{A,B} = ⟨e_A, e_B⟩**, where e_A = 1_{A+} − 1_{A−} with |A+| = |A−| = k, e_B likewise with 5 − k, and A ⊔ B = {1, …, 10} (k = 1, …, 4); there are **(1/8)·Σ_{k=1}^{4} 10!/(k!²(5 − k)!²) = 7875 lines**. Three kinds of points:
- type 1: a general point a e_A + b e_B (a² ≠ b², ab ≠ 0), on one line only;
- type 2: b·e_B with a = 0, i.e. n pairs ±1 and z = 10 − 2n zeros (z ∈ {2, 4, 6, 8}), on the lines through the pairings of the zero set (one line for z = 2);
- type 3: the 126 points e (five pairs ±1, all y_i² equal; Jacobian rank 1), each on 125 lines.
For A primitive (nonic or decic field) none of these lines or points is defined over ℚ (the Galois group would have to preserve a nontrivial partition of the conjugates).

**S65.2 (the singularities are terminal; V̄ and every resolution are rationally connected).** [numerical] (`sing65.py hess`) + [exact] (`sing65.py type3`, `type2`, Singular over ℚ) + *unchecked* (assembly).
- Type 1: with ∇p5 = λ∇p3 + μ∇p1 at y (λ = (5/3)(a² + b²)), the Hessian of p5 − λp3 − μp1 is diag(10y_i(2y_i² − a² − b²)), nonzero on every coordinate. On T = ker dp1 ∩ ker dp3 (dimension 8) its rank is **6 on 12 of 12 random points (k = 1, …, 4)**, with radical exactly ⟨e_A, e_B⟩. So V̄ is, along the open part of each line, a line times an ordinary double point of dimension 5 (a quadric cone in 6 variables): terminal.
- Type 3: at e = (1⁵, (−1)⁵), with y = e + z, p3 ≡ 3Σe_i z_i² + Σz_i³ and p5 − (10/3)p3 ≡ (20/3)Σz_i³ + O(z⁴) on Σz = 0, and both initial forms are invariant under z ↦ z + te modulo each other. **The tangent cone is the cone over Y = {Σe_i z_i² = 0, Σz_i³ = 0} ⊂ P⁷** (on {Σz = 0}/⟨e⟩). Singular, exact over ℚ: Y is a (2,3) complete intersection 5-fold of degree 6 whose singular locus is **125 reduced points**, one for each singular line through e (Σ_{k=1}^{4} C(5,k)²/2 = 125), so they are nodes. Blowing up e gives exceptional divisor Y with discrepancy 7 − 2 − 3 = **2**; along the 125 nodes the strict transforms of the singular lines pass with their type-1 transversal double points. Terminal.
- Type 2: at y = e_B (n pairs, z zeros), after eliminating the two smooth directions, the local equation on a smooth 7-fold is g = −5Q(x) + (5/3)C(u) + (terms of higher weight), where Q = Σ_B e_i w_i² is nondegenerate in the 2n − 2 variables x (on {Σ_B w = 0}/⟨e_B⟩) and C = Σ_Z u_i³ on {Σ_Z u = 0} (z − 1 variables u); with weights 3 on x and 2 on u, every other term has weight > 6. Singular, exact over ℚ: **{Σu = 0, Σu³ = 0} ⊂ P^{z−1} has exactly C(z, z/2)/2 = 3, 10, 35 singular points for z = 4, 6, 8**, each of Jacobian multiplicity 1 (nodes; z = 6 is the Segre cubic), at the pairings of Z, i.e. along the singular lines. The weighted blowup has discrepancy 3(2n − 2) + 2(z − 1) − 7 = 7, 9, 11 (z = 8, 6, 4); for z = 2 the cubic vanishes on the one u-direction and the point is a type-1 point (transversal quadric of rank 6). Terminal, *unchecked* (the weighted-leading-form step).
- **So V̄ is a Gorenstein Fano 6-fold (K = O(−1)) with terminal singularities, hence rationally connected (Q. Zhang, Crelle 2006: log terminal Fano varieties are rationally connected), and so is every resolution X̃ of every twist V_A (rational connectedness is a geometric, birational property).**

**S65.3 (Theorem: no Brauer–Manin obstruction on V_A).** [proof] *unchecked* (citations not rechecked against the sources). For every étale ℚ-algebra A of degree 10 and every smooth projective model X̃ of V_A, **Br(X̃) = Br(ℚ)** (the same over any number field).
- Algebraic part. V̄ is a complete intersection of dimension 6 (lci, Cohen–Macaulay) singular in codimension 5, so it is normal, and **locally factorial** by Grothendieck's theorem (SGA2 XI 3.14: an lci local ring regular in codimension ≤ 3 is a UFD). Grothendieck–Lefschetz (SGA2 XII 3.7, complete intersections of dimension ≥ 3) gives **Pic V̄ = Cl V̄ = ℤ·H**. For a resolution π: X̃ → V_A (defined over ℚ; Hironaka's resolution is functorial) the exceptional prime divisors E_i of X̃̄ are permuted by Galois, they are independent in Pic (negativity lemma), and Pic(X̃̄ ∖ ∪E_i) = Cl(V̄_sm) = ℤH. So **Pic X̃̄ ≅ ℤ·π*H ⊕ ⊕_i ℤE_i, a permutation module**, and H¹(ℚ, Pic X̃̄) = 0 (Shapiro: H¹(G_L, ℤ) = 0). Hence Br₁(X̃)/Br(ℚ) ≅ H¹(ℚ, Pic X̃̄) = 0 (H³(ℚ, G_m) = 0). Another smooth projective model is birational to this one, and Br of smooth projective varieties is a birational invariant.
- Transcendental part. By the Lefschetz theorem for complete intersections with lci singularities (Hamm–Lê; Dimca, *Singularities and topology of hypersurfaces*, ch. 5), H^i(V̄, ℤ) = H^i(P⁹, ℤ) for i < 6: H² = ℤ (generated by c1(H)), H³ = 0. Removing the singular lines (real codimension 10) changes nothing in degrees ≤ 3 (lci have rectified homotopical depth = dimension), so H²(V̄_sm, ℤ) = ℤ·c1(H), H³(V̄_sm, ℤ) = 0, and the Kummer sequence gives Br(V̄_sm) = 0 (Pic ⊗ ℚ/ℤ fills H²(ℚ/ℤ)). Since X̃̄ ⊃ π⁻¹(V̄_sm) ≅ V̄_sm and Br of a regular integral scheme injects into Br of a dense open, **Br(X̃̄) = 0**: no Artin–Mumford classes (the transversal double points have normal quadrics of dimension 4, not the 3-fold nodes of Artin–Mumford).
- Consequence: **V_A has no Brauer–Manin obstruction, algebraic or transcendental, for any A.** The only possible obstruction to V_A(ℚ) ≠ ∅ that the conjectures allow is a local one, and S64 excludes it at every real place and every unramified prime.

**S65.4 (conditional closure).** *unchecked* (short, given S65.2–S65.3 and the conjecture). Assume Colliot-Thélène's conjecture for smooth projective geometrically rationally connected varieties over ℚ. If V_A has a smooth ℚ_v-point for every place v, then X̃(A_ℚ) ≠ ∅, Br = Br(ℚ) imposes nothing, and **V_A(ℚ) is dense in ∏_v V_A(ℚ_v) (weak approximation), in particular Zariski dense.** The genuineness conditions of S63.3 (u ∉ ℚ, R ≢ 0, distinct crossings; for decics u generating K) cut out a proper closed subset, nonempty complement by the planted points of S63.3, so a genuine ℚ-point exists. Hence, under the conjecture:
- **every nonic field K over ℚ** (in particular the totally real PSL(2,8), PΓL(2,8), A_9 and S_9 cases, the only open ones for rational nonics) **whose ramified local algebras are covered by S64.1–S64.4** (or checked one by one, a finite computation per K) **is closed by row B5 with one crossing**; this includes **the PSL(2,8) r = 9 test field and the S_9 control** (fully everywhere locally soluble, S63.5);
- **every primitive decic field K** with W_K everywhere locally soluble is closed the same way (S63.4); for W_K the real places and the m ≥ 2 unramified types are covered by the involution lemma, and the unramified types with m ≤ 1 are (9,1), (7,3), (7,2,1), (7,1,1,1), (5,3,2), (5,3,1,1) (Chevalley–Warning applies: 1 + 3 + 5 < 10; their exact singular counts are not computed).
So the rational degree bound reaches 10 conditionally on Colliot-Thélène's conjecture and on the finitely many local checks per field left by S64.6. Over P (scope (ii)) nothing changes.

**S65.5 (status and decision).**
- **Done:** the Brauer–Manin side of the global step (S65.3) and rational connectedness (S65.2). What is left for scope (i) is (1) the conjecture itself, i.e. an unconditional construction of ℚ-points on V_A, and (2) the local checks of S64.6.
- **No unconditional theorem applies off the shelf:** the circle method needs far more than 9 variables for a (3,5) system (Browning–Heath-Brown, forms of differing degrees), and the fibration method needs a fibration of V_A over ℚ with fibres satisfying the Hasse principle. The natural map V_A → V̄/S_10 = P(2,4,6,8,10,7,9) (coefficients of E and R) is finite, so V_A(ℚ) ≠ ∅ says exactly that the 6-parameter family E(w²) + R(w) is versal for A (essential dimension of S_10 is at least 5, so no dimension obstruction).
- **Next:** (1) an unconditional mechanism for V_A(ℚ): a fibration of V_A (or of a variety dominating it) over P¹ or P² with rationally connected fibres, fibres of a type where the Hasse principle is a theorem (quadrics, Châtelet-type surfaces, cubic hypersurfaces in ≥ 10 variables), using the P⁴'s of pairing configurations and the type-3 tangent cones (cones over the (2,3) 5-fold Y, which has rational structure); (2) the local leftovers: the S64.6 shapes for p ≥ 11 and the wild algebras at 2, 3, 5, 7; for W_K the six unramified types with m ≤ 1; (3) over P (r = 9): the S62.4 items and G3_S.
