## 0av. Session 71 (Nov 21): results

Topic: the Nov 21 order, item (1) (a P-dial on the fixed rational curve's shape, via offsets), then a first slice of items (2) and (5) (power curves under rules (6)–(7), and decics over ℚ). **Mechanisms named:** (a) an offset of the P-affine image of the ℚ-quintic graph of row B5, whose distance d might free the invariant I; (b) power curves crossed with toolkit partners, read in the curve's own coordinate c, as new engines for nonics over P (r = 7, 9) and decics. **Tests that could fail:** (a) whether a crossing of the offset at a generator keeps the B5 incidence with I free; (b) whether a power-curve row has a Tschirnhaus variety with a P-point source, or reaches r = 7, 9 in low codimension. **Results:** (a) fails: offsets replace the rationality gap by a degree-9 point on a foot cover of genus ≥ 1 and lose the B5 structure (S71.1). (b) In c every power-curve row is lacunary (S71.2); with graph partners there is nothing new over P in degrees 9, 10; with Bézier partners the y³ = x⁴ rows reach r = 7 in codimension 3 but have no P-point source (S71.3). Over ℚ, x^10 × ℚ-sextic reduces decics with a nonreal root to a (2,3) Fano target V_3, which fails over the S70.2 3-field (S71.4).

**S71.1 (offsets do not free I; item (1) closed).** *unchecked* (short, complete) + [exact] (`offs71.py`).
- Setting: Γ = A(graph of E0), E0 ∈ ℚ[x] of degree m ≥ 2, A a P-affine map, O_d the offset of Γ at P-distance d. Pulling A back, O_d is the offset of the graph in a positive definite metric with P entries: the point over the foot x is (x, E0(x)) + (d/s)·M⁻¹(−E0′(x), 1), with s² = q_M(E0′(x)) and q_M(u) = (−u, 1)M⁻¹(−u, 1)ᵀ a positive definite binary quadratic in u.
- The foot map makes O_d birational to the double cover C_M: s² = q_M(E0′(x)). So a crossing point whose coordinates generate K (degree 9) gives (x, s) ∈ C_M(K) with x ∉ P: **a point of degree 9 with residue field K on C_M** (the per-layer square condition of G1a.3, now with a generating foot).
- **Genus.** Write q_M = (u − α)(u − ᾱ), α ∉ ℝ. The roots of E0′ − α and E0′ − ᾱ are disjoint and conjugate, so the odd-multiplicity roots of q_M(E0′(x)) number 2k and genus(C_M) = k − 1, with k ≡ m − 1 mod 2. For **m = 5** (E0′ quartic), k = 0 would make E0′ − α = G², G = A + iB with A, B real, and E0′ real forces AB constant, so E0′ constant: impossible. **So genus ≥ 1 for every metric**, with equality iff E0′ − α has a double root, i.e. α = E0′(c) with E0″(c) = 0 and c ∉ ℝ (then Re α, |α|² ∈ L ⊂ P, so the matched metric is P-rational). [exact] for E0 = x⁵ + 2x⁴ − x³ + 3x² − x: genus 3 for a random metric, 1 for the matched one. (m = 4: a real cubic E0′ with non-real critical points gives genus 0 in the matched metric, *unchecked*; m = 6: genus 0 needs two critical points of E0′ with the same non-real critical value, finitely many shapes, *unchecked*.)
- **Degrees.** The offset of a quintic graph has degree 18 and meets a cubic Bézier in 54 points (Res_x of the normal and distance equations: degree 54 in t, squarefree) [exact]. The B5 incidence (crossing polynomial E(w²) + R(w) of degree 10, L_135 genuine, S62.1) is gone.
- **Count.** A generating crossing needs (i) a degree-9 point with residue field K on C_M (genus 3; genus 1 for the matched metric, whose j-invariant runs over a countable set as E0 runs over ℚ-quintics) and (ii) the nine conjugates of the offset point on the partner: through nine given points a polynomial cubic is 3 conditions over P (singular, flex at infinity tangent to the line at infinity). With the matched metric the dials left are d and the translates x* + E(P) of one K-point of the genus-one curve: 2 dials for 3 conditions, on top of (i). **So d does not act on I; the offset trades the one-invariant gap on the P-dense L_135 for a harder problem.** The Bézier side gives nothing either: the Pythagorean-hodograph Béziers of shape (t², cubic) are C = (2/(3c))t³ − (c/2)t + e, and their offsets meet the quintic graph in a degree-20 polynomial.

**S71.2 (power curves in their own coordinate: the lacunary lemma).** *unchecked* (elementary) + [numerical] (`lac71.py`).
- Rules (6)–(7) give the whole real locus of y^q = x^p (gcd(p, q) = 1; the other branches by reflections), with the P-rational parametrisation (x, y) = (c^q, c^p). If the partner has implicit equation F = Σ f_ij x^i y^j over P, the crossings are the roots of G(c) = F(c^q, c^p)/c^m (m = multiplicity of the cusp c = 0), with support in {iq + jp − m}. **So in the coordinate c the Tschirnhaus variety lies in the lacunary locus {e_{n−e}(c) = 0 for every exponent e missing from the support}** (a weighted complete intersection of elementary symmetric functions), cut by the conditions that make F a toolkit curve. When F runs over a linear system (graphs with all coefficients free), Ts_c is exactly the lacunary locus.
- Filters: (Descartes) a real-rooted polynomial has no two consecutive vanishing coefficients (trailing zeros aside), so a support missing two consecutive exponents has no totally real members. (Reciprocals) e_k(c) = e_n(c)·e_{n−k}(1/c), so vanishing top coefficients are power sums of w = 1/c (e_{n−1}(c) = e_{n−2}(c) = 0 ⇔ Tr w = Tr w² = 0).
- **Graph partners over P (P-quartic graphs with all P-affine maps):** support {p, 0, q, 2q, 3q, 4q}, degree max(p, 4q). Degree 9: (p, q) = (9, 1), x⁹ = quartic (V_4, T1), or (9, 2), c⁹ = Q(c²) (exponents 9, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0), the reciprocal of the T2 odd engine t·R(t²) − e (Ts = V_odd in 1/c). Degree 10: only (10, 1), V_5 (S51.2). **So power curves give no new engine over P in degrees 9 and 10 with graph partners.**
- **Graph partners over ℚ (ℚ-graphs of degree ≤ 6):** degree 10: (10, 1) with a sextic, Ts = V_3 = {e1 = e2 = e3 = 0} (S71.4); (p, 2) with a quintic and p odd ≤ 9: the lacunary CIs {e_i = 0, i ∈ S} with S = {3,5,7,9}, {1,5,7,9}, {1,3,7,9}, {1,3,5,9}, {1,3,5,7}: weight sum ≥ 16, so K = O(≥ 6) on P⁹, general type (W_K, row B5, has Fano index 1).
