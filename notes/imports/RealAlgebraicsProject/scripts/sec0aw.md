## 0aw. Session 72 (Nov 21): results

Topic: item (1) of the S71 order, decics over ℚ with a nonreal root through V_3 = {Tr u = Tr u² = Tr u³ = 0} ⊂ P(K_0) (x^10 × ℚ-sextic, S71.4). **Mechanisms named:** (a) Hilbert's 3-plane: a ℚ-P³ Λ inside the quadric Q = {Tr u² = 0} and the cubic surface S_Λ = Λ ∩ {Tr u³ = 0}, with a ℚ-point forced by a Galois-stable set of skew lines on S_Λ (2 conjugate skew lines give points; 3, 4, 5, 6 skew lines blow down to a del Pezzo surface of degree 6, 7, 8 or to P², all with the Hasse principle), uniformly in Λ if the monodromy of the 27 lines over the family of Λ is smaller than W(E6); (b) secant descent: points of degree prime to 3 on plane sections C_Π = Π ∩ {Tr u³ = 0} (Π a ℚ-plane in Q); (c) cubic forms in ≥ 10 variables mapping to V_3 (Heath-Brown). **Tests that could fail:** (a) the Witt index of Tr u² on K_0 over ℚ (is there a ℚ-P³ at all?) and the 27-line monodromy; (b), (c) whether the reformulation gains anything over V_3(ℚ) itself. **Results:** (a) a ℚ-P³ exists only for s ≥ 4 and a local condition, which fails for both dense S_10 test fields with s ≥ 4 (S72.1); the monodromy is all of W(E6), so Galois-stable skew sets occur only on a thin set of Λ (S72.3). (b) is equivalent to the problem (S72.4). (c) cannot enter through linear maps (S72.4). Side results: V_3 is smooth and locally soluble at every unramified p ≥ 11 (S72.2); with P-dials the decic gap is two rational invariants of degrees 30 and 20 on the P-dense V_3 (S72.5).

**S72.1 (Witt index of the trace form on K_0 over ℚ).** *unchecked* (short, complete) + [exact] (`witt72.py`).
- On K_0 (dimension 9) Tr u² has signature (r + s − 1, s). Over ℚ the Witt index is **exactly s for s ≤ 3** (split off hyperbolic planes one at a time: while the remainder has dimension ≥ 5 and is indefinite it is isotropic (Meyer); after s planes it is definite), and for **s ∈ {4, 5} it is 4 iff the residual ternary form (signature (2,1) or (1,2)) is isotropic at every prime, else 3**. So ℚ-lines in Q need s ≥ 2, ℚ-planes s ≥ 3, ℚ-P³'s s ≥ 4 plus a local (Hasse-invariant) condition; for s = 1 (r = 8) Q is Lorentzian and holds no ℚ-line.
- [exact] (PARI `qfsolve`, iterated Witt decomposition; S_10 certified by a 7-cycle plus an odd Frobenius class, `pick72.py`): x^10 + x³ − 2x + 1 ((r, s) = (2, 4)): index 4. Dense fields: x^10 − 4x^9 − 4x^8 − 4x^7 + 4x^6 − 4x^5 + 2x^4 − x³ + 2x² − 4x + 4 ((2, 4)): **index 3, the ternary residual is anisotropic at 367** (a ramified prime); x^10 + 4x^9 + 4x^8 − x^7 + 2x^6 − 4x^5 + 3x^4 + x³ + 4x² − x + 4 ((0, 5)): **index 3, anisotropic at 17**; x^10 + 4x^9 + x^8 + x^7 + 3x^6 + 4x^4 − 4x³ + 2x² + 4x − 2 ((4, 3)): 3; x^10 − 4x^9 − 4x^8 + 3x^7 − 4x^6 + x^5 + 4x^4 + 3x³ + x² − 2x − 1 ((6, 2)): 2; x^10 − 2x^8 + x^5 − 3x + 1 ((4, 3)): 3.
- Remark: for x^10 + x³ − 2x + 1 the ℚ-P³ already contains a line of V_3, because θ itself has p1 = … = p6 = 0 (charpoly x^10 + (degree ≤ 3)); any field with a generator of charpoly x^10 + (degree ≤ 6) is closed by the engine itself. Test fields must be dense. For the dense fields the `qfsolve` planes have cubic coefficients of size 10^19–10^21 (field discriminants ~10^19), so small-height searches on V_3 are out of reach there (`surf72.py`).

**S72.2 (V_3 is smooth; local solubility at unramified p ≥ 11).** *unchecked* (short, complete).
- The Jacobian rows (1, …, 1), (2x_i), (3x_i²) have rank < 3 iff the coordinates take at most two values (Vandermonde). With values a (k times) and b (10 − k times), p1 = p2 = 0 force a = b = 0 in characteristic 0 and in characteristic p ∤ 30 unless p | k (only p = 7, k = 7). So **V̄_3 is a smooth (2,3) complete intersection** (Fano 6-fold of index 4), with smooth reduction at every unramified p ≥ 11. Chevalley–Warning (degrees 1 + 2 + 3 < 10 variables) gives a nonzero F_p-point, Hensel lifts it: **V_3(ℚ_p) ≠ ∅ for every unramified p ≥ 11**, and V_3(ℝ) ≠ ∅ iff r ≤ 8 (S71.4). Bad cases: p = 2, 3 (the integral model degenerates: Q ≡ p1² mod 2, Tr u³ ≡ p1³ mod 3), p = 5 ((1 : … : 1) is a singular F_5-point for every twist), p = 7 (singular points a on a Frobenius-stable 7-set, 0 on the rest) and ramified p. (Local results serve the paused conditional route; recorded because they are short.)

**S72.3 (Hilbert's 3-plane: the 27 lines have full W(E6) monodromy).** [numerical] (`we6_72.py`).
- Split model over F_p: V = {Σx = 0} ⊂ F_p^10, Q = Σx², T = Σx³ (the twist does not change geometric monodromy). Random maximal isotropic Λ (dimension 4, built from null spaces of the previous vectors), S_Λ = P(Λ) ∩ {T = 0}, exact point count #S_Λ(F_p) = p² + a·p + 1 with a = 1 + Tr(Frob | E6). W(E6) by BFS on the six simple reflections (51840 elements), distribution of a: −2: 0.0015, −1: 0.0668, 0: 0.2250, 1: 0.4016, 2: 0.2528, 3: 0.0469, 4: 0.0046, 5: 0.0007.
- Observed: p = 31, 2500 surfaces: −2: 0.0008, −1: 0.0644, 0: 0.2192, 1: 0.3812, 2: 0.2740, 3: 0.0516, 4: 0.0084, 5: 0.0004; p = 37, 1500 surfaces: 0.0013, 0.0673, 0.2680, 0.3833, 0.2293, 0.0467, 0.0040, 0. Every surface smooth (a·p ≡ #S − p² − 1 always divisible by p). The distribution matches W(E6) within sampling error, including the rare classes a = −2 (trace −3) and a = 4, 5; a proper subgroup would shift these proportions (not tested class by class, so the identification is numerical).
- **Consequence:** by Hilbert irreducibility the line group of S_Λ is W(E6) for Λ outside a thin subset of OG(4,9)(ℚ); a Galois-stable skew set needs a special Λ, i.e. a ℚ-point on a cover of degree ≥ 216 of OG(4,9). Mechanism (a) is dead as a uniform mechanism (it offers only a thin-set search, and only when the Witt index is 4).

**S72.4 (secant descent and cubic forms: equivalent or inapplicable).** *unchecked* (short).
- A plane cubic with a point of degree prime to 3 has a ℚ-point (index divides 3; Riemann–Roch in genus 1); on C_Π a quadratic point x suffices, since the line xx̄ lies in Π ⊂ Q and its third point is rational. But this is **equivalent to the problem**: V_3(ℚ) ≠ ∅ ⇔ V_3 has a quadratic point x with B(x, x̄) = 0 ⇔ some ℚ-line of Q has a binary cubic Tr(u³)|ℓ with a rational root (write x = a + √δ·b: B(x, x̄) = 0 and the trace conditions make a, b span a ℚ-isotropic line, and (1 : √δ) is a root of the binary cubic, which then splits off a rational root). The same holds for tangency (a ℚ-line of Q tangent to {Tr u³ = 0}: the double root is rational) and for ℚ-lines of X_A tangent to Q (S67 setting): each is a ℚ-point problem of the same kind.
- **Heath-Brown cannot enter linearly:** linear spaces in Q have projective dimension ≤ 3 (S72.1), every polynomial family u = ℓ(x) + q(x)·o (o isotropic, q quadratic) lying in Q is an isotropic linear space, and the rational parametrisation of Q (projection from a ℚ-point) turns Tr u³ into a sextic in 8 variables; Weil restriction gives m cubic conditions, not one. So mechanism (c) of the S71 Next list is dead in linear form.

**S72.5 (P-dials for decics: a gap of two invariants).** *unchecked* (short).
- (ax + e)^10 crossing a P-affine image (x, cx + dS(x) + f) of a ℚ-sextic graph gives, for X = ax + e, the charpoly X^10 − [dS((X − e)/a) + linear]; P-affine maps with a shear into x (a12 ≠ 0) give degree 60. So u ∈ V_3(P) (dense for r ≤ 8) is good iff the sextic part T = t6X⁶ + … + t2X² (t6 = −e4, t5 = e5, t4 = −e6, t3 = e7, t2 = −e8) has rational invariants J1 = t6·t3′²/t4′³ and J2 = t6·t2′/t4′² (primes: depressed coefficients), i.e. N3²/N4³ and N2/N4² with N4, N3, N2 of degrees 10, 15, 20 in u. **The decic gap (over ℚ with P-dials, and over P) is two invariants of degrees 30 and 20 on the P-dense V_3**, against B5's single invariant on L_135 for nonics (S62). Over ℚ alone the problem stays V_3(ℚ).
- Over P (remark): Artin–Schreier (Euclidean + no odd-degree extensions ⇒ real closed) reduces even degrees to odd ones only through odd-index subgroups; for A_10 / S_10 the smallest is the pair stabiliser (index 45), so it gives no reduction of decics to nonics.
