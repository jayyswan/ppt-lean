## 0x. Session 47 (Nov 18): results

Topic: priority item 1 after session 46 (A_9, r = 5 over P): the degeneration loci R′ and R of the two-point slice (S46.1, S46.3).

Notation as in S46.1 (frozen archive 45–46): Q1, Q2 ∈ V_3, m = Tr(Q1Q2), a = Tr(Q1²Q2), b = Tr(Q1Q2²), L the 4-dimensional space of directions u, the pencil Π(λ) = λ1·Tr((a − 2mQ1)U²)|_L + λ2·Tr((b − 2mQ2)U²)|_L with base curve E ⊂ P(L), and S3 the cubic surface. Δ(λ) = det Π(λ) is the discriminant of the pencil, a binary quartic.

**S47.1 (E as a p3-divisor condition).** *unchecked* (elementary).
- For u ∈ L with Tr(u²) = −2m, the conic q(κ) = Q1 + κu + κ²Q2 lies in V_2 (the coefficients of p2|q are 0, 2Tr(Q1u), Tr(u²) + 2m, 2Tr(Q2u), 0), and p3|q is a binary sextic in κ with coefficients 0, 3Tr(Q1²u), 3(a + Tr(Q1u²)), Tr(u³) + 6Tr(Q1Q2u), 3(b + Tr(Q2u²)), 3Tr(Q2²u), 0. The equations of S46.1 other than the cubic one say exactly that every coefficient except the middle one vanishes. So **the points of E are the conics C ⊂ V_2 through Q1 and Q2 with p3|C = c·κ³, i.e. with p3-divisor 3Q1 + 3Q2**, and S3 is the condition c = 0. (A real point of E with s < 0 gives u = U/(i√|s|); substituting κ = iκ′ turns it into a real conic through Q1 and −Q2, which also lies in V_3's cone.)
- For any plane ⟨Q1, u, Q2⟩ ⊂ {p1 = 0}, the conic C = Π ∩ V_2 contains Q1, Q2, and p3|C has 4 further zeros. So **a P-plane through two P-points of V_3 gives further P-points of V_3 by a quartic step** (when a residual root is real). This is a cheap supply of P-points of V_3, not of conics in V_3.

**S47.2 (the loci R′ and R: real points exist).** [numerical] (`deg47.py`) + *unchecked* (classical classification of pencils of quadrics in P³; expected dimensions).
- **R′: E contains a line.** A pencil of quadrics in P³ whose base curve is a line ℓ plus a twisted cubic (meeting ℓ twice) has Segre symbol [22]: Δ is a square with two double roots, both of rank 3 (*unchecked*, classical). Then ℓ is the only line in E, so it is defined over P for a P-point Q2, and E ∩ S3 = (ℓ ∩ S3) + (twisted cubic ∩ S3) splits as 12 = 3 + 9: **a cubic step, which always has a real root.** Expected codimension 2 in Q2 (pencils containing a given line: 6 conditions; lines in P³: 4 parameters), so R′ is a 3-fold in the projective 5-fold V_3. The spurious case ℓ ⊂ {A1 = A2 = A4 = 0} (s ≡ 0 on ℓ) costs 9 conditions against 9 parameters, so it is not the generic point of R′ (none was found).
  [numerical] `deg47.py line 5 30 2` and `deg47.py line 5 60 4` (r = 5, Q1 fixed; Q2 and two spanning vectors of ℓ by Levenberg–Marquardt with 3 random affine slices on Q2): 43 points of R′. On every one, Δ has two double roots (root separation ≤ 10⁻⁵ at LM accuracy) at which the member has rank 3, as [22] predicts. ℓ ∩ S3 has 1 real point on 41 samples and 3 on 2 (both with m > 0). Of the 15 samples with m < 0, 13 give a real conic (s > 0), and **one has p4 with 2 real roots on that conic** (min p4/|y|⁴ = −0.072); on the other 12, min p4/|y|⁴ lies between 0.011 and 0.16.
- **R: a rank-2 member.** Δ has a double root at which the member has rank 2. E is the union of two conics, cut by the two planes of that member; the member is unique, so the pair of planes is defined over P, and each plane is defined over P when the member's nonzero part is indefinite (P is Euclidean). Then E ∩ S3 splits as 12 = 6 + 6: **two sextic steps**, each needing a real root. Codimension 2 (rank ≤ 2 quadrics have codimension 3 among quadrics in P³).
  [numerical] `deg47.py rank2 5 30 3` and `deg47.py rank2 5 60 5`: 62 converged points, of which 28 are spurious (m = 0, where q1 and q2 become proportional) and 34 genuine. Of these, 9 have m < 0 and real planes; 5 of the 9 have real points of E ∩ S3 with s > 0, and **3 have such a point with p4 real-rooted** (min p4/|y|⁴ down to −0.024).
- So both loci meet every real condition (m < 0 by Lemma C, s > 0, p4 real-rooted) on open parts of their real points: no real obstruction for r = 5 here.

**S47.3 (no mechanism; decision).**
- A P-point of R′ or R is a P-point on a codimension-2 subvariety of V_3 (a 3-fold; or of an 8-fold in V_3 × V_3 if Q1 moves as well). The known supplies of P-points of V_3 (a P-line in V_2 and a cubic step; a P-conic in V_2 and a sextic step; S47.1) are dense families, and a dense family of dimension d meets a codimension-2 locus in a subvariety of dimension d − 2 with nothing that splits its equations: the S42 / S46 pattern again. No P-rational family is known to lie inside R′ or R. **So the two-point slice program for A_9, r = 5 is closed, together with its degenerations, unless a mechanism for P-points on R′ or R appears.** Further degenerations (E = ℓ ∪ ℓ′ ∪ conic, or four lines: cubic steps only) have codimension ≥ 3 and fare the same.
- A_9, r = 5 over P remains "a P-conic in V_3 with plane signature (2, 1) on which p4 has a real root" (equivalently a P-line on the quintic F5, S45.2), with no mechanism; M_{9,K} is the uniform target as before.

## 0y. Session 48 (Nov 18): results

Topic: priority 1 of the Nov 18 scope decision: certify the S44.2 chain for r ≤ 3 over P, uniformly in K, so that a nonic over P with at most 3 real roots becomes an allowed step. **Mechanism tested:** the S44.2 chain (P-conic in V_2 and a sextic → Q ∈ V_3(P); a quintic pencil root → a P-rational conic bundle on S_Q; a sextic fibre of C_Q → a P-line in V_3; the quartic p4 → a P-point of V_4; the T1 engine). **Test that could fail:** an interval-arithmetic certificate of its real conditions at one sample per r. **Result:** passes for r = 1 and r = 3.

New status tag: **[interval]**: a rigorous interval-arithmetic computation (mpmath.iv at 60 digits: Krawczyk existence and uniqueness tests, sign changes, enclosures excluding 0), our own code, not independently re-checked.

Notation: A = ℝ^r × ℂ^s. For Q ∈ V_3 let L_Q = {v : Tr v = Tr(Qv) = Tr(Q²v) = 0} (6-dimensional, contains Q). On P(L_Q/Q) ≅ P⁴ the quadrics A1 = Tr(v²), A2 = Tr(Qv²) are well defined, and so is the cubic p3 on S_Q = {A1 = A2 = 0} (p3(v + cQ) = p3(v) + 3c·A2(v)); C_Q = S_Q ∩ {p3 = 0}; M_λ = λA1 + A2.

**S48.1 (the S44.2 chain reduced to four open real conditions, uniformly in K; the enlarged step set).** *unchecked* (complete argument).
- **Conditions at a real point Q* ∈ V_3(ℝ):** (C1) V_3 is smooth at Q* (dp1, dp2, dp3 independent); (C2) det M_λ (a quintic in λ) has a simple real root λ* at which M_{λ*} has inertia (2, 2, 1); (C3) C_{Q*} has a real point v* at which A1, A2, p3 have independent differentials (so S_Q and C_Q are smooth there), with M_{λ*}(T, T) ≠ 0 for the tangent T of C_{Q*} at v*, and v* is not the vertex of M_{λ*}; (C4) p4(Q* + κv*) has a simple real root κ*.
- **Claim.** If (C1)–(C4) hold at one point of A, then for every degree-9 field K over P of signature r, V_4 has a P-point y ≠ 0, reached from P by steps of degree ≤ 6 (sextic, quintic, conic, sextic, quartic), and the real roots of charpoly(y) = x⁹ + c4x⁴ + … + c0 are P-constructible (T1 and the power law, S44.1).
- **Proof.** *Uniformity:* fix K ⊗_P ℝ ≅ A; (C1)–(C4) are stated through traces, so they are invariant under the automorphisms of A (permuting factors, conjugating), hence independent of the choice and of K; K is dense in A. *(a) Density of V_3(P) at Q*:* by (C1) there is w with dp1(w) = dp2(w) = 0 ≠ dp3(w) at Q*, so the real plane Π0 = ⟨Q*, w, w′⟩ (w′ generic) meets V_2 in a conic on which p3 has a simple zero at Q*. Planes spanned by trace-zero elements of K are dense; for such a plane Π near Π0 the conic P(Π) ∩ V_2 is defined over P and has real points, so it is P-rational (P is Euclidean: an indefinite ternary form over P is isotropic), and p3 on it is a binary sextic over P with a simple real root near Q*, which lies in P (fact 3). So P-points of V_3 accumulate at Q*, and since (C2)–(C4) are open in Q they hold at some Q ∈ V_3(P). *(b)* At such a Q, det M_λ is a quintic over P; its simple real root λ near λ* is in P (fact 3), and M_λ has inertia (2, 2, 1). Its kernel (the vertex) is defined over P, and on L_Q/(ℝQ + vertex) it is a quaternary form over P of inertia (2, 2), isometric over P to ⟨1, 1, −1, −1⟩ (diagonalise, scale by squares), i.e. hyperbolic: both rulings of the cone {M_λ = 0} ⊂ P⁴ are P¹-families of planes Π_t defined over P. *(c)* On Π_t, A2 = −λA1, so Π_t ∩ S_Q = Π_t ∩ {A1 = 0}, a conic over P, and C_Q ∩ Π_t is its intersection with {p3 = 0}: a degree-6 map φ: C_Q → P¹ over P. The tangent hyperplane section of the cone at a non-vertex point v is the union of the two planes through v, so the two ruling directions in T_vS_Q are its M_λ-isotropic directions, and M_λ(T, T) ≠ 0 means that φ is unramified at v for both rulings. By (C3), persisting near Q*, φ is unramified at a real point v near v*; for t ∈ P near t* = φ(v) and off the finitely many singular fibres, Π_t ∩ C_Q has a real point v_t near v (implicit function theorem), the smooth conic Π_t ∩ {A1 = 0} has a real point and is P-rational, and p3 on it is a binary sextic over P whose real root v_t is in P (fact 3). So Q + κv_t is a P-line in V_3. *(d)* p4(Q + κv_t) is a quartic over P with a simple real root κ near κ* (C4), so κ ∈ P and y = Q + κv_t ∈ V_4(P), y ≠ 0 since v_t ∉ ℝQ. *(e)* By Newton's identities charpoly(y) = x⁹ + c4x⁴ + … + c0. If K has no intermediate field then K = P(y), α = g(y) with g ∈ P[x] for any α ∈ K, and each real embedding sends α to g(σy), with σy a real root of charpoly(y), P-constructible by T1 and the power law. ∎
- **Corollary (enlarged step set).** Every real root of a polynomial of degree 9 over P with at most 3 real roots is P-constructible: reducible ones split into degree ≤ 8; irreducible imprimitive ones go through a tower of cubics (real roots of cubics over real subfields of P); primitive ones by the claim and the certificate S48.2. Status: *unchecked* chain + [interval] inputs + T1 [Lean] + the power-law step of S44.1. This closes the over-P cases PSL(2,8) r = 1, A_9 r = 1 and S_9 r = 3 by one route with rigorous real inputs, independently of T4 (so no certificate for T4 is needed).

**S48.2 (the interval certificate).** [interval] (`cert48.py`).
- Q* has 6 coordinates fixed as dyadic rationals (multiples of 2⁻⁶⁰); the other 3 are enclosed by a Krawczyk test (radius 10⁻⁴⁰; enclosure width about 5·10⁻⁶¹), so (C1) holds. The basis of L_Q/Q comes from interval Cramer's rule (pivot determinant excludes 0), and A1, A2, p3 are interval objects valid for every Q in the enclosure. (C2): det M_λ changes sign on [λ* − 10⁻²⁵, λ* + 10⁻²⁵], its derivative (exact Lagrange interpolation through 6 integer nodes) excludes 0 there, and interval LDLᵀ pivots give inertia (2, 3) and (3, 2) at the two ends; so the root is simple, dim ker ≤ 1 (the order of vanishing of det bounds the kernel), and the member has inertia (2, 2, 1). (C3): Krawczyk for C_Q in a chart (two coordinates fixed, radius 10⁻³⁰), the tangent from an interval solve, M_{λ*}(T, T) and M_{λ*}v* enclosed with λ ranging over the whole λ-interval. (C4): p4 changes sign on [κ* − 10⁻²⁰, κ* + 10⁻²⁰] and p4′ excludes 0 there.
- **CERTIFIED** for r = 1 (seeds 1, 2: λ* = −0.1362, −0.1591; M*(T, T) = 0.0276, −0.0693) and r = 3 (seeds 1, 2: λ* = −0.3269, 0.0840; M*(T, T) = 0.546, 0.130). About 20–30 seconds per run. The resulting y has normalised p1, …, p4 = 0 to 12 digits and r real conjugates.
