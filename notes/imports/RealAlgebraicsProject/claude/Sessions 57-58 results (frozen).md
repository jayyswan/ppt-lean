## 0ah. Session 57 (Nov 20): results

Topic: priority 2 of the session 56 order (r ∈ {7, 9}): row M2, Bézier × egg, now that Arya has supplied the egg (Section 1a, G1a.7). **Mechanism named:** the crossing of a cubic Bézier (all dials in P) with an egg (shape d0 : d1 and placement in P) at a generator τ of K: for a fixed K the good dials form a 0-dimensional P-scheme; a P-component of degree ≤ 8 with a real point, or a degree-9 component with ≤ 3 real points (S48), would give τ ∈ P. The endpoint is a root of the degree-12 crossing polynomial N(t) (a T5-type engine, not a Tschirnhaus chain), so no charpoly-shape check applies. **Tests that could fail:** (a) the size of the scheme for a fixed generator (≤ 8, or a visible split); (b) the real test: real solutions for r = 7 (S_9 r = 7 is the case nothing in the toolkit reaches except fact 7 and row G). **Result:** (a) fails (≥ 181 solutions modulo a sign, still growing); (b) passes, including with a genuine egg (D > 0 on the egg).

**S57.1 (reduction of the M2 condition).** *unchecked* (short algebra from Arya's parametrisation, normalised to a = ½: x = θ²/(1 + θ²), D = d0 + d1x, y = D(θ − 1)²/(2(1 + θ²)); the single formula covers both branches, since D − 2y = 2Dθ/(1 + θ²)).
- The crossing point at τ has egg parameter θ ∈ K. Put r = 1/(1 + θ²) = 1 − x and s = θr (so s² = r(1 − r)). The Bézier x-coordinate, after the affine placement (l, w), is a cubic in τ: **r = R(τ), R a cubic.** For y, with e = d1/d0: y ∝ (1 + e − er)(1 − 2s); since 1 and r already lie in W4 = span(1, τ, τ², τ³), the y-condition (after the placement y0 and the height) is **s·(1 + e − eR(τ)) = Z(τ), Z a cubic.**
- So, for a fixed generator: **χ_τ divides F(t) = Z(t)² − R(t)(1 − R(t))(1 + e − eR(t))²** (degree 12), 9 equations in the 9 unknowns R (4), Z (4), e. Z ↦ −Z is θ ↦ −θ (the other egg point over the same x), so solutions come in pairs and the count of distinct (R, e) is N/2. Z² has degree 6 < 9, so the t⁷, t⁸ coefficients of F mod χ_τ involve R and e only, and the condition reads: G = R(1 − R)(1 + e − eR)² has remainder of degree ≤ 6 mod χ_τ, and that remainder is the square of a cubic.
- Equivalently: θ ∈ K with 1/(1 + θ²) ∈ W4 (5 conditions) and θr, θr² dependent modulo W4 (4 conditions, then (d0 : d1) by linear algebra). Spurious: R constant (a 2-dimensional family; excluded by q1 ≠ 0); e = −1 makes G = R³(1 − R) (degenerate egg).

**S57.2 (the count for a fixed generator is large).** [numerical] (`m2num.py`, complex Newton from random starts, generic complex τ; distinct nondegenerate solutions modulo Z ↦ −Z, filtered by R nonconstant and a nonsingular Jacobian; lower bounds).
- **≥ 181 distinct solutions (R, e) from 12,000 starts** (45, 82, 112, 134, 164, 181 at 2000-start steps), so N ≥ 362 egg-parameter solutions, still growing. Without the filter most starts land on the spurious families (R constant, e = −1, e = −2). Many genuine solutions cluster near e = −1.
- The exact count did not finish: Singular (9 and 20 variables) ran more than 5 minutes; msolve on the 10-variable system (`m2ms.py`) ran more than 12 minutes at 1.9 GB without finishing.
- So **M2 for a fixed generator is not a step** (as with every natural count in this project). The only structure found is the pairing θ ↦ −θ (a quadratic step over P, which is Euclidean, once (R, e) is known).

**S57.3 (real test: M2 reaches r = 7).** [numerical] (`m2real.py`: τ with r real conjugates, real Newton from real starts, distinct real nondegenerate solutions modulo Z ↦ −Z; a real solution has r_j = R(τ_j) ∈ [0, 1] at every real τ_j, i.e. a real crossing there).
- **r = 7: real solutions exist** (seed 4: 30 from 6000 starts; seed 5: 15; seed 3: 0 from 2500). Most have e < −1, but some have **e = 0.450, −0.816, −0.823**, where D = d0(1 + ex) > 0 on [0, 1]: genuine eggs, seven real crossings at the real conjugates.
- r = 9: 5 real solutions (seed 3), **all with e < −1**: then D changes sign at x = −1/e ∈ (0, 1), the egg has a node, and 9 real crossings are possible. For D > 0 none was found, consistent with the heuristic cap of 8 crossings (convexity). r = 5: 2 real solutions (seed 3), e < −1.
- **Arya's answer (Nov 20, after session 57): nodal eggs are not allowed.** The Triangle handle has range α ∈ [0, 1], and D never vanishes inside the egg; at α = 1 it vanishes at the tangent point x = 0, which gives a cusp (the piriform), not a node. In this normalisation that excludes every e ≤ −1 (D = d0(1 + ex) with a zero in (0, 1]); d0 = 0 is the cusp. Rerun (`m2real.py`, seed 4, r = 7): e ∈ {−0.822, −0.816, 4.421} among 15 real solutions, the rest e < −1; seed 6, r = 9: 4 real solutions, all e < −1. So **M2 reaches r = 7 with allowed eggs** (e = 4.421 and 0.450 have d1 > 0; e ≈ −0.82 is allowed if the handle range covers d1 < 0, not recorded here), **and r = 9 is out of its reach on the evidence** (every real r = 9 solution found is nodal, matching the cap of 8 crossings).
- Consequence: M2 is real-feasible for S_9 r = 7 (unlike x⁹ = quartic, Engine O and every chain shape, S52.1), so a P-mechanism for it would close that case. **Question for Arya:** is a shape d0 : d1 with D vanishing on the egg (e < −1 in this normalisation, a nodal egg) allowed by the Triangle handle? If yes, M2 also has real members with r = 9.

## 0ai. Session 58 (Nov 20): results

Topic: priority 2 of the session 57 order (S_9 r = 7 through row M2), mechanism (ii) of S57.4; priority 1 (the genus of Σ) only as a background run. **Mechanism named: an intermediate cover of the M2 fixed-generator scheme.** For a fixed K and generator τ the M2 solutions (R, Z, e) form a 0-dimensional P-scheme X_τ of degree N (S57.1). If its monodromy group has a block system whose quotient has degree ≤ 8 (or 9 with ≤ 3 real points, S48), or whose blocks are orbits of a group of order 2^a 3^b, the quotient has a P-point by fact 3 (or S48), and the problem drops to a smaller scheme; with a real r = 7 solution on an allowed egg (S57.3) this would give τ ∈ P. The endpoint is a root of the degree-12 crossing polynomial (a T5-type engine, not a Tschirnhaus chain), so no charpoly-shape check applies. **Tests that could fail:** (a) the full count N by monodromy solving (S57 had only the lower bound 2 · 181); (b) the block structure of the monodromy group on X_τ. **Result:** (a) N ≥ 2638 (still growing by 10–40 per loop after 25 loops), far above 8; (b) one new symmetry (S58.1) gives blocks of size 4, but these are quadratic steps only; on the ≥ 662 orbits of the Klein group the monodromy is transitive and (numerically) primitive. **Mechanism ruled out** (for a fixed generator).

**S58.1 (a second symmetry: the egg's horizontal reflection).** [exact] (short algebra) and [numerical] (`m2mono.py`, `m2blocks.py`).
- The map ι: (R, Z, e) ↦ (1 − R, Z/(1 + e), −e/(1 + e)) preserves the condition χ_τ | Z² − R(1 − R)(1 + e − eR)²: with R′ = 1 − R and e′ = −e/(1 + e) one has 1 + e − eR = 1 + eR′ = (1 + e)(1 + e′ − e′R′), so R(1 − R)(1 + e − eR)² = (1 + e)² · R′(1 − R′)(1 + e′ − e′R′)². Geometrically x ↦ 1 − x reflects the egg: D(1 − x) = d0(1 + e)(1 + e′x). Allowed eggs go to allowed eggs (e > −1 ⇔ e′ > −1, since 1 + e′ = 1/(1 + e)), and ι is an involution.
- With Z ↦ −Z (θ ↦ −θ, S57.1) it generates a Klein group V that acts freely on X_τ (no solution with e = 0 or Z = 0 was found). Numerically 198 of 200 ι-images of solutions were already in the solution set before closing it under V. **So X_τ → X_τ/V is a tower of two quadratic steps (Euclidean over P), and only X_τ/V, of degree N/4 ≥ 662, matters.** S57.2 counted pairs, i.e. N/2.

**S58.2 (N, the degree of X_τ).** [numerical] (`m2mono.py`: monodromy solving, triangle loops τ0 → τ1 → τ2 → τ0 in the space of the nine roots, RK4 predictor with Newton corrector, step ≤ 0.02 after the first runs; endpoints accepted only if nonsingular with R nonconstant; colliding endpoints dropped; seed 1, resumed with seed 2 from the same base).
- Growth of the solution set (with signs, closed under V where noted): 2, 4, 6, …, 154, 282, 476, 772, 1186, 1574, 1890 (loose steps, loops 1–15), 2174, 2342, 2454, 2496 (loose), then 2520, 2538, 2550, 2558, 2582, 2588 (tight, seed 1) and 2586, 2628 (tight, seed 2); merged 2638. Closure under V adds the few missing images. **N ≥ 2638, so X_τ/V has degree ≥ 662** (the merged set has 662 classes, 657 of them complete), and S57.2's 181 pairs were at most 14 % of the pairs. The count is not saturated; the growth per loop fell from about 300 to 10–40. No spurious solutions (e = −1, e = 0, R constant) occur among them.
- Loose steps (0.05) gave visibly non-injective loops (path jumps); with step ≤ 0.02, collisions were dropped and every remaining image respected the V-classes except 14 class images (over 8 loops), which were discarded too (V-classes must be blocks, since V is defined over the base, so a violation is a tracking error).

**S58.3 (monodromy on X_τ/V).** [numerical] (`m2blocks.py`: loops as partial permutations; minimal block containing {0, i} for every class i by union–find closure (Atkinson), which is a lower bound for the true minimal block).
- 8 loops (5 from seed 1, 3 from seed 2) with 2464–2562 of the solutions tracked each. The orbit of solution 0 contains 2624 of the 2638 solutions (the rest were found in the last loops and have no images yet): **transitive**.
- For each of the 648 classes i with data, the minimal block containing {0, i} has at least 644 of the 662 classes (the remaining 13 classes are new and untracked). If the true degree M = N/4 is below 1288 (it grows slowly from 662), a block of more than M/2 points is the whole set, so **the action on X_τ/V is primitive.** Hence X_τ/V has no intermediate cover at all, and the only quotients of X_τ are by V.
- Closed cycles of the class-level loops include lengths 109, 101 (primes), 82, 60, 44, 27, 19, 17, 11, 7. *Unchecked:* if the rest of one of those loops has no cycle length divisible by 109 (or 101), a power of it is a single prime cycle, and Jordan's theorem (primitive with a p-cycle, p ≤ M − 3) gives A_M or S_M; the partial permutations don't show the rest of the cycle type.
- **Mechanism ruled out:** an intermediate cover of the M2 scheme for a fixed generator (beyond V, which is two quadratic steps).

**S58.4 (what the fixed-generator scheme is a fibre of).** *unchecked* (dimension count).
- The full M2 incidence W_K = {(y, x) : y ∈ K a generator, x = (R, Z, e), χ_y | F_x} has dimension 9 (18 unknowns, 9 equations) and is a degree-N cover of the affine 9-space K over P (its fibre over y is X_y). A P-point of W_K is a y ∈ K whose fibre has a P-point. Equivalently, M2 is a 7-parameter family of nonics over a P-rational base (a cubic Bézier through three P-points of the egg, with the residual nonic of the 12 crossings; 7 = the dimension of nonics up to affine substitution), so it is dominant over C and real-feasible for r = 7 (S57.3); whether K is a P-specialisation is again the versality question of S41.1. The fixed-generator count N is the degree of this family's classifying map, and S58.3 says its monodromy has no useful intermediate cover. What is left for M2 is the choice of y: a special y ∈ K where X_y splits (the branch locus of W_K → K, or y on a P-rational subvariety where a crossing becomes P-rational by construction).
