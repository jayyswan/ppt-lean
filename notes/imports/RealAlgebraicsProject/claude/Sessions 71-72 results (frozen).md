**S71.1 (offsets do not free I; item (1) closed).** *unchecked* (short, complete) + [exact] (`offs71.py`).
- Setting: Γ = A(graph of E0), E0 ∈ ℚ[x] of degree m ≥ 2, A a P-affine map, O_d the offset of Γ at P-distance d. Pulling A back, O_d is the offset of the graph in a positive definite metric with P entries: the point over the foot x is (x, E0(x)) + (d/s)·M⁻¹(−E0′(x), 1), with s² = q_M(E0′(x)) and q_M(u) = (−u, 1)M⁻¹(−u, 1)ᵀ a positive definite binary quadratic in u.
- The foot map makes O_d birational to the double cover C_M: s² = q_M(E0′(x)). So a crossing point whose coordinates generate K (degree 9) gives (x, s) ∈ C_M(K) with x ∉ P: **a point of degree 9 with residue field K on C_M** (the per-layer square condition of G1a.3, now with a generating foot).
- **Genus.** Write q_M = (u − α)(u − ᾱ), α ∉ ℝ. The roots of E0′ − α and E0′ − ᾱ are disjoint and conjugate, so the odd-multiplicity roots of q_M(E0′(x)) number 2k and genus(C_M) = k − 1, with k ≡ m − 1 mod 2. For **m = 5** (E0′ quartic), k = 0 would make E0′ − α = G², G = A + iB with A, B real, and E0′ real forces AB constant, so E0′ constant: impossible. **So genus ≥ 1 for every metric**, with equality iff E0′ − α has a double root, i.e. α = E0′(c) with E0″(c) = 0 and c ∉ ℝ (then Re α, |α|² ∈ L ⊂ P, so the matched metric is P-rational). [exact] for E0 = x⁵ + 2x⁴ − x³ + 3x² − x: genus 3 for a random metric, 1 for the matched one. (m = 4: a real cubic E0′ with non-real critical points gives genus 0 in the matched metric, *unchecked*; m = 6: genus 0 needs two critical points of E0′ with the same non-real critical value, finitely many shapes, *unchecked*.)
- **Degrees.** The offset of a quintic graph has degree 18 and meets a cubic Bézier in 54 points (Res_x of the normal and distance equations: degree 54 in t, squarefree) [exact]. The B5 incidence (crossing polynomial E(w²) + R(w) of degree 10, L_135 genuine, S62.1) is gone.

**S72.4 (secant descent and cubic forms: equivalent or inapplicable).** *unchecked* (short).
- A plane cubic with a point of degree prime to 3 has a ℚ-point (index divides 3; Riemann–Roch in genus 1); on C_Π a quadratic point x suffices, since the line xx̄ lies in Π ⊂ Q and its third point is rational. But this is **equivalent to the problem**: V_3(ℚ) ≠ ∅ ⇔ V_3 has a quadratic point x with B(x, x̄) = 0 ⇔ some ℚ-line of Q has a binary cubic Tr(u³)|ℓ with a rational root (write x = a + √δ·b: B(x, x̄) = 0 and the trace conditions make a, b span a ℚ-isotropic line, and (1 : √δ) is a root of the binary cubic, which then splits off a rational root). The same holds for tangency (a ℚ-line of Q tangent to {Tr u³ = 0}: the double root is rational) and for ℚ-lines of X_A tangent to Q (S67 setting): each is a ℚ-point problem of the same kind.
- **Heath-Brown cannot enter linearly:** linear spaces in Q have projective dimension ≤ 3 (S72.1), every polynomial family u = ℓ(x) + q(x)·o (o isotropic, q quadratic) lying in Q is an isotropic linear space, and the rational parametrisation of Q (projection from a ℚ-point) turns Tr u³ into a sextic in 8 variables; Weil restriction gives m cubic conditions, not one. So mechanism (c) of the S71 Next list is dead in linear form.
