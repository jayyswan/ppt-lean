## 0ab. Session 51 (Nov 19): results

Topic: priority 2 (r ≥ 7) was the plan; the session began by re-deriving the engine at the end of the S49/S50 chains, and found that for n ≥ 10 it is not the T1 engine. **Mechanism tested:** the S49.2/S50.1 chains as routes to an engine in degree n ≥ 10. **Test that could fail:** compute the charpoly shape of points of V_4 in degree n (symbolically by Newton's identities, numerically on real points). **Result:** fails for every n ≥ 10. The session records the correction, the corrected open cases and the one conditional lever that would undo it.

**S51.1 (correction: the engine of V_4 in degree n).** [numerical] + elementary.
- For y in an étale P-algebra A of degree n, charpoly(y) = x^n − e1x^{n−1} + e2x^{n−2} − … ± e_n, and (Newton) p1 = … = pk = 0 ⇔ e1 = … = ek = 0. So V_4 = {p1 = … = p4 = 0} gives charpoly x^n + c_{n−5}x^{n−5} + … + c0: **the engine x^n = (polynomial of degree n − 5)**. This is x⁹ = quartic (T1 + power law) only for n = 9. For n = 10, 11, 12 it is x^n = quintic, sextic, septic, which are not in the toolkit.
- Numerical check (`v4check.py <n> <r> <seed>`, seconds): a real point of V_4 in ℝ^r × ℂ^s has nonzero charpoly coefficients at degrees {9, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0} for n = 9, but {10, 5, 4, …, 0} for n = 10 (r = 2, 6), {11, 6, …} for n = 11 and {12, 7, …} for n = 12. (`cert49.py` printed p5 among "normalised p1..p5" and it was nonzero; this was not noticed in sessions 49–50.)
- **What is retracted:** the claim of S49.2 that the real roots of charpoly(y) are P-constructible for n ≥ 10; S49.3's consequences; S49.4 (no extension of degree 10 with r ≤ 4, 11 with r ≤ 5, 12 with r ≤ 6; rational decics with ≤ 4 real roots); the conclusion of S50.1 (A_10 r = 6); S50.2's consequences (A_9 r = 5 via K × P(i) or K × P; A_10 r = 6 via K × P(i); "every irreducible nonic over P has 7 or 9 real roots"); S50.3 (open cases exactly r ≥ 7; rational decics with ≤ 6 real roots).
- **What stands:** all of S48 (n = 9); S49.1 (steps as non-existence statements; decic groups over P: A_10 with r ∈ {2, 6, 10}, S_10 with r ∈ {4, 8}); the geometry of S49.2 and S50.1 and the [interval] certificates of S49.3 and S50.1 **as statements about P-points of V_4(A)** for étale P-algebras A of degree 10–12 (they would give P-constructible roots if x^n = (degree n − 5) became an engine, S51.3); the padding lemma of S50.2 as a statement about chains for étale algebras (useless for x^n = quartic, S51.2).
- **Corrected status over P.** Nonics: closed r ≤ 3 (S48); **open A_9 r = 5, S_9 r = 7, PSL(2,8) r = 9, A_9 r = 9**. Decics (only A_10, S_10 occur with a real place, S49.1): **all five cases open** (A_10 r = 2, 6, 10; S_10 r = 4, 8). Allowed steps: degree ≤ 8; degree 9 with ≤ 3 real roots. In S49.1 language: every irreducible nonic over P has 5, 7 or 9 real roots; nothing new for decics.
- **Rational degree bound.** Rational nonics: unchanged (T0 closes those with a nonreal root; totally real open). Rational decics: open (closed only when every factor over P has degree ≤ 8, or is a nonic with ≤ 3 real roots).

**S51.2 (the decic engine x^10 = quartic needs V_5).** *unchecked* (counts).
- In degree n the Tschirnhaus variety of x^n = quartic is V_{n−5} = {p1 = … = p_{n−5} = 0}: an affine 5-fold of degree (n − 5)! (projectively a 4-fold in P^{n−1}) for every n, with real points only for r ≤ 5 (n odd) or r ≤ 6 (n even) (Rolle). Padding A = K × B raises n and the number and degrees of the equations without raising the dimension, so it never helps this engine.
- For n = 10 the target is V_5 = {p1 = … = p5 = 0} (degree 120), the decic analogue of D_K (x⁹ = cubic ↔ V_5 in degree 9). The S48 pattern fails at once: lines in V_4 through a point need 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10 conditions on a direction in P⁸ (none through a general point; the Fano scheme of lines of the (1,2,3,4) complete intersection in P⁹ has expected dimension 16 − 14 = 2); conics in V_4 give p5 of degree 10 (not an allowed step); lines in V_3 through a P-point meet V_5 only under a degree-20 resultant condition on the direction (the R_20 wall of S42, now on the surface C_Q). So the decic route via x^10 = quartic is at the same wall as D_K.
- **Mechanisms ruled out:** any chain ending in x^n = quartic for n ≥ 10 that only reaches V_4; padding as a tool for x^n = quartic. **Ruled in:** nothing new.

**S51.3 (the lever: polynomial graphs of degree 5).** *unchecked* (Rolle and density, short) + [numerical]. **Answered by Arya (Nov 19, after session 51): no, quintic graphs are not available as a base curve. S51.3 is moot; treat degree ≥ 5 graphs as unavailable.**
- Engines from the power law and polynomial graphs: if the graph of a monic degree-d polynomial over P is a constructible curve, then x^n = (degree d) is an engine, fed by V_{n−d−1}. The toolkit has d = 3 (fact 4) and d = 4 (T1, Lemma Q). **Whether d = 5 (or higher) is available is a question about the toolkit, for Arya (ground rule: we do not reason about how constructibility is realised).**
- **If quintic graphs are constructible:** (i) x⁹ = quintic is fed by V_3 (n = 9). Rolle: the 4th derivative of x⁹ − (quintic) is 3024x⁵ − 120c5x − 24c4, with ≤ 3 real roots, so ≤ 7 real roots; V_3(P) is dense in V_3(ℝ) at smooth points (S48.1(a)); and K = P(y) is open. So every nonic over P with r ≤ 7 (A_9 r = 5, S_9 r = 7) would be closed with **no chain at all** beyond S48.1(a). Real check (`v3real.py 9 3 <r> 1 8`): V_3(ℝ) has smooth real points (Jacobian rank 3) with separated coordinates and exactly r real conjugates for r = 5 and r = 7 (8 of 8 Newton starts each); none for r = 9 (p2 is positive definite). (ii) x^10 = quintic is fed by V_4 (n = 10), so the S49.3 and S50.1 certificates would restore the decics A_10 r = 2, 6, S_10 r = 4 and, by padding K × P (degree 10), A_9 r = 5 a second time. Only the totally real cases and S_10 r = 8 would remain.
- With sextic graphs: x⁹ = sextic is fed by V_2 (3rd derivative of degree 6, ≤ 9 real roots), but V_2(ℝ) = ∅ for r = 9, so totally real nonics need the odd route (T2) in any case.

**S51.4 (decision).**
- The frontier over P is again: nonics A_9 r = 5, S_9 r = 7 (within Rolle for x⁹ = quintic, beyond reach of x⁹ = quartic chains so far), PSL(2,8) r = 9, A_9 r = 9; all decics.
- **Next:** (1) ask Arya whether quintic (degree-d) polynomial graphs over P are constructible curves; the answer decides between closing r ≤ 7 at once (S51.3) and the old frontier; (2) independently of the answer, the totally real cases (goal 1): the odd route (T2) and row E, without padding (padding only raises the degree of the odd engine, which is fixed at 9 by R quartic); (3) A_9 r = 5 with x⁹ = quartic: conics in V_3 (the S45–S47 lines, which S50.2 had made unnecessary); (4) decics via V_5 (n = 10): only with a new mechanism for the R_20-type wall.

## 0ac. Session 52 (Nov 19): results

Topic: priority order after Arya's answer (quintic graphs not available). Before choosing a family for A_9 r = 5 or for r ≥ 7, the session counted which chains of the S48 type are possible at all for each toolkit engine (S52.1), then tested the two smallest point-to-conic slices that the count leaves for x⁹ = quartic (S52.2). **Mechanism tested:** a finite P-scheme of conics through one P-point of an intermediate variety of x⁹ = quartic, with the last step on the conic of degree ≤ 8. **Test that could fail:** the number of conics through a general point in the slice (≤ 8, or a visible split, would give a new step). **Result: fails.** Both slices give far more than 8 conics (≥ 187 and ≥ 538, unsaturated), so they are not steps. The dimension count rules out every chain to the odd target V_odd for every r.

**S52.1 (chain budget: which engines admit point-to-curve chains).** *unchecked* (expected-dimension counts, elementary).
- **Setting.** n = 9, P(K) = P⁸. For an engine whose Tschirnhaus variety is V_S = {p_k = 0, k ∈ S} (1 ∈ S), a *chain of the S48 type* is: P-points dense on V_{S∖k} (some k ∈ S) → a P-rational curve of degree e through such a point inside V_{S∖k} → cut by p_k, a step of degree e·k, so e·k ≤ 8. Write Σ = Σ_{d ∈ S∖k} d.
- **Counts.** Lines through a general point of the complete intersection V_{S∖k}: a family of dimension 7 − Σ (directions in P⁷ with Σ Taylor conditions). Conics through a general point: dimension 16 − 2Σ (conics in P⁸: 23; lying in a degree-d hypersurface: 2d + 1 conditions; through a point of a variety of dimension 8 − c: 7 − c conditions). Checks: V_3 (Σ = 6): lines 1 (the curve C_Q of S44.2), conics 4 (S45.2); L_135 (Σ = 9): lines −2, conics −2 (Calabi–Yau, S41.4).
- **Census of the toolkit engines (n = 9).**
  - x⁹ = quartic, S = {1, 2, 3, 4} (T1): lines need k = 4 (Σ = 6, dimension 1: the S48 chain; r ≤ 3 by the sign lemma S45.1) or k = 3 (Σ = 7, finitely many lines in V_{124} = {p1 = p2 = p4 = 0} through a point, 48 directions; a P-line with p4 ≡ 0 needs s ≥ 3, Lemma A, so r ≤ 3 only). Conics (k ≤ 4): k = 4 (conics in V_3, dimension 4: the S45–S47 program; through two points 12, S46.1), k = 3 (conics in V_{124} through a point, dimension 2, last step p3 of degree 6: **untested**), k = 2 (conics in V_{134} = {p1 = p3 = p4 = 0} through a point, dimension 0, last step p2 of degree 4: S52.2(b)).
  - odd engine t·R(t²) − e, S = {1, 3, 5, 7} (T2): lines need k = 7 with Σ = 9 (dimension −2); conics need k ≤ 4, so k = 3 (Σ = 13) or k = 1: all negative. **No chain of the S48 type reaches V_odd, for any signature r** (the odd route needs a special P-rational curve, such as a P-line in L_135, i.e. Engine O or Engine S (9,5) lines, S28.1, S37.2).
  - x⁹ = cubic (fact 4), S = {1, …, 5}: lines need Σ ≤ 7 but every k leaves Σ ≥ 10; conics (k ≤ 4) leave Σ ≥ 11. None.
  - Trinomials, fact 7, Engine O, Engine S (2-parameter families): Tschirnhaus surfaces, cut out by 6 or 7 conditions; none.
- **Consequence.** Among the toolkit engines, point-to-curve chains exist only for x⁹ = quartic, whose real reach is r ≤ 5 (Rolle). So **for r ∈ {7, 9} (S_9 r = 7, A_9 r = 9, PSL(2,8) r = 9) no chain of the S48 type exists at all**; these cases need a P-point produced some other way (a special P-rational subvariety such as the line components of L_135, or a new engine). For A_9 r = 5 the chain shapes left are conics in V_3 (4-dimensional family through a point; two-point slices closed in S46–S47) and conics in V_{124} (2-dimensional, untested). The count covers chains whose intermediate variety is some V_{S'}; slices of other shapes (cones, padded algebras, S42's linear slices) are not covered.

**S52.2 (one-point conic slices for x⁹ = quartic).** [numerical] (complex random-start Newton, distinct nondegenerate solutions; lower bounds).
- (a) **Conics in V_3 ∩ Λ through Q**, Λ a 5-dimensional projective linear space through Q inside {Tr = 0} (a P-rational slice, chosen freely): V_3 ∩ Λ is a (2,3) Fano threefold and the conics through Q are finitely many (q = Q + κu + κ²w, u, w ∈ Λ, gauge fixed: 10 equations, 10 unknowns). `conic5.py 5 <seed> 5000 complex`: **≥ 171 (seed 1) and ≥ 187 (seed 2)**, still slowly growing. - (b) **Conics in V_{134} through a point** (finitely many by S52.1; last step p2 of degree 4 on the conic, which would land in V_4): `conicV.py 3,4 5 1 600 complex`: **538 distinct solutions from 600 starts** (almost every start gives a new conic; the true number is in the thousands). Not a step.
- **Mechanism ruled out:** finite one-point conic slices of the x⁹ = quartic tower (V_3 ∩ Λ, V_{134}). **Ruled in:** nothing. Untested and next in line for A_9 r = 5: conics in V_{124} through a point (a 2-dimensional family; first the real test: on a real conic with p1 = p2 = p4 ≡ 0, is p3 forced to keep a sign for r = 5? the conic analogue of Lemma A).

**S52.3 (decision).**
