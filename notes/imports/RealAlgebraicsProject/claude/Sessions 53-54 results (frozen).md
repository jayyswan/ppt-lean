## 0ad. Session 53 (Nov 19): results

Topic: priority 1 of the session 52 order, A_9 r = 5 via conics in V_{124} = {p1 = p2 = p4 = 0} through a P-point (last step p3 on the conic, degree 6, landing in V_4, engine x⁹ = quartic, T1; **charpoly shape of the endpoint: x⁹ + quartic, n = 9, matches T1**). **Mechanism tested:** P-points of V_{124} (dense) → a P-conic through such a point Q inside V_{124}, obtained from a P-map of degree ≤ 8 on the surface S_Q of such conics (a linear projection of the tangent direction, or a pencil on a tangent-hyperplane curve) → p3 sextic step. **Tests that could fail:** (a) the real test (do real nondegenerate conics with p1, p2, p4 ≡ 0 exist for r = 5 with p3 changing sign?), (b) the degrees of the natural maps on S_Q (≤ 8 would be a step). **Result:** (a) passes, (b) fails.

**S53.1 (real test: conics in V_{124} reach V_4 for r = 5).** [numerical] (`c124real.py`, damped minimum-norm Gauss–Newton with 4 random affine gauge rows, 27 unknowns, 21 equations, analytic Jacobian by DFT).
- r = 5, 20 starts (seed 1): 17 converged nondegenerate solutions with Jacobian rank 21 (smooth points of the expected 6-dimensional family of conics in V_{124}); 15 with all 9 coordinate classes [A_i : B_i : C_i] ∈ P² distinct. **p3 changes sign along 10 of the 17** (2 real roots each), with plane signature (2, 1) on 6 and (1, 2) on 4 of those: no Lemma C analogue.
- Controls: r = 7 (seed 2): 0 of 12 nondegenerate conics have a p3 sign change (as Rolle requires, since V_4(ℝ) = ∅ for r = 7); r = 3 (seed 3): 8 of 10 do (one with 4 sign changes).
- So the isotropy/sign lemmas that kill lines in V_{124} (Lemma A: p4 ≡ 0 on a P-line needs s ≥ 3) do not extend to conics. (*unchecked*, short) The Gram matrix of ⟨1, q, q′, q²⟩ along such a conic is [[9,0,0,0],[0,0,0,p3],[0,0,a,p3′/3],[0,p3,p3′/3,0]] with a = Tr(q′²), of signature (3,1) or (2,2), so the S46.2 index count gives no obstruction for s = 2.
- (*unchecked*, elementary) Description: a conic C ⊂ V_{124} spans a plane Π ⊂ {Tr = 0} with p2|Π ∝ C (T|Π nondegenerate of signature (2, 1) or (1, 2), since s = 2 excludes isotropic 3-spaces), and the condition is p4|Π ∈ (p2|Π), i.e. 9 conditions on the 15-dimensional Grassmannian of planes in P⁷: dimension 6; through a point Q, 8 conditions on the 10-dimensional family of planes through Q: the surface S_Q.

**S53.2 (P-points of V_{124} are dense where real isotropic lines of V_{12} meet p4 transversally).** [numerical] (`v12lines.py`) + *unchecked* (short).
- For r = 5 the trace form on {Tr = 0} has signature (6, 2); over P it is diagonal with entries ±1 (P Euclidean), so it has Witt index 2 and P-isotropic planes (P-lines in V_{12}) are dense in the real isotropic Grassmannian. On such a line p4 is a binary quartic, and a simple real root is a P-point of V_{124} (degree-4 step).
- Of 2000 random real isotropic lines (seed 1), p4 changes sign on 1380 (2 changes on 1269, 4 on 111). So V_{124}(P) is dense in an open part of V_{124}(ℝ) of full dimension (the part swept by such lines; *unchecked* that it meets the real conics of S53.1, though nothing suggests otherwise). For r = 1, 3, 7 the script stops (the signature on {Tr = 0} has fewer than 2 or more than 2 negative squares; for r = 7 V_{12} has no real lines, S44.1).

**S53.3 (the natural maps on S_Q have large degree).** [numerical] (`c124count.py`, random-start complex Newton on q = Q + κu + κ²w, gauge l0(u) = 0, l1(u) = 1, 14 equations from p1, p2, p4, plus 2 slicing equations; Q a generic complex point of V_{124}; lower bounds, unsaturated).
- (*unchecked* count) S_Q is a surface: u ∈ U = {Tr u = Tr(Qu) = Tr(Q³u) = 0} (6-dimensional), w subject to 5 linear conditions (Tr w, Tr(Qw), Tr(uw), Tr(Q³w), Tr(Q²uw) fixed by u), gauge 2 (u ↦ αu + 2γQ), and 6 nonlinear conditions (Tr w² and the κ⁴…κ⁸ coefficients of p4): 6 + 4 − 2 − 6 = 2.
- **mode lin2** (tangent direction u in a generic codimension-2 linear space through Q, i.e. the degree of the tangent map S_Q → P⁴ followed by a linear projection to P²): **≥ 328** distinct nondegenerate conics from 600 starts (130, 237, 328 at 200, 400, 600 starts).
- **mode tq2** (conics in the tangent hyperplane {Tr(Qy) = 0} of the quadric {p2 = 0} at Q, which forces Tr(u²) = 0 and cuts a curve in S_Q, plus one linear condition on u: the degree of a linear pencil on that curve): **≥ 299** (111, 208, 299).
- **mode both** (Tr(u²) = 0 and Tr(Q²u²) = 0, i.e. conics through Q in both tangent hyperplanes of {p2 = 0} and {p4 = 0}: a finite scheme): **≥ 34** (7, 19, 34; Newton converges rarely here, count still growing).
- None is ≤ 8, and there is no sign of a split (generic Q, generic slices). **Mechanism ruled out:** low-degree linear or tangent-hyperplane maps on S_Q. **Also ruled out** (*unchecked*, short): conics through two P-points Q1, Q2 of V_{124} on one P-line ℓ ⊂ V_{12} (the two real roots of p4|ℓ): the plane of such a conic contains ℓ, so p2 restricted to it is a line pair and the conic is degenerate.

**S53.4 (decision).**
- For A_9 r = 5 both chain shapes left by S52.1 are now real-feasible: conics in V_3 (S45.2, 4-dimensional family through a point) and conics in V_{124} (S53.1, 2-dimensional family S_Q through a point, defined over P when Q is a P-point, with dense P-points available for Q by S53.2). In both, the missing piece is a P-point on a variety of conics through a point, and the natural low-degree maps on S_Q do not exist (S53.3).
- **Next:** (1) r = 5: structure of S_Q itself: via the projection from Q, S_Q is a surface of lines on the image of V_{124}, a septic hypersurface F7 ⊂ P⁶ (deg V_{124} = 8, minus 1; *unchecked*), the analogue of the quintic F5 of S45.2; compute invariants of S_Q (degree of a hyperplane class from the lin2 count once saturated, the genus of the tq2 curve by a monodromy/branch count) and look for a P-rational fibration or a rational structure; a mechanism must be named before any large computation; (2) r ∈ {7, 9}: unchanged (no chain exists; a positive-dimensional P-rational subvariety of V_odd, L_135 or M_{9,K} with a construction); (3) row E; (4) decics via V_5 only with a new mechanism; (5) osculation and ℚ-arithmetic.

## 0ae. Session 54 (Nov 19): results

Topic: priority 1 of the session 53 order (A_9 r = 5). Before attacking the surface S_Q directly, the session re-ran the chain budget of S52.1 with the full step set of S48.1, which S52.1 had not used: a last step of degree 9 is allowed when the restricted nonic has at most 3 real roots. **Mechanism tested:** dense P-points of V_{124} → a P-rational twisted cubic C ⊂ V_{124} → p3|C, a nonic in the curve parameter with ≤ 3 real roots → S48 step → a P-point of V_4 → x⁹ = quartic (T1). **Charpoly shape of the endpoint: x⁹ + quartic (n = 9), matches T1.** **Tests that could fail:** (a) the real test (real nondegenerate twisted cubics in V_{124} for r = 5 on which p3 has ≤ 3 real roots); (b) a P-mechanism (the two-point slice or the tangent map of the one-point family of degree ≤ 8). **Result:** (a) passes on almost every sample; (b) fails (≥ 246 and ≥ 1920).

**S54.1 (chain budget with the S48 step).** *unchecked* (expected-dimension counts, elementary).
- A last step of degree 9 counts when the nonic has ≤ 3 real roots. A real polynomial of odd degree always has a real root, so only the upper bound is a condition, and it is open. In the notation of S52.1 (curve degree e, last power sum p_k) the new shapes have e·k = 9, so (e, k) = (3, 3): **twisted cubics in V_{S∖3} cut by p3**, for engines with 3 ∈ S.
- x⁹ = quartic, S = {1, 2, 3, 4}: twisted cubics in V_{124} (a (2,4) complete intersection in P⁷, Fano of index 2, dimension 5). Family dimension 28 − 7 − 13 = 8 (= −K·C + dim − 3 = 6 + 2); through a general point 4 (the 4-fold T_Q); through two general points 0 (finite). Plane cubics are excluded: a plane cubic inside {p2 = 0} needs an isotropic 3-dimensional subspace, impossible for s = 2.
- fact 4 (x⁹ = cubic, S = {1, …, 5}): twisted cubics in V_{1245} have expected dimension 28 − 7 − 13 − 16 < 0; odd engine (S = {1, 3, 5, 7}): V_{157}, 28 − 16 − 22 < 0; the 2-parameter engines: none. **So the only new shape is twisted cubics in V_{124}, i.e. x⁹ = quartic, and only for r ≤ 5**: for r = 7 a real twisted cubic in V_{124} would carry a real point of V_4 (p3|C has odd degree), which Rolle forbids, so V_{124} has no real nondegenerate twisted cubics for r = 7.

**S54.2 (real test: twisted cubics in V_{124} pass for r = 5).** [numerical] (`crvreal.py`, damped minimum-norm Gauss–Newton, 36 unknowns, 24 equations plus 4 random affine gauge rows, analytic Jacobian by DFT).
- r = 5 (seeds 1 and 2, 60 starts each, 6000 iterations): 89 nondegenerate converged cubics, all with 9 distinct coordinate classes in P³. **p3|C has 1 real root on 57, 3 on 29, 5 on 3; never 7 or 9.** Among the 39 clean solutions (Jacobian of full rank 28, smallest normalised singular value of [A0, …, A3] ≥ 10⁻²): 27 with 1 real root, 12 with 3, **all ≤ 3**.
- Controls: r = 7 (seed 3, 30 starts): all 30 converged solutions are degenerate (as S54.1 requires). r = 3 (seed 4, 30 starts): 17 with 1 real root, 10 with 3, 3 with 5. Regression: `crvreal.py 5 3 1 2` (conics) reproduces S53.1 (p3 with 2 real roots).
- Consequence (*unchecked*, short): a P-rational twisted cubic in V_{124} close enough to one of these real cubics (an open condition) gives a P-point of V_4, hence a P-constructible generator of K. For A_9 r = 5 the open step is now a P-point on the 8-dimensional variety of twisted cubics in V_{124} (or on the 4-fold T_Q through a P-point Q). The real condition is easier here than for conics (S53.1: 10 of 17).

**S54.3 (no low-degree P-map for twisted cubics).** [numerical] (complex random-start damped Newton; lower bounds, unsaturated).
- **Two-point slice**: twisted cubics through two general points Q1, Q2 of V_{124}, q = Q1 + κu + κ²v + κ³Q2 (u, v trace-zero, 16 equations: coefficients κ¹…κ⁵ of p2 and κ¹…κ¹¹ of p4), counted modulo μ_3 ((u, v) ↦ (ωu, ω²v)): **≥ 246 distinct from 250 starts** (`cub2pt.py 1 250`). Almost every start gives a new curve, so the count is in the thousands. Not a step.
- **Tangent map** T_Q → P(T_Q V_{124}) = P⁴ (cubics through Q with a prescribed tangent vector, q = Q + κu + κ²v + κ³w, 16 × 16, no gauge left): **≥ 1920 (seed 1) and ≥ 1787 (seed 2) from 3000 starts**, still growing almost linearly (`cubtan.py`). Not a step.
- **Mechanism ruled out:** two-point slices and the tangent map for twisted cubics in V_{124}. **Ruled in:** the shape itself (real conditions hold on an open set; P-points of V_{124} are dense, S53.2).

**S54.4 (decision).**
- For A_9 r = 5 there are now three real-feasible shapes, all with dense P-points for the base point and all stuck at the same place, a P-point on a variety of curves through a P-point Q: conics in V_3 (a 4-fold), conics in V_{124} (the surface S_Q), twisted cubics in V_{124} (the 4-fold T_Q). Every natural map tried on them has degree in the hundreds or more (S46, S52.2, S53.3, S54.3).
- The S48 step can be used at every 0-dimensional stage (a P-component of degree 9 with ≤ 3 real points), and for curves: a P-curve with a P-map of degree 9 to P¹ whose real fibres have ≤ 3 real points has dense P-points near them (*unchecked*, short).
- **Next:** (1) A_9 r = 5: a structural mechanism on one of the three families, named first. Candidates: the curve Γ_H of twisted cubics through Q in a P-rational hyperplane section V_{124} ∩ H (H ∋ Q; one-dimensional by the count of S54.1, 3 conditions on T_Q), with its genus and gonality (Theorem G, or a degree-9 map with ≤ 3 real points per fibre); a fibration of the 8-fold of twisted cubics by their spans P³ (each P³ meets V_{124} in a (2,4) curve of genus 9 that must split as cubic + quintic, i.e. a (1,2) + (3,2) split on the quadric surface); (2) r ∈ {7, 9}: unchanged (no chain shape, S52.1, and S54.1 adds none); (3) row E; (4) decics via V_5 only with a new mechanism; (5) osculation and ℚ-arithmetic.
