## 0ar. Session 67 (Nov 21): results

Topic: priority 1, scope (i): the cubic-arithmetic step that S66.5 requires of any global mechanism for V_A, and what it leaves for the quintic. **Mechanism named:** the Hasse principle for the Hermite–Joubert cubic X_A = {Tr y = Tr y³ = 0} ⊂ P(A) (a cubic in 9 variables with 126 nodes, S66.2), as the step that fails over 3-fields, followed by a transfer from X_A(ℚ) to V_A(ℚ). **Test that could fail:** (a) whether a theorem covers a nodal nonary cubic (S66.2 had found none); (b) whether X_A is everywhere locally soluble; (c) whether any transfer X_A → V_A works. **Result:** (a) and (b) pass: **Hooley's fourth nonary paper (Crelle 680, 2013) gives the Hasse principle for cubic hypersurfaces in P⁸ whose singularities are isolated ordinary double points**, and X_A has exactly 126 ordinary nodes [exact]; X_A has smooth ℚ_p-points at every unramified prime for every A [exact]. So the Hermite–Joubert problem for n = 10 is solved over ℚ up to local conditions at ramified primes, in contrast to 3-fields (S66.1). (c) fails for every transfer tried: the quintic condition is the whole remaining gap for scope (i).

**S67.1 (Hooley IV applies to X_A).** [source] (secondary: Heath-Brown's Royal Society memoir of Hooley, which states that Hooley IV, J. Reine Angew. Math. 680 (2013) 23–39, gives a nontrivial zero of a cubic form in n ≥ 9 variables with zeros everywhere locally whenever the singularities of the projective hypersurface are isolated ordinary double points; the same statement as Theorem H in Zhao, arXiv 1211.4215, and in arXiv 2610.08226. Hooley II–III (1991, 1994) needed the double points to be linearly independent, which 126 points in P⁸ are not; Hooley IV was not read) + [exact] (`nodes67.sing`, Singular over ℚ).
- **Sing(X̄) is 126 ordinary double points.** For X̄ = {Σz = Σz³ = 0} ⊂ P⁹ (z10 eliminated, a cubic F in 9 variables), the Jacobian ideal has affine dimension 1, degree (= sum of the Tjurina numbers) 126, and its radical has degree 126: 126 reduced points, each of Tjurina number 1, hence each an A1 singularity. (S66.2 had this by hand: the projective tangent cone at each node is a smooth quadric.)
- **Theorem (S67.1, given Hooley IV):** for every étale ℚ-algebra A of degree 10, **X_A(ℚ) ≠ ∅ as soon as X_A(ℚ_p) ≠ ∅ for every p** (real points are automatic, odd degree). For primitive A the nodes are not ℚ-rational (S65.1), so the point is smooth and a ≠ 0 generates A. The same for nonics: X_{K×ℚ} = {u ∈ K : Tr u³ = (Tr u)³} ⊂ P(K) is the same twisted nodal cubic.
- So the 3-primary input that S66.1 shows any global mechanism needs (a step that fails over some 3-field) exists over ℚ for the cubic part of V_A: **X_A satisfies the Hasse principle**, while over the 3-field of S66.1 it has no points. This is an unconditional replacement for Heath-Brown's 10-variable input of S66.2 with the trace condition included.

**S67.2 (local solubility of X_A; Hermite–Joubert for n = 10 over ℚ).** [exact] (`nodes67.py`, `xloc67.py`) + *unchecked* (assembly, short).
- Unramified p ≥ 7: Chevalley–Warning (1 + 3 < 10) gives #X(F_p) ≡ 1 mod p; for p ≥ 7 the singular F_p-points are exactly the Frobenius-stable nodes (bisections S with Frob(S) = S or S^c). Their number by cycle type (`nodes67.py`): 0 for (9,1), (7,3), (8,1,1), (7,2,1), …; 1 for (10), (5,5), (5,4,1), (5,3,2), (5,3,1,1), (5,2,2,1), (5,2,1,1,1), (5,1⁵), (4,3,2,1); otherwise ≢ 1 mod p except (4,2,2,2) at p = 7 and (2,2,2,1⁴), (2,1⁸) at p = 11. Every type with count ≡ 1 mod p has two equal cycles or an even cycle, which gives an explicit smooth ℚ_p-point (a = (x, −x, 0, …) on two isomorphic factors; a = x with Frob^c x = −x on a factor of degree 2c; a² non-scalar). The other types have a smooth F_p-point, which lifts.
- Unramified p = 2, 3, 5: pair/even-cycle points as above for every type except the two decic types with distinct odd cycles, (9,1) and (7,3) (nonic types: only (9,1), covered by S64.2 since V_K ⊂ X_K). Certified by line sections (`xloc67.py`: an integer line in {Tr = 0} whose restricted cubic is squarefree over ℚ with a ℚ_p-root gives a smooth ℚ_p-point): (9,1) at 2, 3, 5: 17, 20, 26 of 30 lines; (7,3) at 2, 3, 5: 19, 21, 26 of 30.
- **So X_A has smooth points at every real place and every unramified prime, for every A.** At ramified primes X_A ⊃ V_A, so every local point of S64.3–S64.4 and S66.4 is a point of X_A; in addition any factor L = M(π) with π^e ∈ M and e ∉ {1, 3} gives the point (π, 0, …, 0) (the S64.4(a) argument with only k = 1, 3), as does any pair of isomorphic factors or factor with an index-2 subfield.
- **Corollary (Hermite–Joubert, n = 10, over ℚ; given Hooley IV):** a decic field A has a generator a with Tr a = Tr a³ = 0 whenever its ramified completions carry points of X (in particular whenever V_A is everywhere locally soluble, e.g. by S64–S66 for every algebra checked); a nonic field K has u ∉ ℚ with Tr u³ = (Tr u)³ under the same condition. Over 3-fields this fails for some algebra (Reichstein 1999; Brassil–Reichstein), so this is a genuinely arithmetic statement. Not found in the literature searched (BR §9 treats p-fields under the Cassels–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture).

**S67.3 (the quintic half: transfers from X_A to V_A tested, all fail).** *unchecked* (short arguments) + [numerical] (`nodeproj67.py`, identity to 10⁻¹⁵).
- (a) **Cubic last** (a variety Y → V_A over a rational base whose fibres are cubics in ≥ 9 variables with Hooley or Heath-Brown): a linear map into A has image in Q5′ = {Tr y = Tr y⁵ = 0} only on linear spaces in that quintic 7-fold, whose largest known members are the pairing P⁴'s (5 variables); a nonlinear map turns Tr y³ into a form of degree ≥ 6. Dead.
- (b) **ℚ-lines in X_A through a ℚ-point P:** they form the (1,2,3) complete intersection of directions {Tr v = Tr P²v = Tr Pv² = Tr v³ = 0} (dimension 4); on each the quintic is a binary quintic over ℚ with no forced root; forcing a 4-fold root needs the point of V_A first. Circular.
- (c) **Conic-bundle model:** a ℚ-line ℓ ⊂ X_A makes X_A a conic bundle over P⁶ and V_A a degree-10 cover of P⁶ (degree 8 if ℓ ⊂ V_A); a ℚ-point needs a fibre with a rational point, and the branch-locus trick (a unique double point in a fibre is rational) is circular (the branch locus is birational to the ramification locus inside V_A).
- (d) **Projection from a node N** (over the field F_N of N, degree 126 for S_10): N is a double point of both X and Q5′ (∇Tr y⁵ = 5·1 ∝ ∇Tr y at N, since N⁴ = 1), so lines through N in X_A meet V_A in 2N plus a residual cubic, and **V_A is birational over F_N to the nonic hypersurface −(20/3)c³ + 5cqT4 − q²T5 = 0 in P⁷** (directions v, c = Tr v³, q = 3Tr(Nv²), T4 = Tr(Nv⁴), T5 = Tr v⁵; the third point of X on N + κv is y = cN − qv and Tr y⁵ = q³·(that nonic), checked numerically). For primitive A no node, pair of nodes or triple of nodes is Galois-stable (no subgroup of index ≤ 3 stabilises a bisection), so this is not a ℚ-mechanism (and Colliot-Thélène–Salberger's three-conjugate-nodes theorem does not apply).
- (e) **p-field test of what is left:** over 2-fields every odd-degree form system of this size has points (as over ℝ), and the Brassil–Reichstein digit system for p = 5 (10 = 5 + 5: 5y1 + 5y2 = 5y1³ + 5y2³ = 5y1⁵ + 5y2⁵ = 0) has the solution (1, −1), so the valuation argument gives **no obstruction to V_E over p-fields for p ≠ 3** (*unchecked*: the digit point is the singular type-3 point; smooth 5-field points not proved). The only p-field obstruction is at 3 and is carried by the cubic, which S67.1 settles over ℚ. **What remains for V_A(ℚ) is therefore invisible to p-field tests**: a transfer from X_A(ℚ) to V_A(ℚ) must use global information (Hilbert-irreducibility type), not a further valuation-type step.

**S67.4 (explicit ℚ-points of X_K for the test fields: not found).** [numerical] (`xpts67.py`). In the integral basis of the S_9 control (polredbest does not shrink it) the trace tensor Tr(w_i w_j w_k) reaches 6.7·10¹⁶, and a search with 8 coordinates in [−1, 1] (6560 cubics in the ninth, rational roots by factoring) finds no point with u ∉ ℚ. ## 0as. Session 68 (Nov 21): results

Topic: priority 1, scope (i): the quintic step for V_A once X_A(ℚ) is available (S67.5, Next (1)). **Mechanism named:** a *field-independent transfer* X_A ⇢ V_A, i.e. a covariant a = g(θ)/f'(θ) (the dual basis θ^j/f'(θ) makes Tr a = 0 automatic; this is the ansatz of the 2026 degree-7 Hermite–Joubert proof, Chebolu–Mináč–Nikzad–Ure, arXiv 2609.15786: a binary septic covariant of degree 7 and order 5 divided by f'), fed by the ℚ-points of X_A from Hooley IV; plus the node-line residual construction, which removes the quintic whenever the algebra has a rational node. **Tests that could fail:** (a) the exact identity behind node lines; (b) the p-field test of the transfer (a transfer valid over all fields must give points of V over every p-field over which X has generators). **Result:** (a) passes [numerical, 10⁻¹⁴]; (b) passes for every p ≠ 3 and for every 3-field type but one, E′ = L1 × L2 × L3 × F′ with three pairwise non-isomorphic cyclic cubic factors, where X has smooth points but the leading-term system of V forces the singular configuration (1, −1, 0, 0); that type is the single open test. **New reduction:** given Hooley IV, the scope (i) gap is a purely algebraic, field-independent statement, the relative Hermite–Joubert problem RHJ_10 for the generic decic with e1 = e3 = 0, plus the ramified local conditions for X_A.

**S68.1 (node-line residuals, over any field).** [numerical] (`node68.py 40 1`: 40 random nodes, split and non-split; max residual 1.6·10⁻¹⁴; every residual point has Jacobian rank 3 and 10 distinct y_i²) + *unchecked* (short algebra, complete).
- Let F be a field of characteristic 0 and A an étale F-algebra of degree 10 containing ν with ν² = d ∈ F^×, Tr ν = 0, ν ∉ F (a projective node of X_A and V_A defined over F; over F̄, ν = √d·(1_S − 1_{S^c}) with |S| = 5). For v ∈ **Y_ν = {Tr v = Tr(νv²) = Tr v³ = 0} ⊂ P(A/⟨ν⟩)** (a (2,3) complete intersection 5-fold in P⁷) the line ν + κv lies in X_A, and Tr(ν + κv)⁵ = 5κ⁴·Tr(νv⁴) + κ⁵·Tr(v⁵), because ν² = d kills Tr ν⁵, Tr(ν⁴v), Tr(ν³v²), Tr(ν²v³). Hence **y = Tr(v⁵)·ν − 5Tr(νv⁴)·v ∈ V_A(F)** for every v ∈ Y_ν(F) with Tr v⁵ ≠ 0, smooth and a generator for general v. This is S67.3(d) over the base field, for split nodes (A = A1 × A2 of degrees 5 + 5, ν = (1, −1)) and non-split ones (A ⊇ F(√d), ν = √d).
- **The quintic disappears:** when A has an F-rational node, V_A(F) ≠ ∅ follows from a suitable F-point of the quadric-cubic Y_ν. Over ℚ, primitive A have no rational node (S65.1), so this is a tool for local fields, p-fields and imprimitive algebras, not a ℚ-mechanism for the open cases.

**S68.2 (local consequences).** *unchecked* (short) + exact enumeration (`parts68.py`).
- Every field extension of ℚ_p of degree 10 is imprimitive (local Galois groups are solvable, and a solvable primitive group has prime-power degree), so it has a subfield of degree 5 or 2. **Degree-5 subfield M:** L = M(√δ), and the anti-invariant elements x√δ (x ∈ M) form a pairing P⁴ inside V_L whose general point is smooth (five distinct y_i²), so **V_L(ℚ_p) has smooth points**. **Only a quadratic subfield Q = ℚ_p(√d):** ν = √d is a non-split node and S68.1 reduces V_L(ℚ_p) to Y_ν(ℚ_p); a Hermite element v0 of the quintic L/Q (Tr_{L/Q} v0 = Tr_{L/Q} v0³ = 0; it exists over every field, Hermite, Coray) spans a Q-line Q·v0 ⊂ Y_ν as soon as Tr_{L/Q}(v0²) ∈ ℚ_p^×·Q^{×2}, a condition on one class in a group of order ≤ 2 for p odd. Not proved for every L.
- Factor degrees: of the 42 partitions of 10, 17 have no sub-multiset summing to 5 (no split node): [10], [9,1], [8,2], [8,1,1], [7,3], [7,2,1], [7,1,1,1], [6,4], [6,3,1], [6,2,2], [6,2,1,1], [6,1⁴], [4,4,2], [4,3,3], [4,2,2,2], [3,3,3,1], [2⁵]; among nonic algebras K_p × ℚ_p: [9,1], [8,1,1], [7,2,1], [7,1,1,1], [6,3,1], [6,2,1,1], [6,1⁴], [3,3,3,1]. Every other local algebra has a split node and reduces to Y_ν(ℚ_p). With S64.1–S64.4 (involutions, Chevalley–Warning, Kummer and graded points) the local leftovers are now: decic fields with only a quadratic subfield (the class condition), and no-node types without pairs, Kummer factors or unramified structure. A full local theorem is not proved.

**S68.3 (the transfer as a relative Hermite–Joubert problem; reduction).** *unchecked* (short; complete given Hooley IV).
- Let B = A⁸ be the space of monic decics f = x¹⁰ + e2x⁸ + e4x⁶ + e5x⁵ + … + e10 (e1 = e3 = 0, i.e. p1 = p3 = 0), F = ℚ(B), E = F[x]/(f) the generic algebra with its tautological point θ ∈ X_E(F). **Relative Hermite–Joubert problem RHJ_10:** does E contain a with Tr a = Tr a³ = Tr a⁵ = 0 and F[a] = E? Equivalently: can the generic decic with e1 = e3 = 0 be Tschirnhaus-transformed over its own coefficient field into the row-B5 shape E(w²) + w(αw² + β) (e1 = e3 = e5 = 0)?
- **Reduction:** if RHJ_10 holds, then for every degree-10 étale ℚ-algebra A whose ramified completions carry smooth points of X_A, V_A(ℚ) contains generators of A. Sketch: a solution is a covariant a = Φ(θ) = g(θ)/f'(θ), g ∈ F[x] of degree ≤ 8 (Euler: Tr(θ^j/f'(θ)) = 0 for j ≤ 8, so Tr a = 0 is automatic); its indeterminacy and non-generator loci pull back to S_10-invariant proper closed subsets of X̄ and hence of every twist X_A; X_A(ℚ) is nonempty (Hooley IV, S67.1–S67.2), consists of smooth points for primitive A, and is Zariski dense (a cubic hypersurface of dimension ≥ 2 with a smooth ℚ-point is unirational; *unchecked* for the nodal case); so some θ ∈ X_A(ℚ) avoids them, and Φ(θ) ∈ V_A(ℚ) generates A. **So the scope (i) gap for nonics (A = K × ℚ) and primitive decics is RHJ_10 plus the ramified local conditions for X_A** (weaker than those for V_A, S67.2). RHJ_10 is field-independent and allowed by S66.1: the 3-primary input is supplied by Hooley's X_A(ℚ), not by the transfer.
- Covariant form: RHJ_10 asks for g with Tr(g³/f'³) = Tr(g⁵/f'⁵) = 0 in E (weights: θ weight 1, e_k weight k). Not attempted this session.

**S68.4 (p-field test of RHJ_10).** exact enumeration (`parts68.py`) + *unchecked* (field-theoretic arguments, short).
- A positive RHJ_10 gives V_{E′}(F′) ≠ ∅ for every field F′ ⊇ ℚ and every E′ whose X_{E′}(F′) contains generators. Over p-fields the factor degrees of E′ are p-powers. **p = 2:** V has degree 15, so a general plane section has an orbit of odd size, i.e. a smooth F′-point. **p = 5:** the types [5,5], [5,1⁵], [1¹⁰] all have a split node; the quadric of Y_ν (8 variables) has Witt index 4 (no quadratic extensions), so it contains F′-spaces P³, on which the cubic is a cubic surface with F′-points (a line meets it in 3 points, orbits have 5-power size), unirational; S68.1 then gives points. **p = 7:** [7,1,1,1]: the degree-7 factor is cyclic (the transitive 7-group of degree 7 is C7) and Kummer (μ7 ⊂ F′), so (π, 0, 0, 0) with π⁷ ∈ F′ is a smooth point (S64.4(a)); [1¹⁰] split. **p ≥ 11:** split. **p = 3:** [9,1] (X can fail, S66.1), [3,3,1⁴], [3,1⁷], [1¹⁰] (two disjoint pairs of F′-factors: involution points, S64.1), and **[3,3,3,1]**: with two isomorphic cubic factors, swapping them gives three ± pairs; **the single open type is E′ = L1 × L2 × L3 × F′ with three pairwise non-isomorphic cyclic cubics L_j = F′(t_j^{1/3})**. This replaces S67.3(e), whose 5-field argument used the singular digit point.
- For that type X_{E′} has the smooth point (1, −1, 0, 0) (constants on L1, L2; X is singular only at the 126 nodes), hence dense F′-points. The constant leading-term system of V (BR-style digits, k = 1, 3, 5): 3(y1 + y2 + y3) + y4 = 0, 3Σy_j³ + y4³ = 0, 3Σy_j⁵ + y4⁵ = 0, has the solution (1, −1, 0, 0), which is singular on V (y² takes the values 1 and 0); with S = y1 + y2 + y3 = 1 it forces y1, y2, y3 to be the roots of **3x³ − 3x² − 15x + 7** [exact: e2 = −5, e3 = −7/3; irreducible, discriminant 2⁸·3³·7, Galois group S3], which has no root in a 3-closed field containing ℚ. **So the leading terms of any F′-point of V_{E′} sit at the singular configuration (1, −1, 0, 0)** (heuristic: BR's valuation argument not redone). There (a type-2 point with four zeros) the cubic part of the tangent cone is a norm form of L3 (anisotropic) and the quadric part is universal, so the weighted exceptional divisor has smooth F′-points; whether they come from F′-points of V (F′ is not henselian) is the open test.
- **Status:** RHJ_10 passes every p-field test except [3,3,3,1] with distinct cubics, which is undecided. If V_{E′}(F′) has no generator there, no field-independent transfer exists and the quintic step itself needs cubic-type ℚ-arithmetic beyond X_A; if it has, RHJ_10 survives all p-field tests.
