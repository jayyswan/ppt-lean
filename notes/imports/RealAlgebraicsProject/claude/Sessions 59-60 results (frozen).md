## 0aj. Session 59 (Nov 20): results

Topic: the near-term target (totally real nonics over ℚ, i.e. A_9 and PSL(2,8) with r = 9 over P). Only three toolkit rows reach r = 9 with P dials or rational dials: row G (Z_G, degree 180, all dials in P), fact 5 (Z_K, degree 108) and row E (H_E, degree 10, ℚ-sextic). **Mechanism named: a variety Λ with dense P-points contained in the row's hypersurface.** For r = 9 the known P-dense loci are L_135 = {p1 = p3 = p5 = 0} (T3 chain, S25.1) and the P-lines of X = {p1 = p3 = 0}. If Λ ⊂ Z_G with genuine constructions at its general points, every P-point of Λ is a good generator and the case closes; if Λ ⊂ H with H smooth along Λ but the constructions degenerate on Λ, a tangent line at a P-point of Λ gives a residual of degree deg H − 2 (a step when ≤ 8). The endpoint is a crossing of a Bézier with a P-quartic graph (row G) or a ℚ-sextic graph (row E), a T5-type engine, so no charpoly-shape check applies. **Tests that could fail:** (a) is L_135 (or V_odd) inside Z_G; (b) is L_135 inside H_E with genuine points. **Result:** (a) fails; (b) L_135 ⊂ H_E, smooth, but genuine only on V_odd; the tangent-residual step exists (degree 8) but forces a P-sextic.

**S59.4 (row E with a fixed ℚ-sextic and P-valued dials).** *unchecked* (short algebra; dimension count not checked).

With u = q(τ) = α w² + β (w = τ + shift), W4(τ) = span(1, u, w, wu), so **u ∈ K is good iff (u − β)/α = w² is a square in K and S(u) − A − Bu = w(C + Du) for some A, B, C, D ∈ P, i.e. χ_u | (S(u) − A − Bu)² − (u − β)(C + Du)²/α**: the crossing of the graph with the singular cubic (y − A − Bu)² = (u − β)(C + Du)²/α (the implicit form of a Bézier whose x-coordinate is quadratic in t). ## 0ak. Session 60 (Nov 20): results

Topic: priority 1 of the session 59 order (the near-term target, r = 9): row E with a fixed ℚ-sextic S and P-valued Bézier and affine dials (S59.4, dial rules confirmed by Arya on Nov 20), S = T_6 first. **Mechanism named: a low-degree linear slice of the good-generator locus, as for the old row E (H_E has degree 10).** For a fixed K the good generators form a P-subvariety H_S ⊂ K; a P-rational plane meets it in a 0-dimensional P-scheme of degree deg H_S, which is a step if that degree is ≤ 8 (or 9 with ≤ 3 real points) or the scheme splits. **S60.1 (power-sum form; only p1 … p8 and two invariants of S matter).** *unchecked* (short algebra), checked numerically by S60.3's odd residuals (10⁻¹² relative).
- With the Bézier x = α(t + s)² + β and y cubic, and the graph y = λS(x) + (linear), x and y affine maps absorbed: τ is good iff χ_{τ+s}(w)·c(w) ≡ S(αw² + β) modulo polynomials of degree ≤ 3, c a cubic over P (its roots z1, z2, z3 are the three extra crossings). Equivalently the 12 points {τ_i + s} ∪ {z} have power sums **p_k = 0 for k = 1, 3, 5, 7** and **p_{2m} = 2Σ_j v_j^m (m ≤ 4)**, where v_j = (x_j − β)/α and x_j are the roots of S (only s6 … s2 enter; s1, s0 are absorbed by y). p9 of τ never enters.
- So S matters only through its two affine shape invariants: with κ2, κ3, κ4 the central moments of the roots of S, **I3 = κ3²/κ2³ and I4 = κ4/κ2²** (T_6: (0, 3/2)). Conversely, given P-values of the six "virtual" v-power sums (p2, p4, p6, p8)/2, a rational S with matching top part and P-valued α, β exists iff (I3, I4) ∈ ℚ² (α from a square root, P Euclidean; *unchecked*: sign conditions on κ2).
- In the padding language (S50.2): the condition is a P-point y = (τ + s, z) of the 12-dimensional étale algebra K × C (C the cubic algebra of c) on the degree-12 moment variety {Tr(y^k) = π_k(α, β), k ≤ 8}, with two free P-dials (α, β).
