## 0at. Session 69 (Nov 21): results

Topic: priority 1, scope (i): the [3,3,3,1] 3-field test of RHJ_10 (S68.5, Next (1)). **Mechanism named:** residual intersection with a plane through an F′-rational point of V of multiplicity prime to 3 (deg V = 15 ≡ 0 mod 3, and over a 3-field every 0-dimensional scheme of degree prime to 3 has a rational point). **Test that could fail:** the multiplicity of V at the rational configuration P = (1, −1, 0, 0) to which S68.4 found the leading terms forced (a multiple of 3 would give no conclusion). **Result:** mult_P V = 2, so **[3,3,3,1] passes** over every 3-field, for any three cubic factors. **Audit:** S68.4 never treated [9,1] in the case where X has generators; that case is now the single open p-field test.

**S69.1 (multiplicity lemma over 3-fields).** *unchecked* (short, complete) + [numerical] (`mult69.py`).
- Let F′ be a 3-field of characteristic 0 (every finite extension has 3-power degree; so μ3 ⊂ F′ and every element is a square), E′ an étale F′-algebra of degree 10, and Q ∈ V_{E′}(F′) a point of multiplicity m with 3 ∤ m. **Then V_{E′}(F′) contains smooth generators of E′.**
- Proof: in P⁸ = P{Tr = 0} take a generic F′-plane Π through Q. Π ∩ V is finite of degree 15; Q has intersection multiplicity exactly m (the directions of Π miss the projectivised tangent cone, of dimension 5 in the P⁷ of directions); away from Q, Π misses Sing V (7875 lines; the cone over them from Q has dimension ≤ 2) and the non-generator locus N = V ∩ ⋃{y_i = y_j} (dimension 5; cone of dimension ≤ 6, codimension 2 among directions). The residual scheme has degree 15 − m ≢ 0 mod 3 and is defined over F′; its closed points have residue degrees 3^a, so one is F′-rational, a smooth generator. The conditions are open and F′ is infinite.
- The same holds over any field F′ in which every 0-dimensional scheme of degree prime to 3 has a rational point.

**S69.2 ([3,3,3,1] passes; two factors of equal degree d ∈ {2, 3, 4}).** *unchecked* (short) + [numerical] (`mult69.py`).
- If E′ ⊇ A1 × A2 with deg A1 = deg A2 = d, then P = (1 on A1, −1 on A2, 0 elsewhere) ∈ V_{E′}(F′) over any field. X is smooth at P (P² is not scalar). With f = Tr y⁵ − (5/3)Tr y³, df_P = 0 (P⁴ = P²) and the Hessian is 10·diag(P_i), which on T_P X = {Σh = Σ_{A1∪A2} h = 0} is 10(Σ_{A1} h² − Σ_{A2} h²): nonzero for d ≥ 2, identically zero for d = 1. **So mult_P V = 2 for d ∈ {2, 3, 4}** (d = 5: P is a type-3 node, multiplicity 6; d = 1: multiplicity ≥ 3).
- [numerical] Random integer planes through P, R = Res_t(Tr y³, Tr y⁵) of degree 15: d = 3 (seeds 1, 4): order 2 at P, 13 further roots, squarefree, all 13 generators, smooth (one point on seed 1, trial 0 failed the smoothness threshold 10⁻⁹, a borderline numerical value); d = 2 (seed 3): order 2, 13 generators, all smooth; d = 1 (seed 2): order 3, 12 further roots (so d = 1 gives nothing).
- With S69.1: **over every 3-field, every E′ of type [3,3,3,1] (L1, L2, L3 arbitrary cubics, isomorphic or not) has smooth generators in V_{E′}(F′).** The S68.4 heuristic (leading terms forced to P) is consistent with this: the points come from P by a residual step, which a valuation argument does not see. The p-field test of RHJ_10 at [3,3,3,1] passes; X is not needed.

**S69.3 (audit of S68.4: the type [9,1]).** *unchecked* (short).
- S68.4 listed [9,1] with the remark "X can fail" (S66.1), but the test asks for V-generators whenever X_{E′}(F′) has generators, and that case was not treated. Let E′ = K9 × F′ with K9 a field over a 3-field F′. The Galois closure is a 3-group, so K9 ⊃ M ⊃ F′ with M = F′(t^{1/3}) and K9 = M(ρ), ρ³ = m ∈ M (Kummer, μ3 ⊂ F′).
- (a) V_{E′} has no F′-rational singular point (a Galois-stable two-valued pattern is constant on K9, and constants give only 0), so S69.1 gives nothing beyond a smooth point.
- (b) For β ∈ M, y = (βρ, 0) has Tr y = Tr y⁵ = 0 and Tr y³ = 3Tr_{M/F′}(mβ³): **V contains the plane cubic curve {Tr_{M/F′}(mβ³) = 0} ⊂ P(Mρ)** (and its analogue in P(Mρ²)), whose points are smooth generators. So whenever X has the point (ρ, 0) (i.e. Tr_M m = 0), V has it too; and a cyclic Kummer K9 = F′(σ), σ⁹ ∈ F′, gives (σ, 0) ∈ V (S64.4(a), e = 9).
- (c) From a smooth F′-point θ of X the tangent-line parametrisation y(v) = Tr(v³)θ − 3Tr(θv²)v pulls Tr y⁵ back to a degree-15 form G on P⁶ vanishing to order 5 along B = {Tr(θv²) = Tr v³ = 0} (the lines of X through θ). An F′-curve meeting B in a 0-cycle of degree prime to 3 would give an F′-point of V, but B can have index 3 (plane sections: 6 points, orbits 3 + 3), so nothing is forced.
- **So the single open p-field test of RHJ_10 is now [9,1] with X_{E′}(F′) ∋ generators** (it replaces [3,3,3,1]). A counterexample there kills every field-independent transfer; a proof leaves RHJ_10 with no p-field obstruction.

## 0au. Session 70 (Nov 21): results

Topic: priority 1, scope (i): the [9,1] 3-field test of RHJ_10 (S69.4, Next (1)). **Mechanisms named:** (a) Brassil–Reichstein's fixed-point method (Kollár–Szabó going up, Reichstein–Youssin going down) as the complete source of 3-field obstructions; (b) a direct valuation analysis of y = (α + βπ + γπ², c) on K9 = L(π), π³ = ut, over the prime-to-3 closure of k((t)). **Test that could fail:** whether some 3-field F′ and some E′ = K9 × F′ have X-generators but no F′-point of V. **Result: the test fails, so RHJ_10 is false.** The fixed-point test passes for every abelian 3-subgroup of S_10 (S70.1), so BR's method sees nothing, but an explicit valued 3-field has X_{E′}(F′) ∋ smooth generators and V_{E′}(F′) = ∅ (S70.2). Consequences: no rational or multi-valued (degree prime to 3) covariant transfers X-points to V-points; the quintic step over ℚ needs genuinely arithmetic input beyond Hooley's X_A(ℚ) (S70.3). The covariant search is not started.

**S70.1 (the fixed-point test passes for every abelian 3-group).** *unchecked* (short) + [source] (BR §4, through the fetched summary of the proof; Kollár–Szabó going up, Reichstein–Youssin going down, not reread).
- If RHJ_10 held over a prime-to-3 extension F″ of F = ℚ(B), the S_10-equivariant rational map W ⇢ V (W = total space of O(−1) over X, equivariantly birational to the cone {p1 = p3 = 0}) and going up/down would give: **for every abelian 3-subgroup A ⊂ S_10 with a smooth A-fixed F0-point on X (F0 = ℚ(ζ3)), V has an A-fixed F0-point.**
- For an eigencharacter χ of order r, the eigenspace has one coordinate c_O per orbit O on which χ is trivial on the stabiliser, and Tr y^k = Σ_O |O| c_O^k · [r | k]. So for r = 3 the conditions of X and V on P(V_χ) coincide (Tr y⁵ ≡ 0), for r = 9 both vanish identically, and only the trivial character differs (Σ n_O c_O^k = 0, k = 1, 3, 5): orbit types [9,1] (no X-point: 9s + c = 9s³ + c³ = 0 forces 0), [3,3,3,1] and finer (rational V-point (1, −1, 0, …)). **No abelian 3-group gives an obstruction**; in particular BR's method cannot decide [9,1].

**S70.2 (counterexample: [9,1] fails).** *unchecked* (short, complete) + [exact] (`z15_70.py`: resultant in p (chart r = 1) = p⁹·(256p⁶ + 100p³ + 625) up to a constant, sextic irreducible over ℚ; `aniso70.py`: no primitive zero of D_1 mod 5⁵) + [numerical] (`traces70.py`: trace formulas to 10⁻¹²; `sext70.py`: the sextic points are the roots of 3x³ − 3x² − 15x + 7, conjugate-ordered).

**S70.3 (consequences).** *unchecked* (short).
- **RHJ_10 is false**, even over prime-to-3 extensions of F = ℚ(B): a solution a = Σ c_j(e)θ^j (or a 0-cycle of degree prime to 3 of such) specialises at a θ′ ∈ X_{E′}(F′) chosen in the dense ball of S70.2 away from the denominators and the zero locus of a, giving a nonzero point (or a 0-cycle of degree prime to 3, hence a point) of V_{E′}(F′). So **no field-independent transfer X_A(ℚ) → V_A(ℚ) exists** (rational, multi-valued of degree prime to 3, or via any construction valid over all fields of characteristic 0), and the S68.3 reduction of scope (i) to RHJ_10 + X-local conditions is void.
- What the transfer must use instead: something false over this F′. The failing objects are 3-primary and small: plane cubic curves of trace type (D_u, genus 1) and the 15-point scheme Z15. Over ℚ these are arithmetic (Selmer-type) objects; a nonsingular cubic form in ≥ 10 variables can be anisotropic over 3-fields (BR, S66.1) and always has a ℚ-zero (Heath-Brown), so it remains a legitimate input.
- Nothing changes for the conditional route (S65.4: Colliot-Thélène's conjecture plus local solubility), for the local results (no local analogue: over ℚ_p the residue cubics D_u are isotropic by Chevalley–Warning) or for scope (ii).
