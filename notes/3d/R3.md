## Findings summary

1. **No interaction channel responds to the projected geometry. Every one uses the flat, unrotated xfrm box** (or flat outline): smart guides
   (263 real drags), Align, Distribute, group bounding box, selection handles, Size & Position values, Merge Shapes, connector sites. [Measured]
2. **Smart guides:** dragging a flat rectangle past the 3-D oval, final positions plateau (snap) at exactly the flat box lines x = 330, 480 (flat centre), 630
   and y = 170, 270, 370 (rect edge, centre or other edge on them; snap reach about 6 pt). There is **no plateau at any projected-rectangle or
   projected-outline line** (388.4, 398.6, 506.9, 500, 601.3, 625.4; 127.0, 164.0, 383.4, 428.8), over 160 x-drags (2 px steps) and 103 y-drags (4 px steps). [Measured]
3. **Merge Shapes** (Union, Combine, Intersect, Subtract, Fragment, via COM `MergeShapes`, same command as the ribbon): a 3-D oval is merged as its **flat
   outline**, the result is a plain Freeform with no 3-D (e.g. Intersect with a rectangle spans x 420..498.3 = the flat oval). A pasted EMF or PNG picture of
   the 3-D shape is merged as its **frame rectangle** (4 nodes) in all five operations; no projected vector geometry comes out. [Measured]
4. **New: the invisible-rectangle "marker" steers the frame and gives absolute coordinates of the projected-rectangle edges.** Multi-select (or group) the 3-D
   shape with an unfilled, unlined (invisible) flat rectangle, Copy, Paste Special > Enhanced Metafile: the frame is the union of the projected rectangle(s)
   and the invisible rectangle. With a marker at the slide origin (2 x 2 pt, even 0.01 x 0.01 pt) the frame is `(pr_right - 0) x (pr_bottom - 0)` = 625.8 x 429.24
   against model 625.38 x 428.80; with a marker at the bottom-right (958,538, 2 x 2), `960 - width` gives pr_left = 387.96..388.08 (model 388.39) and
   `540 - height` gives pr_top = 126.6..127.4 (model 127.04). Oval, triangle and wave (whose flat, projected-rectangle and projected-outline boxes all
   differ) give the same numbers: the read-out is the projected *rectangle*, not the outline. Residual +0.2 to +0.45 pt on the marker side, so **category (b)**
   (same pixel quantum 0.12 pt and constant pad as R1, but pad about half of R1's 0.725 because the marker side carries none). This removes R1's "position
   carries no information" limitation: pasted position is the slide centre, but the extents against a marker at a known coordinate are absolute coordinates. [Measured]
5. **Connectors:** `BeginConnect`/`EndConnect` to the 8 sites of a 3-D oval put the endpoint on the **flat** site (e.g. site 1 at (480,170), not the projected (480,180.7)). [Measured, COM]
6. Selection handles (screenshot): drawn on the flat xfrm box (885..1127 x 522..668 px = 300..500 x 200..320 pt for a 200 x 120 oval), not around the projected image. [Measured]
7. **Not tested / open:** Morph intermediate geometry, ink-to-shape, Edit Points on the 3-D shape by mouse, freeform/shape drawing with snapping, grid snap by the 3-D
   shape drag (grid 0.5 in default; the one grid-on drag landed on flat-line values), Selection Pane (names only, no geometry, by construction). [Open]

## Usability as construction lines
* Flat channels give nothing new. The only usable read-out is the EMF frame (R1) plus the marker trick above: the picture/group frame becomes a real rectangle
  (R1: Ungroup gives a plain rectangle equal to the frame) of width pr_right - x_marker, i.e. an alignable line/length. Position is still lost on paste (slide centre),
  so use the *size* as a length and re-place it via Align; no channel found converts it into a point at the projected location. [Measured + Heuristic]
* UI reachability: drawing a rectangle, setting No Fill / No Outline, Align to Slide (Left/Top), multi-select, Copy, Paste Special, Ungroup, Merge Shapes are all
  ribbon or mouse actions. The marker may be any invisible rectangle with its top-left on the slide corner and its bottom-right inside the projected box (the inside
  part does not matter; tested 2 x 2 and 0.01 x 0.01 only, interior cases by model). Slide coordinates (0,0) via Align to slide is (a). COM was used for the
  3-D angles and fov (UI spinners/presets reach those), exact positions and the pastes.

## Other things noticed
* Several PowerPoint windows share one process; other agents' modal dialogs (Save As Picture) made COM return "call rejected"/AttributeError for minutes, and foreground
  stealing sent one of my clicks (Grid Settings button) to another stream's presentation (cancelled harmlessly). Mouse sweeps must check the foreground title before each drag.
* A 3-D shape's frame size scaled to the slide when larger than it (800 x 400 flat invisible rect with 3-D: 1515 x 741 model, pasted 960 x 469.5, aspect kept) which limits the marker to a slide-size frame.
* Drag motion is ~0.91 of mouse travel at 73 % zoom after the initial threshold (shape moves less than the pointer); plateau analysis is unaffected.
* Merge Shapes with a pasted picture preserves a 4-node rectangle of the frame; `Nodes` of an AutoShape raises an error in COM until converted to freeform.
* An invisible (no fill, no line) rectangle counts for the frame of a flat group too (800.16 x 400.2 for an 800 x 400 invisible rectangle); this also steers flat shapes' frames (R1's open hypothesis confirmed).
