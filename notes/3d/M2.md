## Summary

The render is, to within 0.02-0.1 pt rms, the following model (**[Measured]**):

    M = Rz(-rev) . Rx(-lat) . Ry(-lon)                     (lat, lon, rev exactly as stored in <a:rot>)
    (X, Y, Z) = M (x - cx, y - cy, z)                      (x,y slide pt, y down; z toward the viewer)
    perspective:   (u, v) = (cx, cy) + d/(d - Z) * (X, Y)  ,   d = k / tan(fov/2)
    orthographic:  (u, v) = (cx, cy) + (X, Y)
    zoom (camera@zoom, default 100000):  extra isotropic scale of (u-cx, v-cy) about (cx, cy)
    plane geometry has z = sp3d@z ("Distance from ground"); extrusion fills z in [z-h, z]   (h = extrusionH)

1. **k = 452.22 pt, not 16 cm.** 15 fits (fov 60/90/120, six rotations, 5 px/pt, 2x shape) give k = 452.22, sd 0.14
   (sem 0.04); with the edge-dilation term fixed to 0 the range is 452.0-452.3. 16 cm = 453.543 pt is excluded
   at about 0.3% (about 8 sd of the scatter). Independent of export scale, slide size, shape size, shape position, fov in 20-120,
   and angles. So d = 452.22 / tan(fov/2) pt (= 5,743,200 EMU, 6.2808 in; no clean origin found; 2 pi in = 452.39 pt is within
   noise, probably coincidence). [Measured]
2. **Projection centre = centre of the bounding box of the *rendered flat shape*** (the "visual" bounds of the 2-D
   rendering, NOT `xfrm`, NOT control points, NOT the opaque pixels of a picture):
   custGeom: bbox of the true curve; open/closed paths with a line: bbox *including the stroke* (miter spikes included);
   pictures and group frames: the frame (`xfrm`) centre; groups: one scene on the group, one pivot for all children (group bbox).
   Rotation pivot and perspective principal point coincide (free principal-point fit: offset <= 0.1 pt at fov 90). Depth
   (extrusion, `z`) does not enter the pivot. [Measured]
3. **Orthographic / oblique / isometric are affine; perspective is a homography** to within the 0.02-0.2 pt fit noise
   (all 62 preset camera types tested). Oblique presets act only on depth (flat shape unchanged; extrusion shear). [Measured]
4. **No vanishing-plane singularity is exposed.** While the nearest point has Z < d the map is the exact homography
   (IoU 0.9997). When rotation would put geometry at Z >= d, PowerPoint **pulls the camera back**: effective
   d' = max(d, Zmax + epsilon) with epsilon about 1.3 pt (0.7-1.7 measured); no clipping, no wrap-around, no back-projected
   ghost. The nearest vertex then lands ~d'/epsilon (~100-300x) from the pivot, so the image is huge, but it is the same homography with d'. [Measured]
5. **Depth map:** (x,y,z) -> c + d/(d-Z)(X,Y) with M(x,y,z) verified for extrusion (back face at z = -h, front face at the plane)
   and for `sp3d@z` (shift toward the viewer; even unrotated, scales the shape by d/(d-z)). [Measured]
6. Also found: `camera@zoom` (isotropic image scaling about the pivot), legacy perspective cameras ignore `rev`,
   default fov 65 (legacy) / 45 (modern), presets' implied rotations (table 6), isometric presets are only approximately ideal isometric,
   3-D renderer dilates edges by ~0.05-0.25 pt (not in the 2-D renderer).

## 1. Method and noise floor

Test polygons/curves are filled black (or stroked black) on white, exported with `Slide.Export` at 2-6 px/pt. Edge points
are the 50%-crossing (between the local interior grey and white) along the outward normal, by bilinear interpolation;
residuals are signed distances to the model outline, with one free "edge dilation" delta.
Controls: an unrotated, un-3-D pentagon: edge rms 0.024 pt, delta -0.0006 pt, binary area exactly 43600.00 of 43600.
In 3-D the renderer applies its own anti-aliasing and lighting (black fill appears 5-60/255 grey, up to ~150 under some
light directions) and edges are wider by delta = 0.05-0.25 pt (e.g. orthographic, angles 0: area +0.33%).
Hence a floor of about 0.02-0.1 pt rms. Caveat: lit grey fill means area/moment methods (sum of coverage) are biased by up to 1%; I used 50%-crossing edges and
masks thresholded relative to the interior grey. [Measured]

* lon 90 (shape exactly edge-on) draws nothing; lon 100 is the mirror image of lon 80 (same image, near side swapped), as the model predicts for the
  matrix, so "behind the camera" for a *whole* shape never occurs because the camera is pulled back.
* A naive "front-only" or "front plus mirrored back piece" model fails: the back piece covers 6-393k px at lon 60-80 and is never drawn (IoU .85-.54).

* Practical: points can get arbitrarily close to the vanishing line (nearest point 0.7-1.7 pt in front of the camera, magnification d'/epsilon ~ 200-500 at these sizes);
  the exact image of the shape is available only up to the slide edge (the image extent is huge).

## Other things noticed

* **Everything flat behaves as an image of the 2-D rendering, not of the `xfrm`:** an `xfrm` larger than the path leaves the 3-D pivot at the path bbox. A designer hitting the "frame != geometry" case gets surprising rotation centres.
* The pivot includes the stroke *and its miter spikes* (line join and miter limit change the pivot). A round join changes nothing; a sharp miter shifts the pivot (a 26 pt shift at 40 pt width in a triangle).
* Bezier outlines are projected as exact curves (sd 0.11 pt vs the exact projective image of the Bezier, i.e. no re-fitting to 4 Beziers visible at this accuracy); the earlier B conclusion (homography of the outline) stands.
* Shadow with a white colour displaces the apparent pivot (free-pivot fit x +30.0 = half the offset): effects may enter the bounds [Open].
* 3-D scenes force the 3-D renderer: fill colours get lighting (black becomes 5-60/255, up to 154 for some preset/light combinations), edges are ~0.1 pt wider; the exported pixel result is not the 2-D renderer's.
* `legacyPerspective*` presets ignore `rev` (also with explicit fov), including for the group scene created by COM `Perspective = True` (prst legacyPerspectiveFront, fov 5400000).
* COM `ThreeD` on a group writes a single scene3d into `grpSpPr`; children keep `ThreeD.Visible = -1` but share the group's pivot and projection (rendered as one flat unit).
* `zoom = 0` and edge-on (lon = 90) renders produce an empty image (no error).
* Slide-size / export-resolution independence of the model: same geometry in pt at 1.5-6 px/pt and slide 1000-2400 pt wide.
* 62 preset cameras: 36 = identity on a flat shape (legacy oblique/perspective, oblique, orthographicFront, perspectiveFront);
