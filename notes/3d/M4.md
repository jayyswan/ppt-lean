# M4: which objects accept 3-D, and how 3-D interacts with other features

Backend: desktop PowerPoint 16 via COM (pywin32); `Slide.Export` PNG at 4 px/pt; numpy/scipy edge-distance fits
(centre fits project the flat-render edge points and compare with the 3-D render's edge; rms in pt).
Camera for nearly every test: COM `Perspective=True`, FieldOfView 80, (RotationX, RotationY) = (20,45) unless stated
(stored `legacyPerspectiveFront`, `lat`=Y, `lon`=-X; formula of probe A, `d = 453.54/tan(fov/2)`).
Scripts/renders: `C:\Users\<user>\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\C--dev-Powerpoint-lean\c222b849-70f5-4b01-ad09-a19ea61d2b9d\scratchpad\3d-M4\`
(`lib.py`, `t1.py` .. `t22.py`). Everything was set through COM, so nothing here is UI-verified: "accepts 3-D" means COM
accepted it and the render changed. The Format-pane view itself is [Open] (section 7).

## Summary

1. **Which objects accept 3-D.** [Measured] Yes: every preset autoshape tried (rect, oval, wave, double wave, arc,
   block arc, chord, pie, moon), lines and straight/elbow/curved connectors, freeforms (open and closed), text boxes,
   legacy WordArt, **groups**, **pictures** (PNG with alpha, opaque, cropped, crop-to-shape, SVG icon, chart pasted
   as EMF, table pasted as EMF). No: **table frame, chart frame, SmartArt**; `ThreeD.Visible=-1` is accepted without
   error but reads back 0. A chart has its own unrelated 3-D view (Rotation/Elevation/Perspective) on 3-D chart types.
2. **What is rotated.** The render is an exact homography (rms 0.1-0.2 pt, render noise) of the object's own plane. A curve
   inside a picture (a trendline in a chart-EMF picture) is carried exactly. [Measured]
3. **Projection centre = bbox centre of the object's path points, not always the `xfrm` frame.** [Measured]
   - Rect, ellipse, text box, picture, group, SVG: the frame. A cropped picture: the *cropped* frame. Transparent
     padding counts: a picture whose ink is only in its left 40 % still rotates about the frame centre.
   - Presets whose path is smaller than the frame (chord, block arc): centre of the drawn path bbox (rms 0.15 pt; 8 pt
     if the frame centre is assumed).
   - Freeform Bézier: centre of the **control-point hull** (= the freeform's frame), e.g. y 100..300 although the curve
     spans y 148..300. rms 0.19 pt with hull centre, 2.9 pt with curve-bbox centre.
4. **Order of operations.** 2-D `Rotation` and flips act on the plane first; the camera (fixed in slide axes) then
   projects. "3-D then 2-D rotation" is rejected (rms 3-74 pt against 0.005-0.01 pt). [Measured]
5. **Groups rotate as one rigid plane about the group-bbox centre.** A group's 3-D overrides its children's 3-D.
   **Ungroup copies the group's scene to each child**, which then rotate individually. [Measured]
6. **Effects.** Transparency: footprint unchanged. Glow, soft edge and shadow offset live in the plane (foreshortened).
   Reflection is an in-plane mirror but **enlarges/shifts the shape's own 3-D image (~10-15 %)**, cause unknown. Shadow
   shifts it ~2 pt. [Measured; mechanisms Heuristic]
7. **Text.** Text lies in the shape plane (warped text too). `ThreeD.ProjectText=0` is `<a:flatTx/>` ("Keep text
   flat"). `TextFrame2.ThreeD` is a separate text-only scene in `bodyPr`. [Measured]
8. **Realizing base curves** (section 5): ellipse, rectangle, cubic Bézier native; polynomial, power and exponential
   curves via a chart trendline pasted as an EMF picture; sine via the wave preset (a Bézier approximation) or a chart.
   Cropping a picture moves the projection centre without moving the curve, a free placement parameter.

## 1. Method [Measured]

For each object: render flat; set 3-D; render again; compare with the A-formula projection of the flat ink under a
hypothesised centre. Metrics: coverage of the 3-D ink by dilated projected points; edge-distance rms of projected flat
edge points against the 3-D edge; best-fit centre by Nelder-Mead. COM `Left/Top/Width/Height` never change under 3-D.

## 3. Interactions

### 3.1 2-D rotation and flips [Measured]
Right triangle 300x160 filled black, 3-D (20,45), `Rotation` 30 and -50, `FlipH`, `FlipV`. Model "project the
rotated/flipped plane about the unchanged centre": rms 0.005-0.01 pt, max 0.25 pt (one pixel). Model "project, then
rotate/flip the image": rms 3-74 pt. So the camera angles are in slide axes and do not follow the shape. 3-D Z rotation
is separate from `Shape.Rotation` (`xfrm rot`).

### 3.2 Crop and transparency [Measured]
Crop changes the frame, hence the centre. Fill transparency 0.5 leaves the footprint unchanged (difference 0.1 %).
Alpha pictures rotate with their full frame.

### 3.3 Shadow, glow, soft edge, reflection [Measured; mechanisms Heuristic]
Rect 240x140 at (350,170), grey, no line, (X,Y) = (15,60).
- **Shadow** (red, blur 0, offset 30/20): visible region matches "offset in the plane, then project" with IoU 0.84;
  a screen-space shift gives 0.35. It also moves the shape by ~2 pt (centre-fit rms 1.8 vs 0.13 without).
- **Glow** (radius 20, red): shape unmoved (rms 0.15). At lat 70 the glow extends 8.3 pt horizontally but only 4.8 / 2.8 pt
  above / below: rendered in the plane and foreshortened (thresholded measurement).
- **Soft edge** (type 6, radius 50): ink shrinks by 35 / 27 / 21 / 17 pt (left/right/top/bottom); flat: 25.
- **Reflection** (type 5): the strip continues the edge directions (in-plane mirror) but the shape itself comes out
  larger by ~10-15 % and shifted; no centre hypothesis fit (rms 9 pt for the best, including the frame+reflection union).
  Mechanism [Open]. Avoid combining reflection with a 3-D read-out.

### 3.4 Text [Measured]
- Shape 3-D rotates the text, warped text (`WarpFormat=5`) included.
- `ThreeD.ProjectText=0` writes `<a:bodyPr><a:flatTx/></a:bodyPr>` and an empty `<a:sp3d/>`; text stays upright while the
  frame is rotated. COM default is `ProjectText=-1`.
- `TextFrame2.ThreeD` (Text Options, text-effect 3-D Rotation) writes `<a:bodyPr><a:scene3d>`: the frame stays flat and only
  the text rotates. If the shape already has 3-D, `TextFrame2.ThreeD.RotationX/Y` instead rewrote the shape's own
  `spPr` scene (lat/lon replaced).

## 4. Groups [Measured]

XML: `<p:grpSpPr>... <a:scene3d><a:camera prst="legacyPerspectiveFront" ...>`. Group of A(300,150,150x100) and
B(500,280,100x70): union image bbox equals the prediction about (450,250) to 0.4 pt (rigid plane about the group bbox
centre). Group `Rotation` 30 then 3-D: coverage 0.97 (2-D first). Group with 3-D and children with 3-D: render
identical to group-only (child scenes ignored, retained in XML). Flat group, one child with 3-D: child projected about its
own centre (bbox 296.5 vs prediction 296.8), sibling flat. Ungroup of a 3-D group: every child receives the group camera,
the picture changes to individually rotated pieces (bbox (296.5,144.5)-(598.5,351.3) vs group plane (273.8,168.3)-
(577.8,301)); not reversible.

## 5. Realizing the base curves as 3-D-rotatable objects

| Base curve | Carrier | Class |
|---|---|---|
| ellipse | oval preset | (a) exact conic |
| rectangle (boundary) | rect preset, no fill | (a) |
| `cubic_bezier` | freeform with one curve segment; centre = **control-hull bbox** | (a) to render noise |
| `poly_graph` deg <= 6 | chart trendline (polynomial order 2..6) on a scatter series, copy, Paste Special, EMF picture; or a node-fitted freeform | trendline: idealized exact (like trendlines); freeform: (b) |
| `power_law`, `exp_two` | power / exponential trendline via the same chart route | idealized |
| `sine` | wave preset (Bézier approximation of a period; adj for amplitude/phase), or a pasted chart | wave: (b); project idealization assumes true sine |
| line, segment | line / connector | (a) |

Chart-EMF route, tested with a polynomial (order 2) trendline on the default scatter-lines chart: paste gives type 13,
3-D accepted, trendline, axes and gridlines rotate in the picture plane (visual check plus rms 0.18 pt). The rotation
centre is the **picture frame centre**, so the curve's offset from the centre is its offset inside the chart. Resizing the
picture is a non-uniform scale of the plane and cropping moves the centre without moving the content; both act before
the camera (2-D operations first).

## 6. Other things noticed

- Table, chart and SmartArt: assigning `ThreeD.Visible` raises no error but reads back 0; always read back.
- **Chart-native 3-D** [Measured via COM]: 3-D chart types (`xl3DLine` -4101, `xl3DArea` -4098, `xl3DColumn` -4100,
  `xl3DPie` -4102) expose `Rotation` (default 20; 40 accepted), `Elevation` (15; 30 accepted), `Perspective` (30),
  `RightAngleAxes`, `DepthPercent`. Adding a trendline to a 3-D line chart raises a COM error, so this gives no rotated
  trendline. `ChartArea.Format.ThreeD` accepts `Visible` and `RotationY`; its render effect was not checked.
- `ThreeD` COM members: `Visible, RotationX/Y/Z, FieldOfView, Perspective, PresetCamera, SetPresetCamera,
  IncrementRotationX/Y/Z/Horizontal/Vertical (the pane's arrow buttons), ResetRotation, Z (distance from ground), Depth,
  Bevel{Top,Bottom}{Type,Depth,Inset}, ContourColor/Width, ExtrusionColor/Type, PresetExtrusionDirection,
  PresetLighting*, PresetMaterial, LightAngle, ProjectText, PresetThreeDFormat/SetThreeDFormat`.
- Reflection changing the shape's own 3-D image suggests effect bounds enter the 3-D layout; shadow too (~2 pt). Glow and
  transparency do not.
- Merge Shapes keeps 3-D on the flat outline (probe C); Ungroup copies group 3-D to each child (here).
- `AddChart2` fails with "chart data grid is already open" if a chart workbook is still open in one of my own
  presentations; close `Chart.ChartData.Workbook` first.
- COM `AddPolyline`/`AddCurve` with numpy arrays raised a type error (harness issue, not a PowerPoint limit).
- `AutoShapeType` on a picture (crop to shape) works, and the 3-D image is that of the cropped outline.
- The wave preset is a closed outline (top and bottom wave edges), not an open sine arc.
- Not tested: 3-D models, Morph (M1), freehand ink, Convert-to-Shape on SVG (probe C: `ExecuteMso` fails; the result
  would be a freeform, covered by that row), OLE/video, non-legacy cameras with Z rotation, recolour/artistic effects on
  pictures, extrusion/bevel interplay (M3).
