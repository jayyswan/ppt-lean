## Summary of findings

1. **The UI never creates a legacy camera.** All 25 gallery presets are non-legacy (`isometric*`, `perspective*`, `oblique*`).
   Spinners on a flat shape create `orthographicFront`; spinners on a preset keep that preset's `prst`. No `legacyPerspective*`
   or `legacyOblique*` appears on any route [Measured]. So the legacy "ignores `rev`" quirk of probe A is not reachable from the UI;
   every UI camera honours `rev` (checked: `rev` is written and COM Z changes on `perspectiveFront`, `orthographicFront`, `obliqueTopLeft`, `isometric*`).
2. **Gallery presets store only `prst`**, no `<a:rot>`, no `fov`. The implicit orientation is internal; COM reports it (table in section 1).
   `isometricLeftDown` is `(X,Y,Z) = (-45, 35, 0)`, i.e. **35, not 35.264** [Measured]. Its axis foreshortenings are
   0.81517/0.81915/0.81517 versus the true isometric 0.81650 each [Measured by numpy on COM angles]. Top/Bottom Up/Down are the
   same orientation family (same foreshortening set, permuted) expressed as non-round Euler triples (45.2809, -35.3963, -60.1624).
3. **Three different "step" rules.**
   - Rotation **spinner** (More/Less): edits one Euler component (X=`lon`, Y=`lat`, Z=`rev`, shown in 0..359.9). More goes to the *next multiple of 10 above*, Less to the *previous multiple of 10 below*, clamped to [0, 359.9], no wrap (More at 359.9 does nothing; Less at 0 does nothing; More from 5 gives 10; Less from 15 gives 10; Less from 359.9 gives 350) [Measured].
   - Rotation **arrow buttons** (Left/Right, Up/Down, Clockwise/Counter-clockwise): each multiplies the current rotation matrix on the left by a **5-degree rotation about a fixed screen axis**, then re-extracts and stores the Euler triple. Fit residual below 1e-6 on all six buttons from a generic pose [Measured, numpy fit]. Stored values drift by a few 1/60000 deg (355 deg stores 21299999, 350 deg stores 20999997). They wrap mod 360.
   - **Perspective** spinner and its two arrow buttons: 5-degree steps over [0, 120], clamped; fov 0 allowed [Measured].
4. **Perspective can only be entered through a Perspective preset.** On orthographic/isometric/oblique cameras the Perspective box is
   disabled (shows 0) and the Widen/Narrow arrows do nothing. After a Perspective preset, the box shows 45 (or 80 for the two "Turned ..., Tilted Up" presets) and runs over {0, 5, ..., 120} [Measured]. `fov=0` on `perspectiveFront` renders as an orthographic projection to within a pixel [Measured].
5. **Reset** deletes the entire `scene3d` and `sp3d`-less state back to "no scene" (XML shows no `scene3d`); Perspective box 0 [Measured] (it also removed a custom fov and rot; I did not test whether it removes bevel).
6. **Presets overwrite**: choosing a gallery preset after custom rot/fov removes both (`<a:rot>` and `fov` disappear) [Measured]; it does *not* touch `sp3d` (the `z` set earlier stayed, `z="-44450"`).
7. **Spinner/arrow on top of a preset materializes the preset's Euler triple into `<a:rot>`** (e.g. IsoLD then Y More: `lat=2400000 lon=2700000 rev=0`, prst kept as `isometricLeftDown`) [Measured].
8. **3-D Format**: 12 bevel types (11 pt-style defaults listed in section 5), width/height/depth/contour spinners all step **0.5 pt**, min 0; 11 materials; 15 lighting rigs; lighting Angle spinner steps 10 degrees. All values are on discrete lattices; no UI route to non-lattice values without typing.
9. **Algebraicity.** Every stored angle is an integer multiple of 1/60000 degree, hence a rational multiple of pi, so sin/cos/tan of every UI-reachable stored rotation or fov (fov is a multiple of 5 deg) is algebraic [Proof: Niven/cyclotomic; stored triple is rational degrees]. Exception to settle: the preset orientations are not stored numerically; whether their exact internal Euler angles are algebraic is [Open].

## 2. Rotation controls in detail

Pane layout [Measured]: Presets gallery; X Rotation / Y Rotation / Z Rotation each with a spinner box (More/Less arrows) and two
arrow buttons; Perspective spinner and two arrow buttons (Narrow/Widen field of view); "Keep text flat"; "Distance from ground"
spinner; Reset. Button names: X: Left/Right, Y: Up/Down, Z: Clockwise/Counter-clockwise.

### 2.1 What a click creates from "no scene"

| action | XML after |
|---|---|
| X spinner More (once) | `<a:camera prst="orthographicFront"><a:rot lat="0" lon="600000" rev="0"/>` (box X shows 10) |
| Y spinner More | `orthographicFront`, `rot lat=600000 lon=0 rev=0` |
| Z spinner More | `orthographicFront`, `rot ... rev=600000` |
| X arrow Left | `orthographicFront`, `lon=21299999` (displays 355) |
| Y arrow Up | `lat=300000` (displays 5) |
| Z arrow Clockwise | `rev=21299999` (displays 355) |
| Ribbon Shape Effects > Bevel > Soft Round | adds `scene3d` with `orthographicFront`, plus `sp3d/bevelT` |
| Perspective Widen/Narrow | no effect (box disabled) |

[Measured] The scene created by a spinner/arrow/bevel on a flat shape is *orthographic*, not perspective. COM then reports PresetCamera 19.

### 2.2 Spinner (More / Less) rule  [Measured]
- Edits the single Euler component shown in the box; the other two stored components are unchanged
  (checked on Isometric Top Up: X More gave `lon=19200000` (320), and lat, rev were left at the preset's own values written out).
- More: next multiple of 10 strictly above the current value (5 to 10, 15 to 20, 350 to 359.9 (clamp, `21594000`)).
- Less: next multiple of 10 strictly below (5 to 0, 15 to 10, 359.9 to 350 (stored exactly `21000000`)).
- Clamped to [0, 359.9]: More at 359.9 and Less at 0 do nothing. No wrap. 40 More clicks on Y end at 359.9, stored `lat=21594000`.
- Values stored by the spinner are exact multiples of 600000 (10 deg) except the 359.9 clamp.
- Only 1 decimal is displayed; the spinner cannot produce non-multiples of 10 by itself.
- Z spinner: tested only for More/Less behaviour from flat (More = 10, Less at 0 stays 0); full-range clamp not separately verified [Open, expected identical].

### 2.3 Arrow buttons: 5-degree screen-axis composition  [Measured + numeric fit]
With M = Rz(-rev) Rx(-lat) Ry(-lon) (probe A convention, slide axes x right, y down, z toward viewer), starting from a generic
pose (preset Perspective Contrasting Left, X=-43.9389, Y=10.3964, Z=3.5535) each button gave M' = A M with residual at most 1e-6:

| button | A |
|---|---|
| X Left | Ry(+5 deg) |
| X Right | Ry(-5 deg) |
| Y Up | Rx(-5 deg) |
| Y Down | Rx(+5 deg) |
| Z Clockwise | Rz(+5 deg) |
| Z Counter-clockwise | Rz(-5 deg) |

(Right-multiplication M A fits worse by 0.015-0.06.) So the buttons are **left** multiplications = rotations about the *fixed slide axes*, applied after the existing
rotation. Then the new matrix is converted back to (lat, lon, rev) and stored rounded to 1/60000 deg.
- From the flat state or when other components are 0 this coincides with an Euler edit by 5 degrees (X Left from 0 gives lon = 355).
- On a general pose it changes all three components (IsoLD pose (45,40,0) then X Left: stored `lat=2389038 lon=2309075 rev=21349035`, i.e. 39.817, 38.485, 355.8). Z buttons only change `rev`, by exactly 5 degrees (`21349035` to `21049035`), because Rz is the outermost factor.
- They wrap mod 360 (Right from 355 goes to 0 then 5). Rounding noise: 355 stores `21299999`, 350 stores `20999997` (off by -1 and -3 units of 1/60000 deg), then returning gives back exact values.

### 2.4 Perspective
- Widen +5, Narrow -5, More +5, Less -5; shown value equals the camera fov in degrees. From a preset at 45 or 80 these are multiples of 5, so the reachable set is {0, 5, 10, ..., 120} (25 values). Clamped at 0 and 120 [Measured; 120 matches COM/ECMA limit].
- Stored: `fov` attribute written only when it differs from the preset default (e.g. `fov="3000000"` for 50; `fov="0"` for 0). At fov=45 on `perspectiveFront` the attribute is absent.
- fov = 0 renders as an orthographic projection to within one pixel (bounding box 424..775 x 270..569 px versus 425..774 x 271..568 px for the same rotation on `orthographicFront`, slide export 1920 wide) [Measured]. So perspective presets reach "orthographic with `perspectiveFront` label", but ortho/isometric/oblique cannot reach perspective.

### 2.5 Keep text flat, Distance from ground, Reset
- **Keep text flat** toggles `<a:bodyPr ...><a:flatTx/></a:bodyPr>`; no geometry change on the shape; text-only [Measured].
- **Distance from ground** is `<a:sp3d z="...">` in EMU. Step 0.5 pt (6350 EMU) per More/Less, both directions, negative allowed (read -3.5 pt after 11 Less). Range limits not clicked [Open]. Its effect on projection (a shift along the camera axis, hence a scale d/(d-z) in a perspective camera) belongs to M2.
- A preset gallery click does not reset `sp3d z` [Measured] (z stayed -44450 across preset changes).
- **Reset** returns to no-scene (XML has no `scene3d`), box values 0 [Measured]. It is greyed out on a flat shape.

## 3. Reachable sets (rotation)

Let R(lat, lon, rev) = Rz(-rev) Rx(-lat) Ry(-lon). Orientation/camera state is (prst, rot triple, fov).

[Measured] Generators from any state (sets in parentheses are what each generates):
- S: spinner snaps one Euler component to the neighbouring multiple of 10 (0..350, 359.9).
- B: left-multiply by Rx(+-5), Ry(+-5), Rz(+-5) (fixed slide axes), with 1/60000 deg rounding of the stored triple.
- P: choose any of the 25 presets (resets rot and fov; `sp3d` kept).
- V: fov in {0, 5, ..., 120} (only when the camera is perspective).

Statements:
- [Measured] Starting from flat: the three Euler components after spinner-only clicks lie on the lattice {0, 10, ..., 350, 359.9}^3 (37^3 = 50 653 triples), with the "no scene" start corresponding to (0,0,0); with arrow buttons from the origin and only one axis, multiples of 5.
- [Proof sketch] B alone generates a **dense subgroup of SO(3)** when the rounding is ignored: Rx(5 deg) and Ry(5 deg) have order 72, and a finite subgroup of SO(3) containing two order-72 rotations about perpendicular axes would have to be cyclic or dihedral about a single axis, impossible, so the group is infinite; a closed infinite subgroup that is not one of the one-axis families is SO(3). Hence, up to the 1/60000 deg quantization per click, any orientation is approachable by a word in B. With the rounding the stored set is a finite subset of the 21 600 000^3 grid reachable by words of the 6 buttons; exact structure [Heuristic].
- [Measured] S and B can be interleaved (S snaps one component to a multiple of 10 without disturbing the others), so reachable orientations are `R(lat', lon', rev') applied to words` etc. in a large countable family. No UI path types or drags a continuous angle.
- [Proof] **All stored angles are integer multiples of 1/60000 degree = rational multiples of pi, so all sines/cosines of reachable stored rotation angles are algebraic numbers** (cyclotomic). The same holds for fov: tan(fov/2) with fov a multiple of 5 deg is algebraic, e.g. tan(22.5 deg) = sqrt(2) - 1. What is not algebraic-valued by construction is the stored angle itself in radians (rational multiples of pi times pi); what matters is that the matrix entries are algebraic.
- [Open] The matrix really used by the renderer for presets (not stored) and the exact projection constant k (453.54 pt?) are M2's questions; for presets the exact internal angles are only known to single precision [Measured].
- [Open] Whether the renderer re-quantizes after every click or uses double precision internally: the stored triple is what `SaveCopyAs` shows and what reload uses; the in-memory render before saving was not compared.

## 4. Which route creates which camera

| route | camera `prst` written | `rev` honoured | `fov` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Presets gallery (pane or ribbon), 25 items | the preset's own non-legacy `prst` | yes | omitted (preset default 45; 80 for Heroic) |
| Spinner/arrow on a flat shape | `orthographicFront` | yes | n/a (0) |
| Spinner/arrow on a preset | that preset's `prst`, plus `<a:rot>` | yes | as preset or spinner |
| Ribbon Bevel on flat shape | `orthographicFront` (scene added) | n/a | n/a |
| Perspective spinner | keeps `prst`; writes `fov` | n/a | multiples of 5 |
| COM `ThreeD.Perspective=True` / `SetPresetCamera(1..18)` | `legacy*` (probe A) | **no** (legacy ignores `rev`) | 65 | not a UI route |

So the legacy-camera `rev` quirk concerns only COM-made or old files [Measured/Spec].

## 6. 3-D Models and Morph (brief)

- [Measured] COM reports the commands `Insert3DModelFromFile` and `Insert3DModelFromOnline` as enabled and visible. I did not insert a model (no model file used, no network). [Spec/Heuristic] 3-D models (glb/fbx/obj) are inserted as `am3d` graphic frames with their own camera/orientation (3-D Model Views gallery, Pan & Zoom, a drag handle for free rotation); the free-rotation handle is a mouse-drag (continuous), not a click lattice. Their stored orientation is not `a:scene3d`; separate stream (M4/R) territory.
- [Open] Morph transition: the transition exists (EntryEffect set accepted), but I did not capture frames. [Spec/Heuristic] Morph interpolates matching shapes' 3-D camera/rotation between two slides; only the end slides are read-outs; intermediates are time-dependent and not testable by a static export.

## 7. Other things noticed

- The pane's "Presets" tooltip name changes with state: after a spinner edit the Presets control reads `Presets None`.
- UI display quirk: angles shown in [0, 360) with one decimal, so a stored 354.99998 displays 355; the boxes cannot show the stored precision.
- The arrow buttons make the spinner and the arrows inconsistent: from 5 degrees, spinner More jumps to 10 (+5) but from 10 it jumps to 20 (+10). A user cannot tell from the box which lattice a value belongs to.
- The Z arrow named "Clockwise" decreases the displayed Z (355 from 0) and increases COM Z by 5; the *visual* rotation direction is clockwise on the slide. UI "X Left" arrow decreases displayed X (355).
- When a shape has no scene, the Presets gallery shows "None", and Reset is disabled, Perspective box disabled; the pane shows X/Y/Z 0.
- A Perspective preset can be driven into an exactly orthographic camera (fov=0), but not the reverse: you cannot turn an isometric or orthographic camera perspective.
- The six arrow buttons obey a left-multiplication rule while the three spinners edit Euler components. This asymmetry is not documented; the arrow-button group is a free-ish word set in SO(3) and could provide very precise ("any") orientations by repeated clicks, but each click rounds the stored triple to 1/60000 deg.
- Focus contention: other agents' windows kept taking the foreground; popup galleries only open for the window in the foreground, so a few gallery picks had to be retried. This did not affect recorded values (each was read back from XML).
- Ribbon Home > Shape Effects > Bevel on a plain shape silently adds a `scene3d` (`orthographicFront`, `threePt` light) plus `bevelT`, so bevelling creates a 3-D camera even though the user asked only for a bevel.
- Quick-access toolbar remembered the last-used shape ("Oval"), irrelevant.
